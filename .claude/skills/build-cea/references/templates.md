# Template families

Every family uses the same sections and shared rows, so any two charityevals models can be read
side by side:

```
cost         program_spend, total_expenses, program_expenses → overhead_ratio → cost_total
reach        how many units (people, households, animals, meals) the spending reached
effect       what one unit gets, from the evidence, before adjustments
adjustments  evidence_adj × counterfactual_adj × attribution_share [× implementation_adj]
             [× leverage_funging_adj] = total_adj   (+ unmodelled_benefits_adj where GiveWell uses one)
outcome      outcome_total (native unit) → <prefix>_per_dollar = outcome_total / cost_total  (output.native)
donor        (ours) staff_cost_share, salary_gap (@staff_pay) → salary_sacrifice → donor_cost_share
             [marginal_vs_past_average, past averages only] → <prefix>_per_donor_dollar
             (the output; animal families go on to species)
species      (ours, animal families) sads_<s> → ced_<s> → ced_per_donor_dollar (the output, unit ced)
```

Every skeleton also carries `estimate_basis` and a `source` whose `published` is the build date (the
estimate-age slider counts from it). Family extras: `deaths` (wellbeing, ours: deaths in units of
value → `wellbys_from_uov`), `moved_money` and `careers` (policy-research-meta, ours: the shared rows of
src/engine/moved-money.ts and the career-change step), `conversion` (the climate skeleton, ours:
`air_pollution_weight`, `extra_deaths_per_tco2e`).

The skeletons are `../templates/<family>.json` (and `policy-research-meta-climate.json`), generated by
`../scripts/gen-templates.py`; the required rows, plausible bounds, usual basis and the shared figures
each canonical row must read, which the lint enforces, are in src/engine/templates.ts, and
src/engine/templates.test.ts checks that every skeleton, filled in, passes every invariant and the
lint. The structures
come from evaluator models in the repo: GiveWell's GiveDirectly and AMF (cash, direct health), HLI's
StrongMinds (wellbeing), ACE's The Humane League and Sociedade Vegetariana Brasileira (corporate
campaigns, diet change), and the shared "Your dollar's share" and species steps. What the standard
steps must do is in docs/normalization.md.

## What each shared row means

| Row | Meaning | Typical source |
|---|---|---|
| `program_spend` | spending on the modelled program, same year as reach | audited statement of functional expenses, Form 990 Part IX, annual report |
| `overhead_ratio` | total ÷ program expenses: spreads management and fundraising over programs | same document |
| `evidence_adj` | how far to trust the effect size here: internal validity (bias, publication bias, self-report) × external validity (different setting, population, implementer). 1 = face value | judgment, anchored on the evaluator adjustments for the same intervention |
| `counterfactual_adj` | share of the outcome that wouldn't have happened anyway in this setting | coverage data, trend data, other providers |
| `attribution_share_shared` / `attribution_share` | the charity's share of the resources behind an outcome when others contributed | coalition/co-funder information; judgment |
| `implementation_adj` | share of reported reach that gets the intervention as studied (adherence, coverage-survey vs. admin data, pledges honoured) | monitoring data, trackers |
| `leverage_funging_adj` | would governments or other funders have paid anyway (<1), or does the money pull others in (>1)? | funding-gap data; judgment |
| `unmodelled_benefits_adj` | effects not modelled, as a share of modelled value (GiveWell-style supplementary adjustments) | list each, with sign and size |
| `staff_cost_share`, `salary_gap` | staff's forgone pay, an in-kind contribution counted with credit by share of resources | the share from the charity's financials (salaries ÷ expenses), else `@staff_pay.share_<kind>`; the gap always `@staff_pay.gap_<kind>` (local delivery, health and field, research and policy, advocacy, media, UK charity) |
| `marginal_vs_past_average` | past averages only: the next dollar against the average dollar so far, `$marginal_vs_past_average` (`published` 1) | the shared slider (normalization.md 2.10) |
| `years_of_impact`, `years_until_effect`, `wait_discount` | won reforms: the years a win counts (`$win_years_counted`) and the discounted wait until it takes effect | the shared slider; the commitments' own deadlines |
| `sads_<s>`, `ced_<s>` | animal families: SADs per donor dollar for species `s`, and the same in chicken-equivalent pain days | the species split (a fact such as `@sad_factors.meal_sads_share_<s>`, or a ranged judgment); the `ced_<s>` formula is fixed, its SAD version the shared figure's |

## Conventions (all families)

These settle the questions the development runs kept hitting; follow them so models stay comparable.
The standard steps (units, discounting, the species step, no exchange rates inside a model) are
docs/normalization.md's, not repeated here.

1. **Canonical rows may be formulas.** The id must exist and mean the same thing; how it is computed
   is up to the model. `program_spend = program_expenses × program_share` (a judgment, anchored on
   headcount or the charity's own description) when the charity doesn't split costs by program;
   `staff_cost_share = staff_pay / total_expenses` when the financials give staff pay.
2. **Cost is the charity's own spending.** `cost_total` is what this charity spent (with its overhead).
   Money or in-kind work by co-funders, governments or partners never goes into `cost_total`; it
   lowers `attribution_share_shared`, typically to the charity's share of the total cost of the
   outcome. The charity's share then applies in both credit modes, so nothing is counted twice.
3. **Flows, not stocks.** Reach is per year. Multiply by persistence (`years_of_impact`,
   `pv_annuity_due`) only for what was *added* in the period (new commitments, new institutions,
   new pledges); for a won reform that persistence is `$win_years_counted`, never a figure of your own.
   A year's total flow from an ongoing program (all partner institutions' meals this year) is credited
   for one year, against one year's cost. Count everything the period's wins cover (every hen under
   the commitments), with delivery in `implementation_adj` (the shared fulfilment rate): never a
   delivered-only count and the fulfilment rate together.
4. **Several branches in one family** (new commitments and accountability; two countries): give each
   branch its own prefixed rows (`new_…`, `acc_…`) and sum the branches into `outcome_total`. Keep the
   canonical adjustment rows as outcome-weighted averages of the branches (formulas), so they still
   read as "the model's" counterfactual, attribution and implementation, and say so in the explainer.
   Name branch adjustments `<branch>_<canonical id>` (`acc_counterfactual_adj`) and apply them in
   each branch; the canonical averages then stay outside the result's path (the lint accepts that
   only when every branch's own row reaches the result), so nothing is discounted twice.
5. **One question per adjustment.** Years brought forward and the counterfactual answer the same
   question for accountability work: hens that would have switched on time get either zero years or
   a zero counterfactual, not both. Completion and dosage reduce `implementation_adj` only where the
   evidence shows a dose-response. **Signs**: validity and delivery adjustments are multipliers; a
   downside (funging inside the organisation, bottlenecks, monitoring errors, harms) is a negative share
   of value lost, added in `unmodelled_benefits_adj` as `(1 + share)`, never a positive share subtracted.
6. **Optional rows** a family may add when the evidence calls for them, always feeding `total_adj` or
   `outcome_total`: `implementation_adj`, `leverage_funging_adj`, `unmodelled_benefits_adj`,
   `delay_years` (funding-to-benefit delay, discounted with `$discount_rate`), `indirect_deaths_ratio`
   (direct-health), country- or branch-level splits.
7. **Formulas**: averages with `avg(a, b)`, weighted blends with a weight row (a judgment with a
   range), never a literal 0.5 or 2 that hides a choice.
8. **Currency**: the local-currency amount as an input, times the shared rate
   `@fx.usd_per_<cur>_<year>` (the IRS yearly average where the IRS lists the currency, else the World
   Bank's official rate, for the year the money was spent; `scripts/link-fx.py` adds a missing one).
   Never a typed rate (the lint flags `fx_*` inputs) and never a constant in a formula.
9. **Which year and which programme**: the latest year with audited financials *and* reach data for
   the same period, stated in the summary; the programme(s) the money pays for, by spending (SKILL.md
   step 0), never a case study or pilot standing in for them. A newer run-rate (cheaper current cost
   per person) goes in the explainer as context, or as the high end of a range, never silently as the
   best guess.
10. **Staffing**: count contractors who work like staff (e.g. Brazilian PJ consultants) in
   `staff_cost_share`; the pay gap is relative to what they'd earn elsewhere.
11. **Judgment anchors**: a judgment that drives the result names its reference class in the
    explainer (another model's value, a published estimate, a tracker's rate).

12. **Estimate basis**: keep the template's `estimate_basis` unless the charity's dollar is another
    kind (a corporate campaign whose cost is stunners installed at a unit price: `unit_cost`; a diet
    charity's won menu pledges: `past_average`), and say why. `past_average` multiplies the per-donor-
    dollar row by the template's `marginal_vs_past_average`; the others have no such row (INV-18).

## direct-health (uov, unit_cost)

Chain: people reached × years of protection × baseline mortality the intervention can reduce ×
relative risk reduction = deaths averted, valued at GiveWell's weight for each death's age band
(`value_per_death`: `share_under5`, then `share_5_14_of_over5`, then `share_15_49_of_over15`, the rest
50–74) times `$life_value_multiplier`; stillbirths at `@givewell_shared.uov_per_stillbirth` ×
`$life_value_multiplier`; illness as a share of that value (`morbidity_value_ratio`, never DALYs ×
`$uov_per_daly`); later-life income (yearly ln-consumption gain over `@givewell_shared.long_term_benefit_years`,
after `@givewell_shared.years_until_income_benefits_<program>`, discounted with `$discount_rate`,
× `$uov_per_ln_consumption`).

- Baseline mortality is the rate *from the causes the intervention affects*, in the target age
  group and region, today (mortality has fallen since most trials: say how you adjusted).
- Prefer the effect on all-cause mortality from a systematic review; if you use cause-specific
  mortality × cause share, add `indirect_deaths_ratio` (deaths the disease causes indirectly, per
  direct death; `@givewell_shared.indirect_deaths_per_direct_diarrhea_death` for diarrhoea) and check
  deaths averted per person-year against the all-cause trial result, scaled to today's mortality.
  Counting direct deaths only undercounts badly for malaria.
- If the intervention protects several age groups, add a row per group and sum into
  `deaths_averted_per_person`; set the age shares from the result.
- Don't count illness both in `morbidity_value_ratio` and in `unmodelled_benefits_adj`.
- A cost-share attribution (the charity's cost ÷ everyone's cost per unit) must use the same unit
  basis as `implementation_adj`: if partner costs are per dose actually received, divide by
  `implementation_adj` or the delivery shortfall is counted twice.
- Programs that also hand out cash (conditional incentives) add a `uov_cash` branch valued like
  the cash-income template (ln-consumption × `$uov_per_ln_consumption`, household size from
  `@household_size`) and sum it into `outcome_total`.

## cash-income (uov, unit_cost)

Chain: cost × transfers' share ÷ transfer size = households × `@household_size.<place>`; transfer per
person in PPP (`@<country>.ppp_factor_2017`); ln(1 + consumed share × transfer / baseline consumption)
in year 1, times (1 + later years' gains, discounted with `$discount_rate`); × people ×
`$uov_per_ln_consumption`; plus spillovers and mortality as ratios (mortality × `$life_value_multiplier`).

- Baseline consumption must be per person, per year, in the same PPP base as the transfer.
- Household size is the place's fact (rural where recipients are rural); a size measured in the
  program's own recipients is another population, used only with that reason stated.
- A benefit a study states as a multiple of cash ("1.3× an equal-cost transfer") is that multiple ×
  `@cash.uov_per_dollar`; GiveDirectly as a destination or comparison is `#givedirectly` (live).
- For recurring income gains (livelihoods, agriculture), replace year-1/later-years with
  `ln(1 + gain / baseline) × pv_annuity_due(1, $discount_rate, persistence_years)`, with persistence
  from follow-up studies.

## wellbeing (wellby, unit_cost)

Chain: people treated × (initial effect in SD × duration ÷ 2, linear decay, × `discount_factor` at
`$discount_rate`) × `@wellby_factors.wellbys_per_sd_year` × household multiplier
(`1 + (@household_size.<place> − 1) × @wellby_factors.household_spillover_ratio_psychotherapy`) ×
adjustments; plus deaths averted (suicides), valued at `@moral_weights.<age band>` ×
`$life_value_multiplier` in `uov_valued_outside_wellbys` and entering only through `wellbys_from_uov`.

- Effects in PHQ-9 points become SDs by `@depression.phq9_sd` (one SD in every model); never convert
  depression to DALYs inside the model: the WELLBY slider is calibrated on depression (normalization.md 2.3).
- Use the charity's own trial or M&E only with an evidence discount (response bias, no control
  group, attrition); blend with the meta-analytic prior and say how.
- Completion and dosage go into `implementation_adj`, not into the effect size, and only where the
  evidence shows a dose-response.
- `effect_duration_years` is an input (from follow-up studies), not initial ÷ decay, so the effect
  size doesn't enter twice.
- When `evidence_adj` is calibrated against a charity-specific RCT, state the implied multiple of
  that RCT's effect after all adjustments, so the reader can see nothing is discounted twice.

## corporate-campaign (SADs → ced, past_average)

Chain: animals per year under the commitments won or held to account × share not yet compliant ×
`pv_annuity_due(1, $discount_rate, $win_years_counted)` × the discounted wait until they take effect ×
SADs averted per animal-year (`@sad_factors.*`, one figure per change) × adjustments (pledges kept is
`implementation_adj` = `@sad_factors.commitment_fulfilment_cage_free` or `_broiler`); per donor dollar
× `marginal_vs_past_average`, then the species step (`ced_chicken`; add `sads_<s>`/`ced_<s>` pairs for
other species and list them in `output.species`).

- Count animals per year, not commitments; cite the tracker and the conversion (eggs per hen,
  birds per tonne). All animals the period's commitments cover, not only those delivered (Convention 3).
- `years_until_effect` comes from the commitments' deadlines; 0 for accountability on pledges already
  due. For accountability work, `counterfactual_adj` is the share of compliance that wouldn't have
  happened without the pressure.
- Pig, fish and shrimp pledges have no shared fulfilment record: a judgment with a range and its tracker.
- A SADs figure missing from facts/sad_factors.json goes there with a source, a `change` note and the
  SAD version its weights are on, not inline. The `ced_<s>` row names that version (cage-to-aviary 2024;
  enriched cages, broilers, shrimp 2025). Species weights come only from facts/welfare_ranges.json, in
  the `ced_<s>` rows.

## diet-change (SADs → ced, unit_cost)

Chain: meals replaced per year × discounted years (`$win_years_counted` for a won menu or procurement
change, 1 for a year's flow) × `@sad_factors.sads_per_meal_replaced` × `meal_mix_vs_reference` ×
`@sad_factors.production_response` × adjustments; per donor dollar, split into fish, chickens and pigs
by `@sad_factors.meal_sads_share_<s>`, then the species step (2024 SADs).

- `meal_mix_vs_reference` is 1 in Latin America (the shared meal is Sinergia's canteen meal); elsewhere
  a ratio from one published estimate covering both places (ACE's 2025 meal estimates: US 1.5,
  0.75–3), else 1 (0.5–2). Never a per-meal figure rebuilt from a meat mix of your own.
- Self-reported diet change is inflated: put social-desirability and attrition into `evidence_adj`.
- Institutional changes: `implementation_adj` is the share of planned/pledged meals actually served.
- Production response is always applied; it is why charityevals' diet-change numbers sit below ACE's.
- Count all of the charity's diet-change work in the year, not one case study (SKILL.md step 0).

## policy-research-meta (uov, wellby, SADs → ced or tco2e; past_average)

Three branches summed into `outcome_total`, each 0 with a reason where it doesn't apply:

- **Policy or research won**: units affected per year × value per unit-year × `p_success` ×
  `pv_annuity_due(1, $discount_rate, $win_years_counted)` × the discounted wait (`years_until_effect`),
  × adjustments. The value per unit-year is built from the same shared figures a direct model would
  use, or read live from the model of the downstream programme (`#model-id`). `p_success` needs a
  reference class (share of similar bills passed, similar campaigns won).
- **Money moved or raised**: `money_moved × moved_dollar_net_value`, the shared rows of
  src/engine/moved-money.ts word for word (destinations live with `#model-id`; another destination set
  with `python3 scripts/moved-money.py`); never the charity's or an evaluator's multiple of cash,
  counterfactual share or leverage. A not-yet-proven organisation takes `@meta.success_chance_new_org`
  and the shared success step (`SUCCESS_ADJUSTMENT_FORMULA`). `donor_cost_share` counts donors' new
  money as co-funding with credit by share of resources on.
- **Career changes**: `careers_changed × career_change_value` (the shared formula; years discounted;
  the employer's value live).
- Downstream WELLBYs: rename the `uov_*` rows to `wellbys_*` and report in `wellby`. Downstream animals:
  rename them to `sads_*` and add the corporate-campaign species section (output
  `ced_per_donor_dollar`, unit `ced`). Climate: start from `policy-research-meta-climate.json`
  (tonnes per year once in effect, `years_until_avoided`, and the `conversion` section's
  `air_pollution_weight` = fuel share × the fuel's smoke relative to the average, sourced, and
  `extra_deaths_per_tco2e`; normalization.md 2.5).
- Past spending over past wins is a past average: the per-donor-dollar row takes `marginal_vs_past_average`.
- These models are usually dominated by judgment; say so in the summary.
