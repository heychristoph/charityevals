### Summary

Evergreen Collaborative is a US climate policy group that drafts federal and state climate legislation and pushes for it to be carried out. Giving Green estimated in 2022 that its work on implementing the Inflation Reduction Act (IRA) would keep a tonne of CO₂-equivalent out of the air for about $4.88. On our scale that is **<span data-figure="headline" data-model="evergreen-collaborative-giving-green">0.031</span> years of healthy life per $1,000**: we count these tonnes, mostly from US power plants, for their heat deaths only, which come decades from now and are discounted like any future good.

The model reproduces all 7 of Giving Green's calculated figures in its spreadsheet.

### What the charity does

Evergreen grew out of Governor Jay Inslee's 2020 climate campaign. It writes detailed climate policy (its plans fed into the IRA's clean-energy tax credits), lobbies Congress and the administration, and since 2022 works on making sure the IRA's money is spent well, at federal and state level.

### How the impact happens

Giving Green's back-of-the-envelope chain:

1. The IRA's climate provisions are about $369 billion and are projected to cut about 6.3 billion tonnes by 2032 (Princeton's ZERO Lab): about $59 a tonne.
2. Evergreen is likely to work on about $180 billion of those provisions.
3. Its influence is guessed at 0.25% of that money: about $450 million of IRA spending done well that otherwise wouldn't be, worth 7.7 million tonnes at the IRA's average cost.
4. Half of the $7.5 million budget Evergreen aimed for (its 2022 budget was $5 million) goes to implementation each year, over the IRA's decade.

### How the model works

Giving Green's rows come first, ending at its bottom line in dollars per tonne, followed by its comparisons with an offset price and with its $1-a-tonne benchmark. Our steps follow:

- **Tonnes per dollar:** one over the cost per tonne.
- **Tonnes counted for heat deaths only:** the shared climate rate, "Deaths caused by one more tonne of CO₂", counts deaths from heat and from the fine particles that come with burning fossil fuels, at the world average. Most of the IRA's cuts come from US power plants, and the IRA's health modelling (Energy Innovation and Resources for the Future, 2022) finds about a fortieth to a hundred-and-fiftieth of that average smoke deaths per tonne, so the model reports tonnes averted without burning less fuel, which the site values for their heat deaths only.
- **Staff pay:** with credit by share of resources on, staff's forgone pay counts as part of the cost.

The site turns these tonnes into years of healthy life through the heat deaths they avoid: 23% of "Deaths caused by one more tonne of CO₂", each valued like any adult's death, discounted at "Next year's good counts less by" over "Years until the heat deaths from a tonne of CO₂".

### What drives the result

- **Evergreen's influence on IRA implementation** (0.25%), Giving Green's guess, and the result moves in proportion. Giving Green gives no range; ours runs from 0.05% to the 0.5% in Giving Green's draft.
- **The IRA's average cost per tonne**, which assumes the money Evergreen affects is as effective as the IRA as a whole.
- **Deaths caused by one more tonne of CO₂** and the discounting of heat deaths, values you set.

### Where we differ from Giving Green

- **Giving Green's published figure, not its sheet's.** Giving Green's spreadsheet leaves out the ×10 its own note and write-up describe: it divides by one year of Evergreen's implementation budget ($3.75 million) and gets $0.49 a tonne, while the note on that cell says "multiplied by 10 years of activity" and the write-up reports $4.88, the same calculation with ten years. Our view uses Giving Green's $4.88; the evaluator view keeps the sheet's cells so they reproduce.
- **Tonnes become healthy years** through the shared climate rate. Giving Green stops at dollars per tonne.
- **US power tonnes count for their heat deaths only**, a lower bound: with the IRA's own estimates of air-pollution deaths avoided, the result would be about 1.2–1.9 times higher.
- **Future deaths are discounted.** Like every model on the site, ours discounts future good at the shared rate.
- **Staff pay:** with credit by share of resources, staff's forgone pay (our judgment for policy work) counts as an in-kind contribution.

### Biggest uncertainties

- Whether better implementation work changes how much of the IRA's money cuts emissions, and by how much: Giving Green calls its 0.25% a conservative but highly uncertain guess.
- Since 2025 much of the IRA's clean-energy money has been cut back or repealed, which this 2022 estimate doesn't reflect.
- Giving Green's 2021 estimate for Evergreen ($0.54 a tonne, from its work on the Clean Electricity Performance Program and clean-energy tax credits) is also on the site, but no longer counts: Giving Green replaced it with this one.

### What isn't modelled

Evergreen's state-level policy work, its work on permitting reform and other legislation, and benefits of the IRA other than lower emissions.
