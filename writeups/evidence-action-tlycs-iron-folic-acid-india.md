### Summary

Evidence Action helps Indian states give school-aged children and adolescents a weekly iron and folic acid tablet to prevent anaemia. The Life You Can Save counts the anaemia cases and deaths Evidence Action estimates it averted in 2024, at $70 per DALY; valuing the deaths the site's way and adding the later gains of less anaemia, that is **{{result}} years of healthy life per 1,000 dollars**.

The model reproduces every cell of The Life You Can Save's sheet. The Life You Can Save calls its figures highly approximate.

### What the charity does

Equal Vitamin Access is technical assistance: Evidence Action helps state governments plan, supply and monitor the weekly tablets given in schools and anganwadi centres. In 2024 the states reached 35.8 million children; Evidence Action credits 4.7 million of them to its support, at a cost of 2.5 million dollars.

### How the impact happens

1. **More children take the tablets** where the states' programmes run better.
2. **Less anaemia:** Evidence Action estimates 1.0 million cases averted, about 22% of the extra children; The Life You Can Save splits them into mild, moderate and severe by India's survey prevalence and counts each for a year.
3. **Fewer deaths:** 50 babies' deaths from neural tube defects averted through the folic acid young women take before pregnancy (Evidence Action's estimate).

### How the model works

The Life You Can Save's sheet comes first, ending at its cost per DALY. Our steps follow:

- **Deaths valued the site's way:** these are newborns' deaths, valued like every newborn's death on the site.
- **Later gains:** less anaemia helps children learn, which shows up in later earnings; the sheet counts only health. Our judgment adds 50% (0 to 150%) of the health years as a side effect.
- **Costs:** 10% central costs (our judgment), and with credit by share of resources on, staff's pay below market and the states' cost of the extra tablets (about 20 cents a child, our judgment).

### What drives the result

- The anaemia cases averted per child, from Evidence Action's model.
- How many children are reached because of Evidence Action rather than the states alone.
- How much less anaemia is worth beyond health.

### Where we differ from The Life You Can Save

- **Deaths:** valued by the site's weight for a newborn's death, not 29.2 discounted years of life.
- **Later gains** and **central costs**, steps the sheet leaves out.

### Biggest uncertainties

- The 4.7 million extra children are Evidence Action's own estimate against the states' coverage in 2020-21.
- Whether a year of anaemia averted per case holds for weekly tablets taken irregularly.

### What isn't modelled

- Effects on the states' other nutrition programmes.
