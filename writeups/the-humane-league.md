## Summary

The Humane League (THL) gets food companies to honour the cage-free and broiler-welfare pledges they have already made, by tracking progress publicly and campaigning against laggards. ACE's 2025 analysis covers two accountability programs, one global cage-free and one for Better Chicken Commitment pledges in the UK and EU, and works out to **86 SADs averted per dollar**. With our salary-sacrifice adjustment that's **63 per dollar you give, about 16 times GiveDirectly**. Converted with our default exchange rates between suffering-adjusted days (SADs) and GiveWell's units of value, which is where most of the uncertainty is.

The model reproduces all 40 of ACE's intermediate figures.

## How the impact happens

1. Companies covering about 114 million hens a year have cage-free pledges in THL's global program; some report progress, some don't.
2. THL's pressure brings implementation forward by some years. ACE estimates how many, separately for companies that report progress and those that don't.
3. Each hen moved out of a cage avoids about 7.9 SADs (Ambitious Impact's estimate).
4. ACE discounts for other organisations' contributions, for the share of the impact that would have happened anyway, and for the time value of future benefits.

## What drives the result

After the cross-cause exchange rates and your weight on animal suffering, the largest factual levers are the **share of impact ACE attributes to the years THL brings forward**, and the **implementation dates** companies would otherwise have reached.

## Where we differ from ACE

- **Salary sacrifice (on by default).** We guess staff are 65% of program cost and earn 35% less than in comparable for-profit jobs, so the forgone pay is an in-kind donation of about 35% of cash spending. That leaves each donated dollar about 74% of the credit ACE gives it. Both numbers are guesses with wide ranges; replace them with the charity's real figures when we have them.
- **Credit for shared outcomes.** ACE's discounts for other organisations' contributions are built into its inputs here, so the full-credit switch doesn't change this model yet.

Switch to ACE's model at the top of the page to see ACE's figures exactly.

## Biggest uncertainties

- How much earlier companies comply because of THL rather than other pressure.
- Several inputs are confidential data THL shared with ACE; the sheet shows them but they can't be checked independently.
