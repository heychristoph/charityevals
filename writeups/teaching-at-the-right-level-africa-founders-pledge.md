### Summary

Teaching at the Right Level Africa helps African governments group primary pupils by what they can actually read and do, for daily remedial sessions, an approach Pratham and J-PAL developed in India. Founders Pledge estimates it raises test scores by 0.03 to 0.05 standard deviations, summed over all pupils reached, per dollar donated. Turned into income with Founders Pledge's own method, that is **<span data-figure="headline" data-model="teaching-at-the-right-level-africa-founders-pledge">28</span> years of healthy life per $1,000**.

Founders Pledge publishes no sheet for this recommendation; the model takes the page's figure and applies Founders Pledge's education method.

### What the charity does

TaRL Africa trains government teachers and officials, supports partners such as Young 1ove in Botswana and JICA in Madagascar and Niger, and adapts the approach to each country's schools. Governments run and pay for the sessions.

### How the impact happens

Trials of the approach have raised pupils' test scores by 0.1 to 0.3 standard deviations, and up to 0.56 in a recent Madagascar trial. Better reading and maths raise later earnings.

### How the model works

The model's one evaluator row is Founders Pledge's 0.04 standard deviations per dollar (0.03 to 0.05). Founders Pledge's own 2023 education method turns test scores into income: 19.1% higher earnings a year per standard deviation, of which it keeps 17.5% for how well the evidence replicates, from a few years after school for 40 working years, shared with the household, discounted at the shared rate. The income gain is then valued like every income gain on the site. Because the page's figure is Founders Pledge's finished estimate, no further shrinking is added.

### What drives the result

- **The discount rate:** the earnings come a decade and more after school.
- **Founders Pledge's figure** (0.03 to 0.05).
- **How income is weighed against health** (Your values).

### Where we differ from Founders Pledge

- **From test scores to healthy years:** Founders Pledge's page stops at test scores; we apply its own 2023 method, as in its Imagine Worldwide model.
- **Credit by share of resources:** when on, the governments' spending on the program (about as much as the donation, our judgment) and staff's forgone pay count as cost. Off by default.

### Biggest uncertainties

Founders Pledge's page says it hasn't accounted for the government spending the program relies on; its Imagine Worldwide model nets that out and discounts for funging, so this result isn't adjusted the same way and is somewhat favourable. Whether test-score gains turn into earnings at the rate the method assumes.

### What isn't modelled

Benefits of schooling beyond earnings (health, agency), and any value of TaRL Africa's influence on education policy beyond the programs it supports.
