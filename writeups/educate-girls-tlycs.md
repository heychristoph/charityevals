### Summary

Educate Girls' community volunteers find girls who are out of school in villages of Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh, bring them into school, and teach children in grades 3 to 5 remedial reading and maths. The Life You Can Save estimates 5.23 learning-adjusted years of schooling per $100 for this work (Vidya) from Educate Girls' FY 2023-24 costs; turning the schooling and learning into later earnings, that is **<span data-figure="headline" data-model="educate-girls-tlycs">24</span> years of healthy life per 1,000 dollars**.

The model reproduces every cell of The Life You Can Save's sheet. The Life You Can Save calls its figures highly approximate.

### What the charity does

Educate Girls trains village volunteers (Team Balika) who go door to door to find girls aged 6 to 14 who are not in school and persuade their families to enrol them, and who teach a remedial curriculum, Gyan ka Pitara, in government primary schools. In FY 2023-24 it enrolled 411,461 girls and taught 260,083 children in grades 3 to 5. A second programme, Pragati, offers second-chance learning to adolescent girls and young women; the sheet leaves it out.

### How the impact happens

1. **Enrolment:** each girl enrolled who completes a year (85%, Educate Girls' figure) gets a year of school she would otherwise have missed. The Life You Can Save counts one year per girl.
2. **Learning:** the randomised evaluation of Educate Girls' programme found large learning gains; The Life You Can Save takes 1.25 standard deviations and keeps half for delivery at scale: 0.625 per child.
3. **Learning-adjusted years:** the learning gain is worth 2.9 years of school (4.7 years per standard deviation, a World Bank estimate), and every year of school is scaled by how much pupils in India learn in a year.
4. **Cost:** Educate Girls' audited total less Pragati, 12.5 million dollars, about 19 dollars per child reached.
5. **Earnings (our step):** better reading and maths in childhood raise adult earnings, shared by the earner's household for a working life.

### How the model works

The Life You Can Save's sheet comes first, ending at its learning-adjusted years of schooling per $100. Our steps follow:

- **Back to learning gains.** Learning-adjusted years are the sheet's own unit, with nothing on the site to convert them. The site values learning by the later earnings it brings, so the year of school each girl gets is turned back into the learning it brings at the sheet's own rate (a fifth of a standard deviation), next to the 0.625 standard deviations of each child taught.
- **Earnings.** The site's shared figures for low- and middle-income countries: 40% of a test gain lasts into adult skill, and each standard deviation of adult skill raises earnings by 20%; the gain starts about 9 years later and lasts a working life, shared within the household, discounted at the shared rate and valued like every income gain on the site.
- **What would have happened anyway:** we judge that 60% of the girls enrolled wouldn't have enrolled without Educate Girls, and that 80% of the learning gain wouldn't have happened, since state programmes work in the same schools.
- **Beyond earnings:** a small side effect for schooling's other gains (later marriage and first births, healthier children).
- **Staff pay:** with credit by share of resources on, Educate Girls' staff's pay below market counts as part of the cost.

### What drives the result

- **How much of a childhood test gain shows up in adult earnings.** The shared figures come from studies of adults' skills and earnings and of how fast test gains fade, not from Educate Girls' pupils.
- **The learning gain at scale:** 1.25 standard deviations halved for fidelity, most of the result. It is large for a remedial programme.
- **The discount rate**, since the earnings come 9 to 50 years after the money is spent.
- **How many of the girls would have enrolled anyway.**

### Where we differ from The Life You Can Save

- **The unit.** The Life You Can Save stops at learning-adjusted years of schooling; we value the schooling and learning by later earnings, with the site's shared figures for earnings per standard deviation and for how much of a test gain lasts (The Life You Can Save sets no value on a learning-adjusted year).
- **What would have happened anyway** (60% of the enrolment and 80% of the learning, our judgments), a step the sheet leaves out.
- **Discounting** the later earnings at the shared rate. The sheet has no earnings step; the evaluator view applies ours with no discounting.

### Biggest uncertainties

- How much of the children's test gain lasts into adult skill (40%, from 20% to 70%) and what that skill earns (20% per standard deviation, from 10% to 30%).
- How well the learning sessions are delivered at this scale: the 50% kept is The Life You Can Save's judgment.
- Whether a year of school for a girl who was out of school brings the learning of an ordinary year; she may also stay longer than the one year counted.

### What isn't modelled

- Pragati, Educate Girls' second-chance programme (3.2 million dollars in FY 2024): the sheet's bottom line is Vidya's.
- Years of school beyond the first for the girls enrolled.
