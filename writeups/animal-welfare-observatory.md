### Summary

Animal Welfare Observatory (Observatorio de Bienestar Animal) campaigns in Spain for corporate pledges: the European Chicken Commitment for broilers and cage-free eggs. ACE's 2025 analysis works out to **<span data-figure="headline" data-model="animal-welfare-observatory">24</span> years of healthy life per $1,000** you give. That uses our default exchange rates between suffering and healthy life, which is where most of the uncertainty is.

The model reproduces all 23 of ACE's intermediate figures.

### How the impact happens

Companies make pledges; some implement them, on a timeline the campaigns can bring forward; each affected chicken or hen lives with less suffering. ACE discounts for implementation risk, other organisations' role and time.

### What drives the result

Among factual inputs, the **expected implementation date** for companies' pledges moves the result most (about 19 to 29 years of healthy life per $1,000 on its own).

### Where we differ from ACE

- **Salary sacrifice (on by default).** We guess staff are 65% of program cost and earn 35% less than in comparable for-profit jobs, so the forgone pay is an in-kind donation of about 35% of cash spending. That leaves each donated dollar about 74% of the credit ACE gives it. Both numbers are guesses with wide ranges; replace them with the charity's real figures when we have them.
- **Credit for shared outcomes.** ACE's discounts for other organisations' contributions are built into its inputs here, so the full-credit switch doesn't change this model yet.
