## Summary

The Against Malaria Foundation (AMF) buys long-lasting insecticide-treated bed nets and funds their distribution in mass campaigns run with national malaria programs. GiveWell models eight locations. This page shows the Democratic Republic of Congo, the median one: **0.049 units of value per dollar, about 5.6 times GiveDirectly** on this site (GiveWell reports 14.6 times its own cash benchmark). Across locations the result runs from about 1.9× (Chad) to 8.8× (Guinea) GiveDirectly.

The model reproduces all 93 of GiveWell's intermediate figures for DRC, and those of every other location.

## How the impact happens

1. AMF pays for nets; other funders and governments pay for distribution, so the model counts everyone's spending.
2. Nets reach households. How many people sleep under them, and for how long they stay effective, determines the years of protection bought.
3. Protected children under five die of malaria less often. Trials give the reduction; GiveWell adjusts it for local malaria burden and insecticide resistance.
4. Older children and adults also die less often, and children who avoid malaria earn more as adults. GiveWell adds these as adjustments on top of the under-five benefit.
5. GiveWell adjusts for leverage (other funders crowded in) and funging (funders who would have paid anyway).

## What drives the result

GiveWell's own 25th–75th percentiles matter most:

- **Malaria mortality among young children** in the location. The result moves between about 4× and 7× GiveDirectly across GiveWell's range.
- **Effective coverage years per net**: how long nets last and keep working.
- **How much a life is worth relative to income gains** (your moral weights).

## Where we differ from GiveWell

We add nothing beyond the shared accounting choices. AMF delivers through partners, but GiveWell already counts their spending in the cost, so shared and full credit give the same answer. We haven't yet estimated AMF's staff salary gap, so salary sacrifice doesn't change this model.

## What isn't modelled

Room for more funding, the timing of future campaigns, and AMF's organisational strength. GiveWell covers these in its charity review.
