### Summary

Greener by Default helps hospitals, universities, stadiums and corporate cafeterias serve plant-based meals by default. ACE's 2026 analysis works out to **<span data-figure="headline" data-model="greener-by-default">0.03</span> years of healthy life per $1,000** you give. That uses our default exchange rates between suffering and healthy life, which is where most of the uncertainty is. ACE's own figure is 2.14 SADs averted per dollar (0.33 to 13.0).

The model reproduces ACE's calculated figures (31 rows; the rest are inputs).

### What the charity does

Founded in 2023, Greener by Default works with institutions and the food-service companies that run their kitchens in the United States and Europe, using behavioural science to make plant-based dishes the default or a larger share of the menu, and tracks procurement data to see how many animal products are replaced. Its partners include Sodexo and Nuffield Health, whose 35 UK hospitals now serve plant-based meals to patients by default.

### How the impact happens

Fewer animal products served means fewer animals raised. ACE totals the suffering spared per engagement over five years of impact, net of any increases in other animal products (crediting Greener by Default a quarter as much for increases as for decreases), and divides by all of its 2025 to mid-2026 spending, research included. The engagement rows are redacted at Greener by Default's request; the totals by programme are public. Healthcare carries almost all of it: one engagement, which collects no procurement data, is about 90% of the modelled impact, sized with ACE's standard US meal estimate.

### What drives the result

After the value judgments about animals, the years a menu change counts, how much a shrimp's suffering counts against a chicken's (shrimp are about half of ACE's standard meal estimate), and how far production falls when fewer meals are eaten.

### Where we differ from ACE

- **Species (ours).** ACE's 2026 SADs count pain-weighted days for every species alike. We split them by species (shrimp from ACE's own figure without shrimp, 1.19 SADs per dollar; chickens, fish and mammals by our judgment) and count each with Rethink Priorities' welfare range against a chicken, so a shrimp's day counts far less than a chicken's.
- **Years counted (changed for consistency).** ACE counts 5 years (3 to 10); we use the shared "A won reform would have happened anyway … years later", and rescale ACE's totals by the discounted years.
- **Discounting.** ACE discounts at 1.4% a year; our view uses your discount rate.
- **Production response (changed for consistency).** ACE counts one meal less served as one meal less produced; we take the shared fall in production per unit fall in consumption, as every diet-change model does.
- **Past average, not the next dollar.** ACE divides past spending by past results; our view counts the next dollar at Greener by Default's next-dollar figure. With "Credit by share of resources" on, staff's forgone pay counts as an in-kind donation.

### Biggest uncertainties

The largest engagement has no measured data, and the species mix of the meals replaced is unknown: ACE's result ranges from 0.12 to 2.48 SADs per dollar on its assumptions about that engagement alone.
