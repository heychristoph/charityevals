### Summary

Central Square Foundation helps Indian state governments run structured teaching of foundational literacy and numeracy in public schools, and helped design Uttar Pradesh's reform and India's national education policy. Founders Pledge's page gives gains of 0.29 standard deviations in numeracy and 0.43 in literacy, for under £2.50 per student. After discounts for the evaluation's design and for the government's part, and turned into income with Founders Pledge's own method, that is **<span data-figure="headline" data-model="central-square-foundation-founders-pledge">15</span> years of healthy life per $1,000**.

The discounts and the step to income are ours; Founders Pledge's page gives only the gains and the cost.

### What the charity does

Central Square Foundation designs teaching materials and training, sets up demonstration districts, and then helps governments scale what works; programs pass to government ownership after about five years. It has worked with 11 state governments.

### How the impact happens

An evaluation by Education Initiatives of over 15,000 students in 10 districts of Uttar Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh found the gains above. Better reading and maths raise later earnings.

### How the model works

Founders Pledge's figures come first: the mean gain, 0.36 standard deviations, for £2.50, 0.144 per pound. Our steps follow:

- pounds to dollars;
- **non-randomised evaluation:** we keep 60% (40% to 90%) of the gain, because district comparisons tend to overstate effects;
- **Central Square Foundation's share:** half (a quarter to all) of the gain is credited to its £2.50; governments pay the teachers and run the program;
- a 50% weight on Founders Pledge's 0.1 prior, as its own rule asks for a national scale-up with government involvement;
- then Founders Pledge's own 2023 education method turns test scores into income: 19.1% higher earnings a year per standard deviation, of which it keeps 17.5% for how well the evidence replicates, from a few years after school for 40 working years, shared with the household, discounted at the shared rate. The income gain is then valued like every income gain on the site.

### What drives the result

- **Central Square Foundation's share of the gain** (25% to 100%).
- **The non-randomised discount** (40% to 90%).
- **The discount rate.**

### Where we differ from Founders Pledge

- **The three discounts above** are ours; the page states none.
- **From test scores to healthy years:** Founders Pledge's page stops at learning; we apply its own 2023 method.
- **Credit by share of resources:** when on, the government's spending (about as much as Central Square Foundation's, our judgment) counts as cost instead of the share discount, and staff's forgone pay too. Off by default.

### Biggest uncertainties

The evaluation's design and the years of exposure behind the gains aren't published; if the gains took several years at £2.50 a year, the result is too high. India's national literacy mission also runs in comparison districts.

### What isn't modelled

Central Square Foundation's influence on national policy beyond its own programs, which could be large.
