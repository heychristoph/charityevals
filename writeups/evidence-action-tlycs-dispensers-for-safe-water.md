### Summary

Evidence Action installs chlorine dispensers at village water points in Kenya, Uganda and Malawi so families can make their drinking water safe. The Life You Can Save turns Evidence Action's 2024 estimates of children's deaths and diarrhoea cases averted into DALYs, at $817 per DALY in Kenya, $122 in Uganda and $172 in Malawi; valuing the deaths the site's way and adding what the sheet leaves out, that is **<span data-figure="headline" data-model="evidence-action-tlycs-dispensers-for-safe-water">17</span> years of healthy life per 1,000 dollars**.

The model reproduces every cell of The Life You Can Save's sheet. The Life You Can Save calls its figures highly approximate.

### What the charity does

Dispensers for Safe Water puts a chlorine dispenser beside a community water source; a local promoter keeps it filled and shows households how to add the right dose to each container. In 2024 the programme gave about 9.5 million people access in the three countries, 1.25 million of them children under five, at a cost of 15.6 million dollars.

### How the impact happens

1. **Safer water:** chlorinated water carries fewer of the germs that cause diarrhoea.
2. **Fewer deaths and illness:** randomised trials of water chlorination found fewer deaths among young children. Evidence Action applies that effect to the children with access and estimates 2,970 deaths and 864,000 diarrhoea cases averted in 2024 (its own estimates; Uganda's and Malawi's are under review).
3. **DALYs:** The Life You Can Save counts each death at the child's remaining life expectancy, discounted, and each case at its disability weight for the length of an episode.

### How the model works

The Life You Can Save's sheet comes first, one section per country. Our steps follow:

- **Pooled by money.** The sheet gives three country figures and no total; we divide all the cost by all the DALYs.
- **Deaths valued the site's way.** Each child's death is valued like every child's death on the site, not as discounted life expectancy.
- **Side effects:** later earnings of children kept healthy (the site's shared ratio), the health of people over five who drink the same water (our judgment, 10%) and treatment costs spared (the shared figure).
- **Central costs** of 10% (our judgment), and with credit by share of resources on, staff's pay below market.

### What drives the result

- Evidence Action's estimate of the deaths averted: nearly all of the result.
- The value of a child's death, set by the value-of-a-life slider.
- How much the side effects add.

### Where we differ from The Life You Can Save

- **Deaths:** valued by the site's shared weight for a death under five, not 28.6 to 28.8 discounted years of life.
- **Disability weights:** GBD 2019's, as everywhere on the site (the sheet's GBD 2021 weights differ in the third decimal).
- **Pooling** the three countries by money, where the sheet stops at three figures.
- **Side effects** and **central costs**, steps the sheet leaves out.

### Biggest uncertainties

- The deaths averted are Evidence Action's own model, and the Uganda and Malawi figures may change after its review.
- How much diarrhoea falls when chlorination depends on households using the dispensers; usage isn't in the sheet.

### What isn't modelled

- Time and fuel saved where households would otherwise boil water.
- Evidence Action's scaling down of half its Kenyan dispensers from 2025.
