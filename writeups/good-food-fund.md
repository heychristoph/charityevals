## Summary

Good Food Fund (GFF) promotes plant-rich diets and food system change in China through a cooking network (Mama's Kitchen), an annual Good Food Summit and a youth leadership program. ACE recommends GFF but says it couldn't produce reliable estimates of suffering averted, because the inputs would be too speculative; its cost-effectiveness analysis "did not play a meaningful role" in the recommendation.

ACE does report reach: about 0.3 people per dollar for Mama's Kitchen, 2.5 for the Summit (mostly online views) and 0.5 for the youth program. This model combines them into about **0.9 people reached per dollar**, or **0.7 per dollar you give** after our salary-sacrifice adjustment. Reach says nothing by itself about how many meals or animals are affected, so the ranking rests on two shared assumptions you can change: farmed animals spared per person reached (default 0.18, from ACE's 2016 figures for video views and leaflets, weighted by GFF's mix of reach) and suffering-adjusted days per animal spared (default 22, ACE's 2024 ratio). Both are wide judgment calls.

## Caveats

- ACE's review gives per-dollar ratios rounded to one decimal, not program budgets. Program spending here is back-calculated from those ratios and is approximate.
- Online views overstate engaged viewers.
