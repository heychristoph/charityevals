### Summary

Good Food Fund (GFF) promotes plant-rich diets and food system change in China through a cooking network (Mama's Kitchen), an annual Good Food Summit and a youth leadership program. ACE recommends GFF but says it couldn't produce reliable estimates of suffering averted, because the inputs would be too speculative; its cost-effectiveness analysis "did not play a meaningful role" in the recommendation.

ACE does report reach: about 0.3 people per dollar for Mama's Kitchen, 2.5 for the Summit (mostly online views) and 0.5 for the youth program. This model combines them into about **0.9 people reached per dollar** (0.7 per dollar you give if you turn on "Credit by share of resources", which counts staff's forgone pay). Reach says nothing by itself about how many meals or animals are affected, so the ranking rests on two steps of ours: farmed animals spared per person reached (default 0.18, from ACE's 2016 figures for video views and leaflets, weighted by GFF's mix of reach), and the suffering behind each animal spared, about 8.7 days weighted by intensity, from the shared figures for a replaced meal that every animal-spared model uses. That suffering is split between fish, chickens and pigs and weighed against a chicken's by Rethink Priorities' estimates, which comes to about **<span data-figure="headline" data-model="good-food-fund">0.44</span> years of healthy life per $1,000**. Both steps are wide judgment calls.

### Caveats

- ACE's review gives per-dollar ratios rounded to one decimal, not program budgets. Program spending here is back-calculated from those ratios and is approximate.
- Online views overstate engaged viewers.
