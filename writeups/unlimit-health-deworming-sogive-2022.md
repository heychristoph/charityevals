### Summary

Unlimit Health, called the SCI Foundation (Schistosomiasis Control Initiative) until 2023, helps governments in sub-Saharan Africa treat children and adults for schistosomiasis and soil-transmitted worms. SoGive's 2022 analysis of its deworming work, the only public cost-effectiveness model of it we have, comes to **<span data-figure="headline" data-model="unlimit-health-deworming-sogive-2022">10</span> years of healthy life per $1,000**.

That is SoGive's figure on our scale; SoGive's own figure is £37 per year of doubled consumption. Almost all of the value comes from higher adult earnings decades after treatment, and SoGive trusts that link much less than GiveWell does.

### What the charity does

Unlimit Health funds and advises national programs that give out praziquantel and albendazole through schools and communities. SoGive's model covers fourteen programs, weighted by where the SCI Foundation's money went in GiveWell's 2022 figures: Ethiopia, the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Madagascar, Tanzania, Côte d'Ivoire, Uganda, Liberia, Burundi, Zanzibar (Pemba and Unguja), Sudan, Niger, Mauritania and Malawi. Part of the treatment goes to adults; only the share given to children counts toward the income gain.

### How the impact happens

1. Children who are dewormed carry fewer worms for the rest of the year.
2. The model assumes this lifts their earnings as adults, starting about eight years later and lasting about 40 years, based on the Kenyan deworming trial's 20-year follow-up.
3. That income gain is valued on a log scale, so it counts for more for poorer people, then discounted back to today.
4. Small extra effects (deaths averted, other health effects, drug resistance, worse areas reached at the margin) enter as supplementary adjustments.

### How the model works

SoGive took GiveWell's 2022 deworming model for each charity and changed one main thing: the replicability adjustment, which scales the Kenyan trial's earnings effect down to what SoGive expects a program to achieve elsewhere. Our page traces every cell from SoGive's bottom line through the SCI Foundation tab and the shared tabs (moral weights, GiveDirectly, value of other funders' money). Every calculated row reproduces SoGive's sheet in the evaluator view.

### What drives the result

- **The replicability adjustment.** SoGive uses 7.2% of the Kenyan effect, against GiveWell's 13% in the same 2022 model. SoGive builds it from the mechanisms that could carry the effect (weight gain, schooling, cognition), each combined with the Kenyan trial's earnings result after correcting for "economic losers" in the treatment group. The result moves roughly in proportion to it.
- **The discount rate.** The benefits arrive 8 to 48 years after treatment, so the result is very sensitive to how much later years count (Your values).
- **How a doubled income compares with a healthy year** (Your values), since the whole benefit is income.
- **The share of treatments that reach children** and each country's worm burden, which differ a lot between programs.

### Where we differ from SoGive

- **Discount rate:** linked to the shared rate under Your values; SoGive's sheet uses GiveWell's 4%, pinned in the evaluator view.
- **Value of income and of a life:** linked to the shared assumptions; SoGive uses GiveWell's 2022 moral weights, pinned in the evaluator view.
- **Credit by share of resources:** when on, each program's value per dollar is SoGive's value per dollar of everyone's spending, instead of GiveWell's leverage and funging adjustment for what governments' and other funders' money would have done otherwise. Governments and drug donors pay a large share of these programs, so at default settings this lowers the result by roughly two fifths.
- **Staff pay:** with credit by share of resources, staff's forgone pay counts as an in-kind contribution (our default for delivery programs: staff pay about 15% of cost, a 10% pay gap).

### Biggest uncertainties

- Whether deworming raises adult earnings at all outside the Kenyan trial. SoGive's and GiveWell's adjustments differ by almost two times, and both are judgments.
- The country weights and costs are GiveWell's 2022 figures; Unlimit Health's programs have changed since, and GiveWell stopped recommending it in 2022.

### What isn't modelled

Room for more funding, organisational strength, and Unlimit Health's work beyond deworming.
