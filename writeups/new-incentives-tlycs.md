### Summary

New Incentives gives mothers in northern Nigeria small cash payments when they bring their infants for routine vaccinations. The Life You Can Save estimates $347 per DALY averted from its 2024 figures; valuing the deaths the site's way and counting illness for as long as it lasts, that is **<span data-figure="headline" data-model="new-incentives-tlycs">3.9</span> years of healthy life per 1,000 dollars**.

The model reproduces every calculated cell of The Life You Can Save's sheet. The Life You Can Save calls its figures highly approximate.

### What the charity does

New Incentives enrolls infants at government clinics and pays their caregivers a small amount at each routine vaccination visit, to cover travel and lost time. It enrolled 1.74 million infants in 2024, at $17 each (its annual report).

### How the impact happens

1. **Coverage:** Nigeria's coverage of the routine vaccines rose from 2022 to 2024 (pentavalent from 39% to 65%).
2. **Deaths:** the Lives Saved Tool estimates the child deaths that rise averted in 2023 and 2024; the sheet credits New Incentives with its share of Nigeria's under-fives, 4.4%.
3. **Illness:** diarrhoea, pneumonia and meningitis cases from the same tool; tuberculosis, diphtheria, whooping cough, hepatitis B and measles from national figures in the literature and the rise in coverage.
4. **DALYs:** each death counts life expectancy at birth discounted at 3%, and each case its disability weight.

### How the model works

The Life You Can Save's sheet comes first, ending at its $347 per DALY. Our steps follow:

- **Deaths valued the site's way.** Each child's death averted counts like every child's death on the site.
- **Disability weights:** GBD 2019's best estimates, where the sheet takes the low end of each range; tuberculosis at GBD's weight for people without HIV.
- **Lengths of illness.** A disability weight is the share of a year of health lost while ill; the sheet counts each case as a full year. We count acute illnesses for about 11 days and tuberculosis for a year.
- **Tuberculosis cut to New Incentives' share.** The sheet credits it with the tuberculosis averted among all Nigeria's children.
- **Beyond the children enrolled:** a side effect for the protection vaccinated children give others.
- **Credit by share of resources:** with it on, staff's pay below market and the government's and Gavi's money for the vaccines count as part of the cost.

### What drives the result

- **Deaths averted**, nearly all of the result: Nigeria's national gain from the Lives Saved Tool, credited by share of under-fives.
- **The value of a child's life**, the site's slider.
- **Whether the national gain measures what New Incentives added.**

### Where we differ from The Life You Can Save

- **Value of a death.** The Life You Can Save counts 28.9 discounted years; we count GiveWell's value of a death under five at the value-of-a-life slider.
- **Disability weights:** GBD's best estimates (for example 0.051 rather than 0.032 for a moderate acute infection) and tuberculosis without HIV (0.333 rather than 0.438).
- **Lengths of illness** (our judgment) and **tuberculosis credited by share** (a correction).
- **The side effect** (10%, our judgment) and **staff pay and others' costs** under credit by share of resources.

### Biggest uncertainties

- The deaths are Nigeria's national gain, credited by New Incentives' share of the country's under-fives, not the vaccinations it was measured to add in its own areas.
- The Lives Saved Tool's figure is summed over 2023 and 2024, against one year's infants and costs.
- The tool's assumptions about how much each vaccine cuts deaths in Nigeria.

### What isn't modelled

- New Incentives' work outside the infants counted in 2024.
- Longer-term effects of illness averted (hepatitis B's chronic infection, stunting after repeated diarrhoea).
