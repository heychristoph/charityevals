### Summary

EIDU puts structured lesson plans and personalised digital learning (including onebillion's software) on low-cost smartphones in Kenyan pre-primary classrooms, working with county governments. Founders Pledge's page gives $11 per pupil a year and a preliminary gain of 0.23 standard deviations. Turned into income with Founders Pledge's own method, that is **<span data-figure="headline" data-model="eidu-founders-pledge">8.8</span> years of healthy life per $1,000**.

Founders Pledge links a cost-effectiveness sheet from the page, but it is private; this model uses the page's figures.

### What the charity does

EIDU digitises structured pedagogy (scripted lessons and teacher training) and gives each class a smartphone with exercises for pupils. It plans to reach every pre-primary child in Kenya, with governments taking over the costs over time.

### How the impact happens

Structured pedagogy has raised school readiness by about 0.25 standard deviations in a randomised trial, and onebillion's software by about 0.4. EIDU's own preliminary results suggest 0.23. Better early learning raises later earnings.

### How the model works

Founders Pledge's figures come first: 0.23 standard deviations for $11, about 0.021 per dollar. Our steps follow: a 50% weight on Founders Pledge's 0.1 prior, because the 0.23 comes from preliminary internal evaluations; then Founders Pledge's own 2023 education method turns test scores into income: 19.1% higher earnings a year per standard deviation, of which it keeps 17.5% for how well the evidence replicates, from a few years after school for 40 working years, shared with the household, discounted at the shared rate. The income gain is then valued like every income gain on the site. Because the children are 4 to 5, the wait until work is 13 years rather than Founders Pledge's 8.

### What drives the result

- **The discount rate.**
- **The weight on the prior** (25% to 75%).
- **The wait until work** (8 to 14 years).

### Where we differ from Founders Pledge

- **Shrinking towards the prior** and **the wait until work** for pre-schoolers, as above.
- **From test scores to healthy years:** Founders Pledge's page stops at learning; we apply its own 2023 method.
- **Credit by share of resources:** when on, staff's forgone pay counts as cost. Off by default.

### Biggest uncertainties

The 0.23 is EIDU's own preliminary figure; the page doesn't say over what period it was measured (we assume one year, matching the $11 a year). A later independent trial by EdTechHub found larger gains, but this model reproduces Founders Pledge's 2023 page.

### What isn't modelled

Benefits beyond earnings, and the value of EIDU's platform for testing and improving lessons.
