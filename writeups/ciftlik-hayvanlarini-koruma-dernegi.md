### Summary

Çiftlik Hayvanlarını Koruma Derneği (CHKD) campaigns for cage-free egg commitments from companies in Turkey. ACE's analysis (2024 evaluation, renewed in 2025) works out to **<span data-figure="headline" data-model="ciftlik-hayvanlarini-koruma-dernegi">43</span> years of healthy life per $1,000** you give. That uses our default exchange rates between suffering and healthy life, which is where most of the uncertainty is.

The model reproduces ACE's intermediate figures (6 calculated rows; the rest are inputs).

### How the impact happens

Companies make cage-free pledges after CHKD's campaigns; some keep them; each hen moved out of a cage avoids suffering for as long as the change holds.

### What drives the result

**How many years earlier companies go cage-free** because of the campaigns is the largest factual lever (about 11 to 59 years of healthy life per $1,000 on its own), followed by **the share of companies that keep their commitments** (about 19 to 66).

### Where we differ from ACE

- **A chicken against a person.** ACE's figures count a hen's suffering at 46% of a person's (Ambitious Impact's weight). We count days of a chicken's pain and let the chicken slider (default 33%) set how they compare with a person's, as in every animal model.
- **Salary sacrifice (off by default).** Turn on "Credit by share of resources" and we count staff's forgone pay as an in-kind donation: we guess staff are 65% of program cost and earn 35% less than in comparable for-profit jobs, about 35% of cash spending. That leaves each donated dollar about 74% of the credit ACE gives it. Both numbers are guesses with wide ranges; replace them with the charity's real figures when we have them.
- **Credit for shared outcomes.** ACE's discounts for other organisations' contributions are built into its inputs here, and we keep them.
