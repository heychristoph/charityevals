### Summary

Çiftlik Hayvanlarını Koruma Derneği (CHKD) campaigns for cage-free egg commitments from companies in Turkey. ACE's analysis (2024 evaluation, renewed in 2025) works out to **<span data-figure="headline" data-model="ciftlik-hayvanlarini-koruma-dernegi">59</span> years of healthy life per $1,000** you give. That uses our default exchange rates between suffering and healthy life, which is where most of the uncertainty is.

The model reproduces ACE's intermediate figures (4 calculated rows; the rest are inputs).

### How the impact happens

Companies make cage-free pledges after CHKD's campaigns; some keep them; each hen moved out of a cage avoids suffering for as long as the change holds.

### What drives the result

**The share of companies that keep their commitments** is the largest factual lever (about 27 to 92 years of healthy life per $1,000 on its own).

### Where we differ from ACE

- **Salary sacrifice (on by default).** We guess staff are 65% of program cost and earn 35% less than in comparable for-profit jobs, so the forgone pay is an in-kind donation of about 35% of cash spending. That leaves each donated dollar about 74% of the credit ACE gives it. Both numbers are guesses with wide ranges; replace them with the charity's real figures when we have them.
- **Credit for shared outcomes.** ACE's discounts for other organisations' contributions are built into its inputs here, so the full-credit switch doesn't change this model yet.
