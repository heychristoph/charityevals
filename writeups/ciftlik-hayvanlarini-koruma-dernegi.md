### Summary

Çiftlik Hayvanlarını Koruma Derneği (CHKD) campaigns for cage-free egg commitments from companies in Turkey. ACE's analysis (2024 evaluation, renewed in 2025) works out to **<span data-figure="headline" data-model="ciftlik-hayvanlarini-koruma-dernegi">17</span> years of healthy life per $1,000** you give. That uses our default exchange rates between suffering and healthy life, which is where most of the uncertainty is.

The model reproduces ACE's intermediate figures (6 calculated rows; the rest are inputs).

### How the impact happens

Companies make cage-free pledges after CHKD's campaigns; some keep them, by a deadline a few years out; each hen moved out of a cage avoids suffering for as long as the change counts before it would have come anyway.

### What drives the result

**How many years a won commitment counts** before it would have come anyway is the largest lever after the value judgments about animals (about 4 to 53 years of healthy life per $1,000 over the slider's 1 to 20 years). ACE credits 20.5 years; the shared default is 5. Next come the SADs a hen is spared each year out of a cage (about 10 to 29) and **the share of companies that keep their commitments** (about 7 to 18).

### Where we differ from ACE

- **A chicken against a person.** ACE's figures count a hen's suffering at 46% of a person's (Ambitious Impact's weight). We count days of a chicken's pain and let the chicken slider (default 33%) set how they compare with a person's, as in every animal model.
- **Years counted (changed for consistency).** ACE credits 20.5 years of each commitment (4 to 37); we use the shared "Years a won reform counts before it would have come anyway", 5 by default, the same for every model that credits a won reform.
- **The wait (ours).** ACE counts the hens from the year the commitments were won; we discount the wait until they take effect (4 years, 2 to 7) at the shared rate, as every model does.
- **Compliance (changed for consistency).** ACE's 63% of companies keeping their pledge becomes the shared 77% of cage-free commitments fulfilled (Chicken Watch, North America), the same for every cage-free model.
- **Credit for the wins.** ACE gives CHKD all the credit (filed as the charity's data; we file it as a judgment). With "Credit by share of resources" on, CHKD gets its share of the resources all groups put into the wins instead: our judgment, 80% (50% to 100%).
- **Salary sacrifice (off by default).** Turn on "Credit by share of resources" and we count staff's forgone pay as an in-kind donation: we guess staff are 65% of program cost and earn 35% less than in comparable for-profit jobs, about 35% of cash spending. Both numbers are guesses with wide ranges; replace them with the charity's real figures when we have them.
- **Past average, not the next dollar (ours):** ACE's estimate divides ÇHKD's past year of campaign spending by the cage-free commitments it won, an average. The next dollar does less than the average did, since an organisation pursues its best opportunities first: like every model built on an organisation's past spending over its past wins, our view counts the next dollar at the share of the average set under Your values, "How much the next dollar does compared with the average dollar so far" (50% by default, our judgment; 18% to 100%). The evaluator view keeps 100%.
