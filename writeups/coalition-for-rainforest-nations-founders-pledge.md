### Summary

The Coalition for Rainforest Nations (CfRN) represents rainforest countries in the UN climate negotiations. Founders Pledge estimated in 2018 that its past work kept a tonne of CO₂-equivalent out of the air for about 24 cents, by bringing forward money that pays countries to protect forests. On our scale that is **<span data-figure="headline" data-model="coalition-for-rainforest-nations-founders-pledge">0.73</span> years of healthy life per $1,000**: forest tonnes avoid heat deaths, which come decades from now and are discounted like any future good, and a little smoke from the fires used to clear forest.

The model reproduces all 7 of Founders Pledge's calculated figures in its spreadsheet.

### What the charity does

CfRN is a small diplomatic organisation. It put "reducing emissions from deforestation" on the UN agenda in 2005 and helped build REDD+, the mechanism through which rich countries pay forest countries to keep forests standing. It also helps member countries draw up national forest plans.

### How the impact happens

Founders Pledge's chain:

1. Funders pledged $5–9.8 billion for REDD+ over 2006–14, about $940 million a year in the realistic case.
2. CfRN is credited with bringing that money forward by about 3 years (Founders Pledge's judgment: 1 to 6).
3. Small cuts for forest loss moving elsewhere (5%) and for money that would have come later anyway (10%).
4. REDD+ money is taken to save a tonne for about $15 (from the Center for Global Development's review; $5 to $30).
5. CfRN's whole budget for 2004–17, $39 million, divided by those tonnes.

Founders Pledge also estimates an effect through national forest plans but leaves it out of its total, as do we.

### How the model works

Founders Pledge's rows come first, ending at its bottom line in dollars per tonne. Our steps follow:

- **Tonnes per dollar:** one over the cost per tonne, discounted for the years until the tonnes are avoided.
- **Forest tonnes without fuel smoke:** the shared climate rate, "Deaths caused by one more tonne of CO₂", counts deaths from heat and from the fine particles that come with burning fossil fuels. A forest left standing saves no fossil fuel, but tropical forest is mostly cleared by fire: Reddington et al. (2015) find Brazil's fall in deforestation prevents 400–1,700 deaths a year from smoke; at IPCC's default carbon in South American rain forest (about 710 tonnes of CO₂ a hectare) that is about 0.003–0.011 of the world-average fossil-fuel smoke per tonne of CO₂. The model's row "Air pollution these tonnes cut, relative to an average fossil-fuel tonne" is 0.005 (0–0.03); Founders Pledge leaves this smoke out.
- **Staff pay:** with credit by share of resources on, staff's forgone pay counts as part of the cost.

The site turns these tonnes into years of healthy life through the heat deaths they avoid: 23% of "Deaths caused by one more tonne of CO₂" (0.43 per 10,000 tonnes at the defaults), each valued by the age it comes at like every death on the site, discounted at "Next year's good counts less by" over "Years until the heat deaths from a tonne of CO₂" (60 by default; at 4% a year such a death counts about a tenth of one today).

### What drives the result

- **Years CfRN brought REDD+ funding forward** (3, range 1–6), Founders Pledge's judgment, and the result moves in proportion.
- **Cost of saving a tonne with REDD+ money** ($15, range $5–30). Later evidence on how much REDD+ payments actually reduced deforestation is mixed, which would push this up.
- **Deaths caused by one more tonne of CO₂**, a value you set.

### Where we differ from Founders Pledge

- **Years a won reform counts.** Founders Pledge credits CfRN with three years of additional REDD+ funding and national plans. Our view reads the shared "Years a won reform counts before it would have come anyway" (5 by default, 1 to 20), like every model that credits a won reform (docs/normalization.md 2.9), each year after the first discounted at the shared rate. Founders Pledge's 3 stays in the evaluator view.
- **Deaths valued by age.** Each death a tonne avoids is valued at GiveWell's moral weight for the age it comes at, over the ages of today's heat and air-pollution deaths (GBD 2023: most are of people over 50), about 30 and 24 healthy years, where the site used GiveWell's all-ages adult value (36.6) until late October 2026: the same rule as every death on the site.
- **A marginal dollar, not the past average.** Founders Pledge's figure is CfRN's 2004–17 budget over the REDD+ funding it helped bring about: an average. The next dollar does less; our judgment counts it at 50% (18% to 100%), one figure shared with every model on the site built on a past average (The Life You Can Save's leverage ratio, the other retrospective climate models). The evaluator view keeps 100%.
- **Tonnes become healthy years** through the shared climate rate. Founders Pledge stops at dollars per tonne.
- **Forest tonnes count their heat deaths and a little clearing-fire smoke** (0.005 of an average fossil-fuel tonne's, Reddington et al. 2015 with IPCC's forest carbon), which Founders Pledge leaves out: about 1.2 times what heat alone gives (0.62 before October 2026).
- **Future deaths are discounted.** Rethink Priorities counts the heat deaths to 2300 without discounting; like every model on the site, ours discounts future good at the shared rate. At 0% the result would be about 6.5; the discount rate and the delay are the biggest levers on this result.
- **The wait for the tonnes is discounted.** Founders Pledge counts a tonne the same whenever it is avoided; REDD+ pays for forest kept after the fact and was slow to pay out, so the forest is kept about 7 years after the average dollar of 2004–17 spending (3 to 12, our judgment from Founders Pledge's dates). Like every future benefit on the site, the tonnes are discounted for that wait at the shared rate, in both of the site's views, since Founders Pledge takes no view on discounting.
- **Staff pay:** with credit by share of resources, staff's forgone pay (our judgment for policy work) counts as an in-kind contribution.

### Biggest uncertainties

- Whether REDD+ money bought the cuts in deforestation it paid for: studies of REDD+ projects find smaller and less lasting effects than the $15-a-tonne figure assumes.
- How much of REDD+ would have happened without CfRN, which is Founders Pledge's judgment.
- The analysis is from 2018 and looks back at 2004–17; what a donation buys now isn't estimated.

### What isn't modelled

Forest benefits other than carbon (rainfall, biodiversity, livelihoods), and CfRN's later work on carbon markets and registries, which Founders Pledge estimated in 2018 as a forward-looking project ($0.12 a tonne) that we haven't imported.
