### Summary

The Coalition for Rainforest Nations (CfRN) represents rainforest countries in the UN climate negotiations. Founders Pledge estimated in 2018 that its past work kept a tonne of CO₂-equivalent out of the air for about 24 cents, by bringing forward money that pays countries to protect forests. On our scale that is **<span data-figure="headline" data-model="coalition-for-rainforest-nations-founders-pledge">6.5</span> years of healthy life per $1,000**.

The model reproduces all 7 of Founders Pledge's calculated figures in its spreadsheet.

### What the charity does

CfRN is a small diplomatic organisation. It put "reducing emissions from deforestation" on the UN agenda in 2005 and helped build REDD+, the mechanism through which rich countries pay forest countries to keep forests standing. It also helps member countries draw up national forest plans.

### How the impact happens

Founders Pledge's chain:

1. Funders pledged $5–9.8 billion for REDD+ over 2006–14, about $940 million a year in the realistic case.
2. CfRN is credited with bringing that money forward by about 3 years (Founders Pledge's judgment: 1 to 6).
3. Small cuts for forest loss moving elsewhere (5%) and for money that would have come later anyway (10%).
4. REDD+ money is taken to save a tonne for about $15 (from the Center for Global Development's review; $5 to $30).
5. CfRN's whole budget for 2004–17, $39 million, divided by those tonnes.

Founders Pledge also estimates an effect through national forest plans but leaves it out of its total, as do we.

### How the model works

Founders Pledge's rows come first, ending at its bottom line in dollars per tonne. Our steps follow:

- **Tonnes per dollar:** one over the cost per tonne.
- **Forest tonnes without fuel smoke:** the shared climate rate, "Deaths caused by one more tonne of CO₂", counts deaths from heat and from the fine particles that come with burning fossil fuels. A forest left standing comes with no fuel smoke, so each tonne counts only for its heat deaths: about a quarter of a fossil tonne.
- **Staff pay:** with credit by share of resources on, staff's forgone pay counts as part of the cost.

The site turns tonnes into years of healthy life with the shared climate rate.

### What drives the result

- **Years CfRN brought REDD+ funding forward** (3, range 1–6), Founders Pledge's judgment, and the result moves in proportion.
- **Cost of saving a tonne with REDD+ money** ($15, range $5–30). Later evidence on how much REDD+ payments actually reduced deforestation is mixed, which would push this up.
- **Deaths caused by one more tonne of CO₂**, a value you set.

### Where we differ from Founders Pledge

- **Tonnes become healthy years** through the shared climate rate. Founders Pledge stops at dollars per tonne.
- **Forest tonnes count for their heat deaths only**, about a quarter of a tonne, because a standing forest cuts no fuel smoke.
- **Staff pay:** with credit by share of resources, staff's forgone pay (our judgment for policy work) counts as an in-kind contribution.

### Biggest uncertainties

- Whether REDD+ money bought the cuts in deforestation it paid for: studies of REDD+ projects find smaller and less lasting effects than the $15-a-tonne figure assumes.
- How much of REDD+ would have happened without CfRN, which is Founders Pledge's judgment.
- The analysis is from 2018 and looks back at 2004–17; what a donation buys now isn't estimated.

### What isn't modelled

Forest benefits other than carbon (rainfall, biodiversity, livelihoods), and CfRN's later work on carbon markets and registries, which Founders Pledge estimated in 2018 as a forward-looking project ($0.12 a tonne) that we haven't imported.
