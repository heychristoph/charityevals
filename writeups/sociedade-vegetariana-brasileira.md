## Summary

Sociedade Vegetariana Brasileira (SVB) works with Brazilian schools and public institutions to replace some meat meals with plant-based ones. ACE's 2025 analysis of the institutional program works out to **9.7 SADs averted per dollar**, or **7.2 per dollar you give, about 1.8 times GiveDirectly**. Converted with our default exchange rates between suffering-adjusted days (SADs) and GiveWell's units of value, which is where most of the uncertainty is.

The model reproduces all 11 of ACE's intermediate figures.

## How the impact happens

Institutions commit to serving plant-based meals; each replaced meal means fewer animals raised, weighted by the suffering in their lives; the change lasts some years.

## What drives the result

**Years of impact**, how long institutions keep the change, is the largest factual lever (about 1.2× to 2.7× GiveDirectly on its own).

## What isn't modelled

SVB's other programs aren't in ACE's analysis, and are left out rather than assumed to be zero or equal.

## Where we differ from ACE

- **Salary sacrifice (on by default).** We guess staff are 65% of program cost and earn 35% less than in comparable for-profit jobs, so the forgone pay is an in-kind donation of about 35% of cash spending. That leaves each donated dollar about 74% of the credit ACE gives it. Both numbers are guesses with wide ranges; replace them with the charity's real figures when we have them.
- **Credit for shared outcomes.** ACE's discounts for other organisations' contributions are built into its inputs here, so the full-credit switch doesn't change this model yet.
