### Summary

Dogs Trust rehomes dogs across the UK and Ireland, helps owners with problem behaviour, fosters the dogs of people escaping domestic abuse, campaigns for dog welfare laws and, through its sister charity Worldwide Veterinary Service (WVS), treats, neuters and vaccinates dogs overseas. Our model of everything but rabies control comes to **<span data-figure="headline" data-model="dogs-trust-charityevals">0.00003</span> years of healthy life per $1,000**, far below other animal charities on the site: care for one dog at a time reaches few animals per dollar. Its rabies vaccination, modelled separately, comes to **<span data-figure="headline" data-model="dogs-trust-charityevals-rabies">0.26</span> years of healthy life per $1,000**, by preventing human deaths.

### What the charity does

In 2025 Dogs Trust spent £162.8m: £64.4m on rehoming (about 10,000 dogs rehomed, 3,546 fostered), £20.0m on prevention (a behaviour support line for 15,639 dogs, Dog School until August, one-to-one coaching), £6.5m on publicity and campaigns, £26.1m on international work and £45.9m on raising funds. Overseas, WVS vaccinated 2.2 million dogs against rabies, treated 158,647 animals and sterilised 43,444; Dogs Trust Ireland rehomed 859 dogs and Dogs Trust Bosnia is winding down a neutering programme of more than a decade.

### How the impact happens

Each branch counts the days of a dog's disabling pain it spares, on Ambitious Impact's pain scale, then converts dogs (and the cats and other animals WVS treats) into chicken-equivalent pain days with the shared welfare ranges. Rehoming and behaviour support spare dogs distress for the years they live in a better home; Freedom and the homelessness vet scheme do the same for a few months; treatment overseas spares weeks of pain; neutering spares the short, hard lives of free-roaming pups that are never born; the 2025 import law, which Dogs Trust campaigned for over ten years, spares smuggled puppies. Survivors of domestic abuse (and their children) who could leave because Freedom kept their dog, and owners whose dog's behaviour problem the advice eased, gain life satisfaction: side effects counted in WELLBYs. Rabies vaccination prevents human deaths and is a direct-health model of its own.

### How the model works

Costs are the 2025 accounts with fundraising spread over the programmes. Reach is Dogs Trust's and WVS's own counts, with our judgments for the sterilisations its overseas grants pay for and for Bosnia. The effects per dog are our judgments: no study measures how much less a rehomed, coached or treated dog suffers. Each branch has its own adjustments for evidence, what would have happened anyway, Dogs Trust's share of the resources (volunteers, foster carers and partners), delivery and missing data. The rabies model takes deaths averted per dose from Mission Rabies' programme in Goa, India (Gibson et al. 2022: 80 deaths averted with 426,119 doses, human rabies down to zero), adjusted for WVS's other campaigns.

### What drives the result

At the site's default weight on animal suffering, the people Freedom and the behaviour line help count for almost all of the dog-welfare model's result; among the dogs, neutering overseas gives nine tenths of the days spared, and rehoming, which takes most of the money, a few percent. The rabies model is driven by how well Goa's result carries over to WVS's other campaigns and by the share of international spending that goes on rabies.

### Biggest uncertainties

- How much a rehomed, coached or neutered-against dog's suffering changes, all our judgment.
- Freedom's effect on survivors' wellbeing and how much of their leaving is owed to the dog's foster care.
- Whether WVS's often one-off vaccination campaigns protect people as Goa's sustained one did.
- The split of international spending between rabies and other work, which the accounts don't give.

### What isn't modelled

Adopters' own wellbeing is counted as zero (studies of pet ownership find no consistent effect). Dogs saved from euthanasia count only through the suffering they are spared, as in every animal model. Publicity spending has no result of its own and counts only as cost.

### How this model was checked

Mechanical checks (scripts/audit-model.ts, scripts/check.ts) and one independent audit round per model with the audit-cea skill. Round 1 (dog welfare: 4 high, 4 medium, 6 low) led to rows for owners' wellbeing in prevention, the survivors' children, how long the survivors' gain lasts, and Freedom's counterfactual and credit as separate rows. Round 1 (rabies: 1 high, 4 medium, 8 low) moved the age split of rabies deaths into the shared facts and set the delivery adjustment to 1, since Goa's deaths per dose already include doses that didn't protect. Not yet re-audited after these changes; the remaining medium and low findings (the neutering judgments, vets trained, other laws campaigned for, the rabies split's anchor, cost per dose against credit, external validity's anchor) are open.
