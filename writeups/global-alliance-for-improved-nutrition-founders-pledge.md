### Summary

The Global Alliance for Improved Nutrition (GAIN) works with governments and food businesses on food fortification, including adding iodine to salt in 17 countries. Founders Pledge recommends its salt iodization program on its recreation of GiveWell's 2014 salt iodization model, with moral weights updated in 2021: **<span data-figure="headline" data-model="global-alliance-for-improved-nutrition-founders-pledge">12</span> years of healthy life per $1,000**.

Founders Pledge's GAIN page gives no figure of its own; this is its shared salt iodization sheet, whose impact row names GAIN and ICCIDD (now Iodine Global Network) together. Iodine Global Network's model is the same analysis, so the two results shouldn't be added up. The model reproduces all 8 of the sheet's calculated figures.

### What the charity does

GAIN helps salt producers iodize salt, helps governments write and enforce iodization standards, and funds testing and surveys. GAIN estimates it has given people access to adequately iodized salt for $0.05 to $0.18 each.

### How the impact happens

1. Children who get enough iodine throughout childhood score about 4 IQ points higher.
2. Higher IQ means about 3.6% higher wages for 40 working years.
3. A year of iodized salt gives a child a fifteenth of a childhood's benefit; children under 15 are 43% of people covered, and coverage reaches about two thirds of households.
4. The sheet gives GAIN and ICCIDD even odds of having caused the improvement.

### How the model works

The model follows Founders Pledge's sheet row by row, ending in units of value (a year of doubled income for one person) per $100,000, which we use per dollar.

### What drives the result

- **The discount rate:** the benefits come 10 to 50 years after the salt is eaten.
- **Whether GAIN makes the difference** (25% to 75%).
- **Cost per person** ($0.05 to $0.10) and how much of the IQ gain lasts.

### Where we differ from Founders Pledge

- **Discount rate:** our view uses the shared rate (4% by default, the same as Founders Pledge's).
- **Weight on income:** GiveWell's exact 1/ln 2 rather than Founders Pledge's rounded 1.44.
- **Credit by share of resources:** when on, leverage is dropped and staff's forgone pay counts as cost. Off by default.

### Biggest uncertainties

Whether GAIN's work changes iodization rates: the 50% is GiveWell's judgment. Founders Pledge's 2022 page cites Bangladesh and Vietnam as evidence that GAIN matters more than GiveWell thought, but the sheet keeps 50%.

### What isn't modelled

Health benefits other than IQ (goitre, cretinism, pregnancy outcomes), and GAIN's other fortification work.
