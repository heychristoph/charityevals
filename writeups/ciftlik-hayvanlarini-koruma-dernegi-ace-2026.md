### Summary

Çiftlik Hayvanlarını Koruma Derneği (ÇHKD) campaigns for cage-free egg commitments from companies in Türkiye, through its Kafessiz Türkiye programme. ACE's 2026 analysis works out to **<span data-figure="headline" data-model="ciftlik-hayvanlarini-koruma-dernegi-ace-2026">0.8</span> years of healthy life per $1,000** you give. That uses our default exchange rates between suffering and healthy life, which is where most of the uncertainty is. ACE's own figure is 30.7 SADs averted per dollar (0.7 to 196).

The model reproduces ACE's calculated figures (9 rows; the rest are inputs).

### What the charity does

Kafessiz Türkiye asks food companies, hotels and retailers to stop buying eggs from caged hens, then follows up until they do. ACE modelled this programme only: 55% of ÇHKD's current work. Its other programme, Future for Fish, funds research on farmed fish welfare and has no outcomes yet.

### How the impact happens

ACE counts four groups of hens: in commitments won in 2024–2025 and already met, in commitments won and not yet met (counted at the share of companies that keep their word), in Migros' new cage-free supply without a public commitment (less 12.5%), and in older commitments met in 2024–2025 (15% of the credit). Each hen-year out of a cage spares suffering, for as many years as ÇHKD brought the change forward, discounted for the wait and for later years counting less. The cost is ÇHKD's 2024–2025 spending with running costs, plus as much again for the accountability work still to come.

### What drives the result

How many years a won commitment counts before it would have come anyway is the largest lever after the value judgments about animals: ACE credits 15 years, the shared default is 5. Next come the suffering a hen is spared each year out of a cage, and how much more ÇHKD must spend to see the commitments through.

### Where we differ from ACE

- **A chicken against a person.** ACE's SADs count a hen's suffering against a person's with Ambitious Impact's weight. We count days of a chicken's pain and let the chicken slider set how they compare with a person's, as in every animal model.
- **Suffering per hen-year (changed for consistency).** ACE's 2026 figure is 17.9 SADs per hen lifetime, 11.9 per hen-year; we use the shared figure for a hen-year out of a battery cage, the same in every cage-free model. ACE's 2026 SADs reweight pain intensities, so they can't be mixed with the older SADs other models use.
- **Years counted (changed for consistency).** ACE credits 15 years (8 to 30); we use the shared "A won reform would have happened anyway … years later".
- **Discounting.** ACE discounts at 1.4% a year; our view uses your discount rate.
- **Compliance (changed for consistency).** ACE's 70% of companies keeping their pledge becomes the shared share of cage-free commitments fulfilled.
- **Credit for the wins.** ACE gives ÇHKD all the credit. With "Credit by share of resources" on, ÇHKD gets its share of the resources all groups put into the wins instead: our judgment, 90% (70% to 100%), and staff's forgone pay counts as an in-kind donation.
- **Past average, not the next dollar.** ACE divides past spending by past wins; our view counts the next dollar at ÇHKD's next-dollar figure. The evaluator view keeps 100%.

### What isn't modelled

Future for Fish (45% of ÇHKD's spending), which ACE judges could be as cost-effective as the cage-free work but has no results yet.
