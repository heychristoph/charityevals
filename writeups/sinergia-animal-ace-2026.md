### Summary

Sinergia Animal campaigns for farmed animals across Latin America and Southeast Asia. ACE's 2026 analysis of its Laying Hens programme works out to **{{result}} years of healthy life per $1,000** you give. That uses our default exchange rates between suffering and healthy life, which is where most of the uncertainty is. ACE's own figure is 5.7 SADs averted per dollar (0.008 to 106), far below its 2025 estimate: Arcor's commitment is very roughly sized, and ACE's 2026 SADs count less suffering per hen.

The model reproduces ACE's calculated figures (23 rows; the rest are inputs).

### What the charity does

Sinergia works in Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Indonesia, Peru and Thailand. ACE modelled its Laying Hens programme (59% of spending): winning cage-free egg commitments from companies and pressing companies that have pledged to follow through. Its pig welfare, institutional-meals and finance campaigns aren't in this estimate.

### How the impact happens

New commitments: ACE sizes each of 51 companies' egg use with The Humane League's "eggstimation" calculator, counts the hens over the years the campaign brought the switch forward, discounted for the wait until each company's transition, and credits half of it to the 2024–mid-2026 spending. Arcor, one of Latin America's largest food manufacturers, is about 88% of it. Accountability: companies with older pledges, in four tiers by their progress, each with the years Sinergia's pressure brought the switch forward, the chance the switch happens because of it, and other groups' share.

### What drives the result

After the value judgments about animals, the years a won commitment counts before it would have come anyway, the suffering a hen is spared out of a cage, and the size of Arcor's supply chain.

### Where we differ from ACE

- **A chicken against a person.** ACE's SADs count a hen's suffering against a person's with Ambitious Impact's weight; we count days of a chicken's pain and let the chicken slider set how they compare with a person's, as in every animal model.
- **Suffering per hen (changed for consistency).** ACE's 2026 figure is 17.9 SADs per hen; we use the shared figure for a hen-year out of a battery cage, times ACE's 1.5-year life. ACE's 2026 SADs reweight pain intensities, so they can't be mixed with the older SADs other models use.
- **Years counted (changed for consistency).** ACE credits 7 years for each new commitment and 0.5 to 3 years for each accountability tier; we use the shared "A won reform would have happened anyway … years later" for both, as for The Humane League's accountability work.
- **Compliance (changed for consistency).** ACE's 80% of companies keeping a new pledge becomes the shared share of cage-free commitments fulfilled.
- **Discounting.** ACE discounts at 1.4% a year; our view uses your discount rate.
- **Credit.** ACE's shares for other organisations stay in both views; with "Credit by share of resources" on, staff's forgone pay also counts as an in-kind donation.
- **Past average, not the next dollar.** ACE divides past spending by past wins; our view counts the next dollar at Sinergia's next-dollar figure. The evaluator view keeps 100%.

### What isn't modelled

Sinergia's other 41% of spending: pig welfare commitments, plant-based institutional meals, campaigns on banks financing factory farms, and its movement-building support for other groups.
