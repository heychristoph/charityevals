### Summary

Malaria Consortium ran an emergency malaria response with the health authorities in 18 high-burden districts of South Ethiopia from mid-2024, after the country's cases rose eightfold in three years. The Life You Can Save reads the fall in the districts' malaria indicators as cases, admissions and deaths averted and estimates $225 per DALY averted; valuing each death averted by age and crediting Malaria Consortium with most of the change, that is **<span data-figure="headline" data-model="malaria-consortium-tlycs-emergency-response-ethiopia">2.9</span> years of healthy life per 1,000 dollars**.

The model reproduces every calculated cell of the sheet's Ethiopia tab. The Life You Can Save calls its figures highly approximate.

### What the charity does

The project, planned for 18 months, supports case management, surveillance and community prevention for the 1.7 million people of the 18 districts, aiming to halve malaria illness and deaths there. It cost $155,033 in 2024/25.

### How the impact happens

1. **Fewer cases:** the districts' malaria cases relative to the region's fell from 45.2% to 32.7%; the sheet reads this as 12.5 fewer cases per 100 people, 214,150 cases.
2. **Fewer deaths:** case fatality fell from 0.01% to zero, so 21 deaths averted.
3. **Fewer admissions:** the admission rate fell from 2.8% to 1.9% of cases, each a severe case of four days.
4. **Illness:** two weeks of mild-to-moderate malaria for each case averted.

### How the model works

The Life You Can Save's sheet comes first, ending at its cost per DALY. Our steps follow:

- **Deaths valued by age.** The sheet counts every death averted as the remaining life expectancy at 25, discounted at 3%. The site values each by GiveWell's moral weights for the ages of sub-Saharan Africa's malaria deaths (most of them young children) and the value-of-a-life slider.
- **What would have happened anyway:** we credit Malaria Consortium with 70% of the change, since the health authorities and other partners responded in the same districts.
- **Staff pay:** with credit by share of resources on, staff's pay below market counts as part of the cost.

### What drives the result

- **How much of the fall the project caused.** The figures are before and after, with no comparison districts.
- **Deaths averted:** a change in case fatality from 0.01% to zero across 214,000 cases.
- **The value of a death averted**, most of the healthy years gained.

### Where we differ from The Life You Can Save

- **Deaths valued by age** with the site's moral weights and the ages of malaria deaths, rather than discounted life expectancy at 25 (21.3 years in the sheet).
- **What would have happened anyway** (70%, our judgment) and **staff pay**: steps the sheet leaves out.

### Biggest uncertainties

- The indicator compares the districts with the region; reading its fall as a fall in cases across the whole population is The Life You Can Save's step.
- The case fatality figures are rounded to 0.01%, so the deaths averted are very uncertain.

### What isn't modelled

- The project's longer-term strengthening of the districts' health system.
- Income gained from less illness.
