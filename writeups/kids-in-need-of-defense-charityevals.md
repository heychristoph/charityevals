### Summary

Kids in Need of Defense (KIND) gives unaccompanied migrant children free lawyers in US immigration proceedings and supports children on the move in Mexico, Central America and Europe. Our model of its legal services and regional work in 2024 comes to **<span data-figure="headline" data-model="kids-in-need-of-defense-charityevals">0.013</span> years of healthy life per 1,000 dollars**, well below the site's global-health median. Its advocacy, modelled separately, comes to **<span data-figure="headline" data-model="kids-in-need-of-defense-charityevals-advocacy">0.016</span>**.

### What the charity does

KIND was founded in 2008 by Microsoft and Angelina Jolie. In 2024 it spent $83.1 million of its own money: $55.3 million on legal services, $7.6 million on regional and international work and $4.2 million on advocacy and communications. On top of that, its pro bono partners gave legal work it values at $137.5 million at law-firm rates. Federal contracts paid for about two thirds of its own spending. KIND served 8,500 children from 16 US offices and about 4,500 more in Mexico and Europe. It closed 580 US cases with permanent relief: asylum, a green card, or a T or U visa. Most of its clients come from Guatemala, Honduras and El Salvador.

### How the impact happens

A child who arrives alone faces a deportation case, usually without a right to a free lawyer. With a lawyer, most children win the right to stay; alone, few do. Permanent status matters in two ways. Some children left without it would be removed to the poverty and gang violence they fled. The rest would stay in the US undocumented, earning less and living under the threat of removal.

### How the model works

- **Cost:** the audited 2024 accounts. Donated legal services are left out; management and fundraising are spread over the programmes.
- **US casework:** the 580 relief cases, scaled to 2024's level of work, because the cases ran over years when KIND was smaller. Each relief is valued in two parts:
  - for the 30% judged to be removed without status, a life at US instead of home consumption for 40 years, discounted, and three years of danger at home avoided;
  - for the rest, status instead of none: 8% more consumption.
- **Central American Minors:** of 323 people helped to apply for refugee status from Central America, a judged 15% reach the US, valued the same way.
- **Psychosocial support:** 2,366 children in Mexico and from Ukraine, valued in wellbeing from trials of psychosocial programmes for children in crises.
- **Advocacy:** a past average over 2023-2024, with two wins: KIND's share as one of five counsel in the J.O.P. v. DHS settlement on children's asylum claims, and the District of Columbia's 2024 guardianship act. Each status gained is valued as in the casework.
- **Adjustments:**
  - evidence;
  - relief that would have come anyway: other lawyers, or children winning alone;
  - credit shared with pro bono lawyers (their time at their own wage), governments and co-counsel;
  - the wait until relief;
  - missing data: no controlled evaluation exists, and the value of status is our judgment;
  - the next dollar against the past average (67%), for the advocacy.

### What drives the result

Permanent status for a child who would otherwise be removed is worth a lot, about 45 units of value. But most children left without status are never removed, so status is worth about 15 units of value on average. KIND's own spending comes to about $100,000 for each permanent relief won, and about half of that relief would have come anyway. The advocacy result rests on the size of the J.O.P. class and on how much the settlement raises class members' chance of asylum. Neither is published.

### Biggest uncertainties

- The chance that a child left without status is actually removed.
- What a lawyer changes: other counsel, and the relief rate of children who appear alone.
- How poor the children's families at home are, against what they consume in the US.
- For the advocacy: the J.O.P. class size, the gain in the chance of asylum, and KIND's share of counsel.

### What isn't modelled

These are counted only as shares of the modelled value: Know Your Rights orientations for 6,500 children in federal custody, the relief of no longer fearing removal, social services for US clients, remittances to families at home, and backlash against the advocacy. Wins not modelled one by one are counted as a share of the two that are: federal appropriations for children's legal services, the Keeping Kids Safe campaign, and protections for children in Europe. KIND's federal funding was cut off in 2025. Its work since then is not in a 2024 model.

### How this model was checked
