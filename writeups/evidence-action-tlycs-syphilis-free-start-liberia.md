### Summary

Evidence Action helps Liberia's health system test pregnant women for syphilis at antenatal visits and treat those infected with penicillin. The Life You Can Save counts Evidence Action's estimate of the stillbirths, newborn deaths and congenital syphilis averted from 2020 to 2024, at $127 per DALY; valuing those deaths the site's way, that is **{{result}} years of healthy life per 1,000 dollars**.

The model reproduces every cell of The Life You Can Save's sheet. The Life You Can Save calls its figures highly approximate.

### What the charity does

Syphilis Free Start brings a dual HIV and syphilis test into antenatal care and makes sure women who test positive get benzathine penicillin. From 2020 to 2024 it tested 510,000 women, 398,000 more than would have been tested without it, at a cost of 4.0 million dollars, including the Global Fund's tests and drugs.

### How the impact happens

1. **Testing and treatment:** a woman with syphilis treated early in pregnancy rarely passes it to her baby.
2. **Outcomes averted:** Evidence Action estimates 1,900 adverse birth outcomes averted, 1,000 of them stillbirths and newborn deaths.
3. **DALYs:** The Life You Can Save counts the deaths at a child's remaining life expectancy, discounted, and the congenital syphilis and preterm births as lifelong conditions.

### How the model works

The Life You Can Save's sheet comes first, ending at its cost per DALY. Our steps follow:

- **Deaths valued the site's way:** the deaths are stillbirths and newborns' deaths without a split; WHO's estimates for 2016 put 70% of syphilis's deaths at birth as stillbirths. Each is valued with the site's weight for a stillbirth or a newborn's death.
- **Mothers' health:** treated mothers and partners are spared syphilis's later harm, a side effect of 5% (our judgment).
- **Costs:** with credit by share of resources off, a donor's dollar is credited with Evidence Action's own spending's outcome, so the Global Fund's tests and drugs drop out of the cost; on, everyone's money counts, as in the sheet. 10% central costs (our judgment).

### What drives the result

- The value of a stillbirth against a newborn's death, and the share of each.
- Evidence Action's estimate of outcomes averted.

### Where we differ from The Life You Can Save

- **Deaths:** valued by the site's weights for a stillbirth and a newborn's death, not 28.8 discounted years of life each.
- **The Global Fund's purchases** count only with credit by share of resources on.
- **Mothers' health** and **central costs**, steps the sheet leaves out.

### Biggest uncertainties

- The outcomes averted are Evidence Action's own estimate of its marginal impact.
- The split of the deaths between stillbirths and newborns.

### What isn't modelled

- The HIV half of the dual test, beyond what Liberia already tested for.
