### Summary

Rewiring America works to get American homes off gas, oil and propane and onto heat pumps and other electric appliances. It designed the Inflation Reduction Act's home-electrification rebates and runs free tools that show households what upgrades cost after incentives. Our model counts its share of the law's home-energy provisions and the households its tools and coaches bring to switch sooner, against a typical year's spending in 2021-2024. It values the tonnes those switches cut, the households' own net gain and cleaner indoor air. That comes to **<span data-figure="headline" data-model="rewiring-america-charityevals">0.00075</span> years of healthy life per $1,000**, most of it from the law's tonnes. The result is very uncertain, and it could be below zero: nobody has measured the tonnes its work saved, and whether the households the law brought to switch gain or lose money on it matters more than any climate figure.

### What the charity does

Rewiring America was a project of the Windward Fund from 2020 until 1 July 2024. Its 2024 Form 990, its first of its own, shows $22.7 million spent, $17.1 million of it on its one programme. That covers half a year of its own operations plus $7.4 million paid to the Windward Fund to settle earlier costs. Its affiliate, the Rewiring Community Investment Fund, repaid it $4.6 million of shared programme costs, so the affiliate's own work is not in this cost. The return describes:

- **Consumer tools and education.** An incentives calculator, a personal planner and a savings tool, used by over 200,000 households in 2024; "Why Electrify" courses and workshops.
- **Coaching and community work.** 990 electrification coaches trained in a four-week course, and demonstration projects in five communities.
- **States, cities and utilities.** Free technical analyses for governments applying for funds and for utility programmes and rates.
- **Research and industry.** Reports on health and jobs, a free database of 3,500 incentives, and deals with manufacturers that it says cut heat-pump prices for its programmes by 40%.

In May 2025 it laid off 28% of its staff after the federal government froze the $2 billion Greenhouse Gas Reduction Fund award it shared with four other groups.

### How the impact happens

Rewiring America installs almost nothing itself. It changes what households and governments do. The model has two branches:

- **The law.** The law's $4.5 billion electrification rebates follow the appliance rebate plan Rewiring America published in June 2021. Together with the $4.3 billion efficiency rebates and the larger home credits, we judge that these provisions are about 2% of the law's projected 2030 cut, about 18 million tonnes a year. Rewiring America gets 25% of the advocacy behind them, which is 0.5% of the whole law. The Union of Concerned Scientists gets 1% and the Environmental Defense Fund 2%.
- **Tools and coaching.** Of 200,000 tool users a year, we judge that 1.5% switch to a heat pump, or appliances worth as much, sooner than they would have, and so does one in four coaches. Each switch cuts 3.2 tonnes a year (NREL's simulation of the US housing stock, refrigerant leaks taken off) for 7 years, until the household would have switched anyway.

Each switch also changes the household's own money and, where a gas stove goes, its indoor air:

- **Households' net gain.** Bills saved less the heat pump's extra cost, spread over its life, at prices before incentives, since the credits and rebates are transfers from taxpayers. For the households the law tipped into switching we put it at $0 a year (−$200 to +$150), and for those the tools brought to switch sooner at $50 (−$150 to +$300). It is valued like any income on the site, against what an American spends in a year.
- **Indoor air.** About 15% of the switches replace a gas stove. Gas stoves are linked to 12.7% of US children's asthma (Gruenwald et al. 2022), which we count at half, since the evidence is observational and disputed.

### How the model works

This is a past average: the period's wins against the period's spending. The law counts the shared years a won reform counts, after the shared three-year wait for rules and credits. Its tonnes take the shared steps every model counting the law uses: the share of a won policy due to advocacy at all (30%), the share that wouldn't have happened anyway (80%) and the share delivered after the 2025 budget law (35%). That law ended both home credits after 2025 and left the rebates funded, so we take 0.8 of the law's delivered share for these provisions. The law's other provisions that follow Rewiring America's asks are left out: the Greenhouse Gas Reduction Fund's awards were terminated, and the heat-pump factory money is small.

The tools' switches take 80% for overlap with the law's projected uptake of its own credits, and 50% for the contractors, rebate programmes and volunteers who also brought them about, of which Rewiring America's own share of the cost it ran with its affiliate is 68%. Both branches take the evidence step (80%) and the shared missing-data step. The rest of the work, mainly manufacturer deals, the incentives database and advice to states, adds 20%. The households' gain and the asthma averted go into tonnes only through the site's fixed bridges, so they move with their own sliders.

The total is divided by a typical year's spending in 2021-2024. Those years are inside the Windward Fund's accounts, so we judge the average year at 0.9 of 2024's reported cost. The tonnes carry a little more air pollution than US power's, since gas is no longer burned at ground level, and 10% of them are upstream methane. The next dollar is valued at 40% of the average: the once-only win is in, its home credits are gone, and new money goes to state work on electricity costs.

### What drives the result

The law's tonnes carry most of the result. The biggest swing is the net gain of the households the law brought to switch: a dollar a year to an American household counts for more than these tonnes' share of it, so a loss of $200 a year would make the result negative and a gain of $150 would multiply it about four times. Then come the shared discount rate and years a won reform counts, the deaths per tonne, the next-dollar figure, the home provisions' share of the law and Rewiring America's share of the advocacy behind them. The result is about two-fifths of the climate median: these provisions are a small part of a law whose credit is spread over many groups, and much of the law has since been cut back.

### Biggest uncertainties

- **Whether switching pays.** Bills fall in most homes, but heat pumps cost more to install, and gas-heated homes save least. No study gives the net for the homes these programmes moved.
- **How much of the law is its doing.** Its plan is in the law, but the home provisions passed as part of a much larger deal that unions, industry and other groups pushed. Its share is our judgment, 10% to 50%.
- **How many tonnes the home provisions bring.** No model splits the law's projected cut by provision, and the states' rebate programmes are slow to open. We use 1% to 5% of the law's cut.
- **Whether the tools change decisions.** No one has measured how many users switch because of them.

### What isn't modelled

The affiliate's Greenhouse Gas Reduction Fund work is its own organisation's and was frozen in 2025. Climate damage other than deaths is left at the shared zero.

### How this model was checked

Independent audits are in audits/rewiring-america-charityevals/. Round 1 found the households' net gain left out on a wrong reason: on the site's own value of consumption, a household's dollars count for more than these tonnes. It is now valued in its own unit and bridged in. Round 1 also moved the indoor-air benefit from a share of the tonnes' value to healthy years from asthma averted, bridged in the same way. The home provisions' share of the law fell from 2.5% to 2%, which is what its own arithmetic gives. The tools now count only Rewiring America's share of the cost it ran with its affiliate, and the period's start rests on the June 2021 rebate plan. Together these lowered the result from 0.0009 to 0.00075.
