## Agents' calls

(Appended as each agent reports.)

### Feeding America (build)
- Year: fiscal 2025, not 2026: 2026 audited accounts exist but reach for it doesn't yet.
- Cost is cash only; donated food ($1.90/lb audited wholesale) enters the credit share, leaving Feeding America about 5% of the resources.
- Members' $184.5M repayments netted out of cost and of the overhead base.
- Food valued as income: wholesale × 1.25 retail × 0.8 in-kind, over the 50M people who got charitable food (2023).
- No separate wellbeing or health branch (would count the same change twice).
- SNAP application help valued net of taxpayers' cost; share caused 0.35 (Finkelstein & Notowidigdo 2019).
- Advocacy past average: $2.5B a year (0.5–8B) of nutrition-programme wins, 10% caused by the coalition, Feeding America 25% of its resources.
- Food banks' cost of $0.25/lb anchored on a roughly 20-cent shared maintenance fee, not verified.
- Next dollar: 39% (20–70%).

### ACE 2026 (import)
- ACE recommends eight charities for 2026; only three have a 2026 review with a cost-effectiveness analysis (Sinergia Animal, Çiftlik Hayvanlarını Koruma Derneği, Greener by Default, a new charity). The other five keep their 2025 reviews.
- ACE's 2026 hen figure (17.9 SADs per hen lifetime) kept in `published`; our view keeps the shared hen-year figure × 1.5 years.
- ACE's 2026 SADs carry no species weight: Greener by Default's enter as raw pain days × welfare range, so the RP-weights switch doesn't move it.
- Greener by Default's species split: shrimp 44% (from ACE's figure without shrimp); the rest our ranged judgment (chickens 65%, fish 57% of what remains, then mammals).
- Greener by Default's years of impact treated as a won reform; ACE's redacted totals rescaled by discounted years.
- Sinergia's accountability tiers' years accelerated read `$win_years_counted`, as The Humane League's do; its attribution probabilities and ACE's other-organisations shares stay ACE's in both views.
- Çiftlik Hayvanlarını Koruma Derneği's resource share 90% (70–100%).
- New next-dollar figure: Greener by Default 56% (30–85%).
- To fix later: the SAD-version check (`AIM_VERSIONS`) accepts only 2024 and 2025; 2026 SADs can't pass through the standard species step yet.

### American Cancer Society (build)
- Year: fiscal year to March 2026 (the 2025 statements cover only a three-month stub).
- Prevention is 45% (30–60%) of patient-support spending, one shared fact: the accounts give no split.
- Screening promotion modelled with research (it changes practice), not with support.
- Advocacy's share of the US cancer burden averted, 0.1%, is the largest driver.
- ACS is 0.25% of world cancer research funding, industry counted in full (as for BHF); the returns-to-research check implies about 12× more per dollar.
- Hope Lodge and rides: money saved plus judged deaths averted.
- Next dollar 46%. Two models: research and advocacy 0.0033, support 0.0036 healthy years per $1,000.
- To fix later: a cash-equivalent factor (0.8) typed in Habitat, Salvation Army and ACS should be one shared fact.

### Make-A-Wish (build)
- Family wellbeing; effect from the one randomised trial alone (Shoshani 2016, d = 0.59), no prior blend: no meta-analysis of wishes exists.
- Missing-data factor 1: the trial tested a Make-A-Wish affiliate and reach is its own count (the "measured" rule is unclear for a sister affiliate).
- Whole US network (national office and 58 chapters), fiscal 2024: $201.4M of programmes, 16,295 wishes; donated advertising excluded from cost.
- Donated advertising at 50% of rate card and volunteers at 40 hours each in the credit share (about 85% to Make-A-Wish).
- Effect: 0.05 years at full effect before the wish, 0.4 years after.
- Hospital savings $750 a wish (0–4,000), our judgment from Patel 2019's odds; about a quarter of the result.
- Household size mixes two- and one-parent families (two-parent share 0.7; the 71% behind it not opened by the auditor).
- Staff pay share 40%, from one chapter and the national office.
- Result 0.0013 healthy years per $1,000, about 1/280 of the global-health median (about $21,600 a wish).
- To fix later: the write-up's "How this model was checked" can't name the final round without changing the receipt's fingerprint.
