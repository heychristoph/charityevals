### Catastrophic-risk template and the Nuclear Threat Initiative (build)
- New family `catastrophic-risk`: per area (AI, pandemics, nuclear), yearly chances of catastrophe by size (extinction-level, one that kills a share of people, a COVID-scale one) × a typical project's cut in that risk per $1M × the charity's spending; the cut counts `$win_years_counted` years, discounted; branches for policy wins (single events) and people trained.
- Deaths valued by the world's age split × the value-of-life setting; extinction also takes the discounted healthy years of people not yet born (future generations count only through the discount rate). The future formula now leaves out people alive today (counted as deaths); conservation and The Nature Conservancy read it too, TNC unchanged.
- Chances to 2100 per area from the Existential Risk Persuasion Tournament (Karger et al. 2023), domain experts' medians as best guesses (the auditor preferred a geometric mean of experts and superforecasters: -34%); spread evenly to 2100.
- Typical cut per $1M from Rethink Priorities' cross-cause model presets × its backfire factor 0.772; a charity differs only through `evidence_adj` (0–1.2).
- Share killed in a catastrophe the world survives: nuclear 45% (anchored on Xia et al. 2022), pandemics 15%, AI 25%.
- New slider `$ai_catastrophe_chance` (3%), per the 2026-11-04 decision.
- Renamed the unused cause `ai-safety` to `catastrophic-risk` (schema, labels, CSS, DESIGN.md).
- Cost per researcher work-year $250k and losses beyond deaths 0: our judgments.
- NTI: 21 healthy years per $1,000 (1.1–410), mostly nuclear work; no policy win or training branch in 2025; next dollar 42%.
