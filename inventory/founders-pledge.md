# Founders Pledge: public cost-effectiveness models

Every Founders Pledge (FP) page we could find that publishes or links a cost-effectiveness estimate,
checked page by page in October 2026, and what we did with each. Each entry has a status: `imported`
(with the model id), or `not imported:` with the reason.

- **Source:** all 202 `/research/` pages in FP's sitemap, the `/programs/` and `/publications/`
  pages, the older full-report PDFs (climate 2018, mental health 2019, women's empowerment 2018,
  education 2019, evidence-based policy 2019, animal welfare 2019, Action for Happiness 2020), every
  Google Doc "full report" those pages link, and about 35 EA Forum posts by FP staff. Every linked
  sheet was tested through its public export.
- **Found:** 103 pages or reports with a cost-effectiveness claim or model; 35 of them link a
  publicly readable sheet.
- **Imported:** 14 models from 13 entries, in `models/global-health/` and `models/climate/`:
  - climate: Clean Air Task Force and Coalition for Rainforest Nations (2018 climate report,
    past-impact tabs, in dollars per tonne of CO₂e, converted with the shared climate rate,
    docs/normalization.md 2.5);
  - global health and wellbeing: Suvita, Imagine Worldwide, Family Empowerment Media, Development
    Media International (2022–23, FP's value units built from DALYs and doubled incomes), StrongMinds,
    Bandhan, Village Enterprise, Action for Happiness (2018–20, dollars per DALY or per doubled
    income), Innovation in Government Initiative, Iodine Global Network, Winning Start and Educational
    Initiatives (2019–21, GiveWell-style units of value).
- **Not imported (90 entries):**
  - recommendation pages with no published model: the 2022–25 global health pages (LEEP,
    Taimaka, Pure Earth, TaRL Africa, Ubongo, Educate Girls and others) state at most one number;
    every global catastrophic risk, AI, biosecurity and animal-welfare recommendation is qualitative;
    FP's climate work since 2020 uses relative "expected impact" reasoning, not dollars per tonne;
  - pages that rest on GiveWell's models (AMF, Malaria Consortium, Helen Keller, New Incentives,
    END Fund, GiveDirectly, deworming): imported under GiveWell;
  - linked sheets that need sign-in (Deworm the World, Sightsavers, Unlimit Health, EIDU, the
    discounts framework, the nuclear-risk sheets, FP's moral-weights sheets) or were deleted;
  - intervention-level or hypothetical sheets with no charity (oral health, the air-pollution
    template, the anti-IPV and anti-alcohol campaigns, violence against women and girls, economic
    growth);
  - No Means No Worldwide: its unit (dollars per sexual assault prevented) has no conversion;
  - TerraPraxis: the page's $1.10 a tonne is not in the linked sheet;
  - The Humane League (2019): hen-years out of cages per dollar needs a species-step bridge; left for
    later;
  - methodology, giving-strategy and supporting-data pages.
- **Licence:** FP publishes no licence; the only notice is the site footer, "© 2026 Founders Pledge".
  The models cite FP's sheets and reproduce their numbers as facts.
- **How:** each sheet was read with `scripts/sheet-dump.py` and written by hand, row by row, as the
  import-cea skill says; every calculated row reproduces FP's value in the evaluator view.
