Result: 0.04656 uov/$ = 23.28 years of healthy life per $1,000 (joint 0.19–2,035; 0.77–359 with shared assumptions at their defaults); cause median 5.70 (×4.09). Verdict: **pass** (0 high, 0 medium, 5 low).

6. Stale text: **resolved**. The fallback is 23, the `mortality_reduction` cross-check uses 1.75 and 6.2, and `people_reached` is relabelled. The write-up has a round-2 entry.
7. Community effect: **resolved**. It is anchored on Hawley et al. 2003, AMF's 95% day-1 coverage and Okumu 2020.
8. PDM coverage: **resolved**. The explainer states the 36-month extrapolation and that hung nets per sleeping space already measure use.
