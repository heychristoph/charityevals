## Drivers

1. **`$life_value_multiplier`** (shared setting, 3.36–6.63×). A visitor setting.
2. **`partner_cost_per_net`** (judgment with sourced anchors, 6.51–3.60×). The country weighting is reasonable: with 63% of nets at $1.5–3.0, it comes to $1.2–1.7. The best improvement would be a DRC or South Sudan campaign costing.
3. **`mortality_reduction` × `indirect_deaths_ratio`** (sourced proxy × calibrated judgment). Together they're pinned by the all-cause trial result. Model them as one calibrated quantity (M2).
4. **`discount_rate`** (shared, 4.15–7.23×), through the income branch and now the timing discount.
5. **`evidence_adj`** (judgment, 3.66–6.33×). Decomposed with reasons (resistance 0.9 × durability 0.9 × setting 0.95). The SMC peer's −5% internal validity and drug-resistance discount would make a useful reference class to name in the explainer.

What would most improve the model: (1) tie the mortality effect and the indirect-death ratio to the all-cause calibration (M2); (2) anchor `trial_usage` on the Asembo Bay adherence figure (M3, +11%); (3) fix the cost-per-death headline and the stale explainers (L1, L2).
