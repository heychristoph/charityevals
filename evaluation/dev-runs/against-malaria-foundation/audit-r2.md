## Drivers

1. `uov_per_daly` (a shared assumption, the visitor's to set): 12.3–49.1.
2. `schooling_gain_per_child_year` (judgment, anchored on Barofsky et al. 2015): 17.2–45.1. This is still the widest judgment, and earnings are 36% of the value. A second anchor would most improve the model: Bleakley 2010's income effects, or a malaria-control schooling study in sub-Saharan Africa such as Kuecken, Thuilliez and Valfort.
3. `discount_rate` (shared): 18.2–44.2.
4. `mortality_reduction` (estimate, Cochrane's CI on severe malaria): 14.6–33.9. Sourced.
5. `life_value_multiplier` (shared), then `baseline_mortality_rate` (WHO's interval), and `external_validity_adj` and `counterfactual_adj` (judgments with stated anchors).

Value shares at defaults: deaths 57%, illness 5%, stillbirths 2%, later earnings 36%. That matches the summary's "three-fifths" and "about a third". Deaths averted: 35,899, about $4,960 each.
