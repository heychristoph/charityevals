## Drivers

1. `uov_per_daly` (shared assumption, the visitor's to set): 7.7–30.9.
2. `mortality_reduction` (estimate, Cochrane severe-malaria RR and its CI): 8.2–22.2. Sourced; the range is the real trial interval.
3. `schooling_gain_per_child_year` (judgment, anchored on Barofsky et al.): 12.2–24.5. The widest judgment. A second anchor (Bleakley 2010's income effect per year of exposure, or Kuecken et al.'s malaria-control schooling effects) would narrow it.
4. `discount_rate` and `life_value_multiplier` (shared).
5. `net_retention_years` (estimate): 12.5–22.3. The biggest gain from evidence: use AMF's own PDM curve and the six countries' own retention fits (finding 3).

Other high-value improvements: the shared indirect-death figure (1) and resource sharing (2), both in place of the model's own choices, and a sourced age mix for malaria deaths (7).
