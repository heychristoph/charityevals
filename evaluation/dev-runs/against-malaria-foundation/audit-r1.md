## Drivers

1. **`life_value_multiplier`** (shared assumption, 2.43–4.53×): a visitor setting, not the model's.
2. **`evidence_adj`** (judgment, 0.55–0.95 → 2.55–4.41×): decomposed into resistance × durability × setting with reasons. The Cochrane authors judged resistance insufficient to downgrade, while the Benin and Tanzania dual-AI data cut the other way, so 0.75 is defensible. A reference class would help: name the external-validity discounts other malaria models in the repo use (e.g. the SMC peer's −5% internal validity and −4% drug resistance).
3. **`mortality_reduction` / baseline mortality** (sourced, 2.61–4.35×): the source is right, but the cause-specific route leaves out indirect deaths (H1). This is the largest fixable bias.
4. **`discount_rate`** (shared, via the income branch, 2.99–5.10×).
5. **`partner_cost_per_net`** (judgment, 2.83–4.51×): it now has a checkable anchor, but newer country data put it lower (M1).

What would most improve the model: (1) calibrate under-five deaths averted against the all-cause trial effect, or add an indirect-death ratio (H1); (2) cost delivery country by country with the 2026 Nigeria micro-costing (M1); (3) move partner costs into attribution so `$full_credit` works and the peer comparison is like for like (M2).
