## Drivers
1. `@sad_factors.sads_per_meal_replaced` × `portion_ratio` × `species_mix_ratio` (0.54–11.0× on the shared fact alone). The shared fact is a sourced estimate. The two scalings are judgments, and the portion one depends on what ACE's per-country figure means, which is undocumented (#3).
2. Shared exchange rates (`animal_moral_weight`, `daly_per_sad`, `uov_per_daly`): site-level judgments, outside this model's scope.
3. `institutional_share` (0.12–0.4, giving 9.6× to 2.9×): a judgment anchored on headcount. A program-level cost split from SVB would settle it.
4. `counterfactual_adj` (0.25–0.8, giving 2.9× to 9.2×): a judgment with no persistence data (#2).
5. `meals_replaced_per_year` (20–55 M, giving 3.3× to 9.0×): an estimate from differences between SVB's own cumulative counts. The inputs are verified; the counts themselves are not independently checked.

What would most improve the model:
- pin down what meal ACE's per-country SADs-per-meal figures represent (#3), which could move the result by about 40% either way;
- build a persistence reference class from SVB's past partner lists (#2);
- get a 2023 cost split by program from SVB.
