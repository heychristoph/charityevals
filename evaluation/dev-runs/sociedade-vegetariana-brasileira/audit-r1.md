## Drivers
1. `@sad_factors.sads_per_meal_replaced`: a shared fact (estimate) with a 20× range, the largest swing (0.27–5.6×). Not SVB-specific.
2. Shared exchange rates (`animal_moral_weight`, `daly_per_sad`, `uov_per_daly`): judgments set at site level.
3. `institutional_share`: a judgment. It is the largest SVB-specific lever, and the best value sits above the staff evidence (#3).
4. `counterfactual_adj` (0.25–0.8): a judgment with no data on whether partners continue without SVB. Once #1 is fixed, its meaning becomes "share of this year's flow that depends on this year's work".
5. `years_of_impact`: a judgment that double counts in its current form (#1). With the fix it becomes 1 and drops out of the tornado.

What would most improve the model: fix #1. Get from SVB (a) a 2023 cost split by program and (b) an annual meal count for 2023 with its counting method (planned or served, which partners are included). Find any evidence on whether municipalities keep the meatless day after SVB support ends.
