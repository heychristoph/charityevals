# Audit: sociedade-vegetariana-brasileira-charityevals, round 1

Result: 22.16 ced/$ = 8.09 years of healthy life per $1,000 (joint 2.7e-6 – 1.4e4; 0.0057 – 1106 with shared assumptions at their defaults); cause median 8.05 (×1.005). Verdict: **fix needed** (2 high).

Scope: Segunda Sem Carne institutional meals (schools, Bom Prato popular restaurants, social assistance, hospitals, companies) plus the municipal-menu advocacy team, 2023, costed at 25% (15–40%) of SVB's 2023 programme costs (R$2.84M). 2023 is the latest year with published accounts (the Prestação de Contas page lists 2014–2023 only, checked 2026-11-03). The accounts don't split spending by programme. On the team page (42 profiles, checked 2026-11-03), besides the 6–8 institutional/policy staff the model counts, there are 7 in the Health and Nutrition department (training health professionals, content), 4 in certification (Selo Vegano, which earns the R$382,157 of 2023 service revenue), 3 in events (VegFest), 4–5 in communications/content, and 2 in volunteering. Communications costs alone were R$283,772, 10% of 2023 programme costs. Several of these programmes are at or above a tenth of spending and aren't modelled or weighted (finding 2).

Mechanical: lint 0 high / 1 medium (spread) / 1 low (estimate basis); invariants on the model: none violated (audit-model). check.ts: the model computes (22.156 ced/$, evaluator view 16.2), 0 expected values (none declared, correct for a charityevals model). The 7 check.ts problems are all `the-life-you-can-save-founders-pledge` unknown destinations (sandbox artefacts). normalization-audit.ts: couldn't run in the sandbox, because the library build stops at the same TLYCS artefact (exit 1), so there are no results to report. The per-model invariants in audit-model passed.
