## Drivers
1. `@sad_factors.sads_per_meal_replaced` × `portion_ratio`: a shared estimate, scaled by a judgment (the reference portion). It is the largest swing (0.30–6.2×), and #1 shows the scaling is set against the wrong kind of meal. Fixing it moves the result to roughly 5.5–8.4×.
2. Shared exchange rates (`animal_moral_weight`, `daly_per_sad`, `uov_per_daly`): site-level judgments, outside this model's scope.
3. `institutional_share` (0.12–0.4): a judgment, now anchored on headcount. Still the largest lever specific to SVB. A program-level cost split from SVB would settle it.
4. `counterfactual_adj` (0.25–0.8): a judgment with no reference class (#3).
5. `meals_replaced_per_year` (20–55 M): an estimate from differences between SVB's own rounded cumulative counts. Verified as inputs, but not independently checked.

What would most improve the model: fix #1, preferably option (b): a model-specific SADs per meal for beef-heavy Latin American canteen meals, which also deals with species mix. Then get from SVB a 2023 cost split by program and an annual meal count with its counting method, and find evidence on whether partners keep the meatless day without SVB.
