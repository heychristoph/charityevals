## Drivers
1. `$daly_per_wellby`: shared slider (2.78–14.96). The visitor sets it.
2. `effect_duration_years`: estimate from StrongMinds' RCT (1.90–4.90). Its best guess should be the LATE shape, 2.6 (finding 1).
3. `rct_effect_phq_points`: estimate, verified (2.28–4.93).
4. `evidence_adj`: judgment, anchored with the stated multiple of the RCT (2.40–4.56).
5. `@wellby_factors.household_spillover_ratio_psychotherapy` and `prior_effect_sd`: a shared estimate and a verified estimate. `rct_weight` (judgment, with a precision reference class) comes next.

What would most improve the model is StrongMinds' announced RCT of the current six-week model, which it is investing in (Q4 2025 report p.4). That trial would settle both the effect and the persistence for adults and for non-staff delivery. After that, the most useful addition would be a 2025 client split by country and channel, with each partner's own costs, for credit by share of resources (finding 2).

Peers: 3.60 against a global-health median of 7.42 (×0.49), inside 10×. Among the hand-built WELLBY models it sits between icddr,b (1.74) and AMF-HLI (9.2), alongside the other therapy and wellbeing programmes. The rise from 2.26 in round 1 comes from the cost per person falling from $23 to $15, which StrongMinds' own figures show. Nothing is unexplained. With finding 1 fixed, the result is about 3.2.
