# Audit: strongminds-charityevals, round 2
Bottom line: 0.0350 WELLBYs/$ = 4.68× GiveDirectly (joint 0.033–180×; rough Monte Carlo 10th–90th percentile 2.0–10.6×). Verdict: **pass** (no high findings).

Previous round (round 1):
- #1 (high, evidence_adj misreads Baird): **resolved**. Baird is now restated with the continuous LATE figures (0.15 / 0.11 / 0 SD, ≈0.19 SD-years, not significant, gains around the cutoff, no effect on moderate/severe). The explainer names "Baird implies about 0.3". The 0.45 is defended as an explicit uplift with stated reasons and counterweights. The write-up's "Effect at scale" paragraph is fixed.
- #2 (medium, dose premise): **resolved**. The explainer now says ~9 sessions and no session–effect association, and gives the dose factor as ~0.95. It also cites a 6-vs-8-session trial, which is real but not in resources (finding 3). The "don't apply to both anchors" point is only partly addressed: see finding 1.
- #3 (medium, joint range): **partly resolved**. The decay range is now 0.25–0.6, with the slow end taken from controlled data rather than the pre-post data, and the joint high fell from 334× to 180×. But the joint low still stacks Baird's fade on Baird's level (finding 2), and no percentile band is reported.
- #4 (medium, NGO-partner credit): **resolved**. The $39 vs $253,271 Partners line is cited; best 0.8, range 0.65–0.9.
- #5–#9 (low): **resolved**. Bass 2006 is cited for the 6-month gap. Tong is described as "non-Western, ~40% LMIC". No discounting is explained. counterfactual_adj is 0.97 with the usual-care controls stated. The Q2 2026 report ($10.55) is in resources and the write-up gives the forward-looking cost.

Mechanical pass: one MEDIUM lint (spread: joint low–high spans 5,489×), covered under finding 2. There are no structural, sourcing or magic-number findings. ## Drivers
1. **uov_per_wellby** (shared judgment; 1.6–12.2×). Not model-specific. In WELLBYs the model is 4.6× `givedirectly-hli`, and the write-up says so.
2. **initial_effect_sd** (sourced estimate, Tong et al. trim-and-fill 95% CI; 1.5–9.8×). Enters squared through duration.
3. **evidence_adj** (judgment with a reference class: Baird per-attender, Atuhumuza SDB-corrected; 2.6–9.4×). Now well argued. The remaining issue is how it interacts with implementation_adj (finding 1) and decay (findings 2, 6).
4. **effect_decay_sd_per_year** (judgment with controlled anchors; 2.3–5.6×).
5. **household spillover ratio** (shared estimate; 3.5–6.2×).

Peer comparison: 4.7× sits between `givedirectly-hli` (1.0×) and `against-malaria-foundation-hli` (10.0×), well within 10× of peers. What would most improve the model: report a percentile band instead of the joint corners, make the Baird-relative credit consistent across evidence_adj and implementation_adj, and swap in the registered StrongMinds RCT (NCT07812311) as the anchor for both effect and decay when it reports.
