## Drivers
1. `$daly_per_wellby`, a shared slider (1.75–9.2). The visitor sets it, not the builder.
2. `effect_duration_years`, estimate, from StrongMinds' RCT (1.4–3.5). Best guess too low by the ITT reading (finding 3).
3. `evidence_adj`, judgment (1.5–2.9). Anchored with a stated multiple of the RCT.
4. `prior_effect_sd`, estimate, from Cuijpers 2018 (1.7–2.9). Verified.
5. `rct_effect_phq_points` and `rct_weight`, an estimate (verified) and a judgment (finding 2).

Cost and reach set the level and are the biggest single fix: moving to 2025 (finding 1) raises the result by ~48%. After that, the model would improve most from (a) a precision-weighted anchor for the blend, (b) the ITT-based duration, and (c) StrongMinds' announced RCT of the current six-week model, once it reports.

Peers: 2.26 against a global-health median of 7.42 (×0.30), inside 10×. Among the WELLBY models it sits with the other therapy and wellbeing programmes (1–2.3 range). Nothing unexplained.
