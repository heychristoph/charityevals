# Audit: the-humane-league-charityevals, round 3

Result: 3.610 ced/$ = 1.318 years of healthy life per $1,000 (joint 5.2e-6 – 886; 0.068 – 15.0 with shared assumptions at their defaults, about 220×). The cause median is 6.56 over 9 animal-welfare models in ced, with THL excluded (×0.20). Verdict: **pass**. No high findings remain; 1 medium and 4 low findings are still to fix.

Scope: the model covers THL's 2025 corporate cage-free work in three branches. Accountability on 131 companies' existing pledges is 56% of the result, THL's own 4 new commitments (1.6m hens) are 25%, and the Open Wing Alliance's other 212 cage-free policies are 19%. Cost is the 2025 audited program expenses ($14.62m), less three exclusions: US legislative and legal work (7.5%), broiler work (8%) and THL UK's policy and fish work (3%). Overhead is then added back, giving $17.61m. THL's split is Global Programs 36% (including the THL UK grant, 14.7%), Education & Mobilization 33%, US Public Policy & Corporate Engagement 17%, and OWA & APA grants 14% (AR p. 17, re-checked). Each excluded part is under a tenth and is left out of both cost and benefit. The year is right: 2025 financials, with reach for the same year.
