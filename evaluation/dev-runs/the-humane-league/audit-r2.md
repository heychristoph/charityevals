## Drivers
1. **Shared exchange rate** (`daly_per_sad`, `animal_moral_weight`, `uov_per_daly`): 0.085×–4.15×. Judgment, shared, not THL-specific.
2. **attribution_share_shared** (judgment, 0.2–0.65): 0.85×–2.75×. Reasoned from THL's role in OWA and the US coalition. No numeric reference class; citing the peer coalition models' attribution would help.
3. **years_accountability** (judgment, 1–6): 1.01×–2.61×. The biggest THL-specific lever. Anchor it with AWO's ~2.5 years and the Cheesecake Factory data point (#4).
4. **accountability_hens** (judgment, 2.9–7.5M): 1.23×–2.15×. Sourced numbers, undefined measure. A definition from THL would settle it (#3).
5. **years_new_commitments / counterfactual_new_commitments** (RP estimates): 1.25×–2.15× for years. The sourced numbers are from an older, movement-wide counterfactual (#1).

What would most improve the model: (a) adjust the new-commitment parameters to 2025 market conditions (#1); (b) THL's definition of "hens spared" and the countries of the 4 new commitments; (c) a peer anchor for accountability years (#4).
