# Audit: the-humane-league-charityevals, round 2

Result: 10.90 ced/$ = 3.98 years of healthy life per $1,000 (joint 4.5e-6 – 5,735; 0.056 – 99 with shared assumptions at their defaults); cause median 6.56 over 9 animal-welfare models in ced, THL excluded (×0.61). Verdict: **fix needed** (1 high finding).

Scope: the model covers THL's 2025 corporate cage-free work in three branches: its own 4 new commitments (1.6m hens), the Open Wing Alliance's other 212 cage-free policies, and accountability on 131 companies' existing pledges (162.5m hens). Cost is 2025 audited program expenses ($14.62m) less US legislative and legal work (7.5%) and broiler work (8%), plus overhead: $18.26m. The year is right (2025 audited statements, 2025 annual report, 990 and OWA review). Branch shares of the result: accountability 79%, new commitments 12%, alliance 9%. THL's split is Global Programs 36% (this includes the THL UK grant, 14.7% of program spending), Education & Mobilization 33%, US Public Policy & Corporate Engagement 17%, and OWA & APA grants 14% (AR p. 17). Every programme of a tenth or more is in the model. Finding 5 covers THL UK's non-corporate work.

Mechanical: lint 0 high / 1 medium / 2 low. The medium is `spread`: joint low–high with shared defaults spans 1,787×. Its fix is "say in the summary that the result is mostly uncertainty", and the summary now says this, so no further change is needed. It is listed below as M. The 2 lows are the `counterfactual_adj` / `attribution_share` diagnostics: the explainers already say "not applied itself", and the lint emits these for every multi-branch model. No invariant violations. `check.ts the-humane-league-charityevals`: no problems for this model. The 7 problems it reports are all for `the-life-you-can-save-founders-pledge` (sandbox artefacts, ignored). ## Drivers

1. `chicken_vs_person` (shared) 0.02 – 10.4. It belongs to the visitor.
2. `$win_years_counted` (shared) 0.86 – 12.2.
3. `daly_per_sad` (shared) 0.98 – 9.8.
4. `$marginal_vs_past_average` (shared) 1.4 – 8.0. The model correctly reads it as a `past_average` estimate.
5. `acc_caused_share_cumulative` (judgment) 1.9 – 7.1 and `acc_years_of_pressure` (judgment) 7.1 – 2.4. These are the builder's own drivers: the first has no valid reference class (finding 2), the second contradicts THL's own pace (finding 1). Next come the shared cage-to-aviary SADs and fulfilment rate, then `acc_attribution_share_shared` and `evidence_adj` (judgments, anchored).

What would most improve the model: derive the accountability flow from THL's own stock and pace (162.5m hens pressed, 7.5m spared a year), so that the years of pressure and the wait follow from data rather than single cases. Then re-anchor the caused share on the pressed pledges' status before and after the campaigns, as the share that wouldn't have been kept at all. After finding 1 the result is about 1.6–2.0 years of healthy life per $1,000, about 0.25–0.3× the cause median. That gap comes from the accountability evidence, and the summary should say so. Everything else follows the template and the shared rules: cost and overhead, the three branches, the discounting, the SAD versions, the species step, `past_average` with `$marginal_vs_past_average`, and the resource shares.
