## Drivers
1. **animal_moral_weight / daly_per_sad / uov_per_daly**: shared judgment assumptions; they span 0.09×–4.5×. Not THL-specific.
2. **years_of_impact** (judgment, 2–20, best 6): 0.66×–4.74×. Anchored to RP but cut for accountability without a reference class. AWO's accountability model (gain about 2.5 years) is one, and should be cited after the split in #4.
3. **attribution_share_shared** (judgment, 0.2–0.65): 0.91×–2.97×. Reasoned, but no reference class; cite how other coalition-campaign models in the repo set attribution.
4. **counterfactual_adj** (judgment, 0.35–0.85): 1.07×–2.59×. Overlaps with years (#3).
5. **accountability_hens** (observed point value): not in the tornado because it has no range, yet it is 82% of reach and its interpretation is unsupported (#1). It can halve the result.

What would most improve the model: (a) a definition of THL's "hens spared", or a range that reflects not having one (#1); (b) splitting commitments from accountability, with one row per counterfactual reason (#3, #4); (c) a regional SAD blend for EU/UK enriched cages (#6).
