# Audit: the-humane-league-charityevals, round 1

Result: 30.79 ced/$ = 11.24 years of healthy life per $1,000 (joint 1.8e-5 – 8,789; 0.19 – 158 with shared assumptions at their defaults); cause median 6.56 over 9 animal-welfare models in ced, THL excluded (×1.71). Verdict: **fix needed** (2 high findings).

Scope: the model covers THL's 2025 corporate cage-free work: its own new commitments, the Open Wing Alliance's other new policies, and accountability on existing pledges. Cost is 2025 audited program expenses ($14.62m) less an estimated 7.5% of US legislative and legal work, plus overhead, for $19.99m in total. Year and financials are right: the 2025 audited statements, with reach from the 2025 annual report, 990 and OWA review. THL's own split is Global Programs 36% (this includes the $2.15m grant to THL UK, 14.7% of program spend), Education & Mobilization 33%, US Public Policy & Corporate Engagement 17%, and OWA & APA grants 14%. The model counts nothing for broiler work. See finding 5.

Mechanical: lint 0 high / 0 medium / 2 low (both diagnostics: `counterfactual_adj` and `attribution_share` are branch summaries). No invariant violations on the model. `check.ts the-humane-league-charityevals`: no problems for this model. The 7 problems it reports are all for `the-life-you-can-save-founders-pledge`, which are sandbox artefacts and were ignored. I did not run `normalization-audit.ts`: it loads the whole library without an exclusion and could print the excluded evaluator models, and the caller listed only the two commands. The per-model invariants ran inside audit-model.ts.

Previous round: none (round 1).

5. `$marginal_vs_past_average` (shared) 4.0 – 22.5. Then `sads_per_hen_year_cage_to_aviary` (shared estimate), the fulfilment rate (shared), and `acc_attribution_share_shared` (judgment, finding 4).

What would most improve the model: rebuild the accountability branch as a flow with a valid anchor (findings 1–3). Accountability is 94% of the result, and the current branch credits each year's pressure with the stock's whole caused compliance. A hen-weighted comparison of pressed and unpressed pledges from Chicken Watch / OWA, plus THL's list of pressed companies with their deadlines, would turn the main judgment into an estimate. Everything else (cost, the new-commitment and OWA branches, the SAD versions, the species step, discounting, `past_average` with `$marginal_vs_past_average`) follows the template and the shared rules.
