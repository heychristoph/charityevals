## Effect
- Imdad et al. 2022 Cochrane CD008524.pub4 (cochranelibrary full text 403; used cochrane.org PLS https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD008524_vitamin-supplementation-preventing-disease-and-death-children-aged-six-months-five-years retr 2026-10-28):
  - "A meta-analysis for all-cause mortality included 19 trials (1,202,382 children)." "risk ratio (RR) 0.88, 95% confidence interval (CI) 0.83 to 0.93; high-certainty evidence"
  - diarrhoea mortality RR 0.88 (0.79-0.98); measles mortality RR 0.88 (0.69-1.11); diarrhoea incidence RR 0.85 (0.82-0.87); measles incidence 0.45 (0.30-0.69); night blindness 0.32 (0.21-0.50); Bitot's 0.42 (0.33-0.53)
  - "One large, recently conducted study, which included about one million children, did not show any effect of VAS." (DEVTA)
- Imdad et al. 2017 version PMC6464706 https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6464706/ retr 2026-10-28: random effects "RR 0.76, 95% CI 0.66 to 0.88"; I2 61%; excl DEVTA RR 0.77 (0.70-0.84), 18 studies; Africa subgroup RR 0.86 (0.75-0.98; 6 studies); high-mortality (>40/1000) 0.89 (0.84-0.94).
- VAD prevalence: Song et al. 2023 J Glob Health 13:04084 https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10416138/ : WHO AFR ages 0-5 30.59% (23.99-37.83) modelled 2019; cites Stevens 2015: SSA 48% (2013).

## Coverage / implementation
- Nyhus Dhillon et al. 2013 PLoS One https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0058629 (HKI Tanzania): survey 65% (62.7-68.1) vs tally 98.5% -> ratio 0.66
- Hodges et al. 2013 GHSP https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4168566/ (MoHS+HKI Sierra Leone Nov 2011): "VAS coverage was 91.8% among children ages 6–59 months" vs "105.1% reported through tally sheets" -> ratio ~0.87
- Vosti et al. 2025 Food Nutr Bull https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13250235/ (HKI co-authors; Burkina Faso JVA+ Dec 2021-Jan 2022): coverage 88% (survey; Kombissiri 86.8, Yako 88.6); "Average cost per child reached was 1.34 USD" (incl caregiver time); excl caregiver ~1.10 (112,517/102,722 per WebFetch calc of Table 1); per diems 48.1%, personnel 34.5%; "National costs, with international support, covered VA capsules and community health worker salaries."
- Neidecker-Gonzales et al. 2007 FNB 28(3) (abstract via search snippet only): ~US$0.50 per capsule Africa (2004 prices), range 0.45-2.25.
