# Audit: helen-keller-intl-charityevals, round 1
Result: 0.04285 uov/$ = 21.4 years of healthy life per $1,000 (joint 0.18–4,754; model's own ranges 0.74–522); cause median 5.71 (×3.75). Verdict: **pass** (no high findings). Ten medium findings should still be fixed. Together they move the result by roughly −20% to +35%, depending on how each one is resolved.

Scope: vitamin A supplementation (a judged 55% of the $76.9m nutrition line, $42.3m) and NTD mass drug administration ($13.1m cash, plus $10.5m of donated Mectizan treated as a co-funder's resource), FY2025 (July 2024–June 2025). Reach is from the same year (Form 990 Schedule O: 47m children, 32.3m people). Against the audited breakdown of $94.3m cash programme spending (nutrition 81.5%, NTD 13.9%, US vision 3.8%, eye health abroad 0.8%), the model covers about 59%. The rest of nutrition (about $34.6m, 37%) is left out under Convention 12. See finding 9.

Mechanical: lint 0/0/0 (no findings). Invariants on the model: none. normalization-audit (with `--exclude helen-keller-vas`): 0 violations in every invariant. No waiver names this model, and the allowlist is untouched.

Previous round: none (this is round 1).

## Drivers
1. `$uov_per_daly` (shared): not the builder's.
2. ntd_worm_burden_ratio (judgment, 0.15): unquantified anchor (finding 4), and no allowance for once- against twice-yearly treatment (finding 3).
3. deworming_consumption_gain (estimate, Hamory et al. 2021): verified, imprecise.
4. mortality_reduction (estimate, Cochrane 2022): verified.
5. external_validity_adj (judgment, 0.65): it counts DEVTA twice (finding 6).

The NTD branch is 45% of the value on 24% of the modelled cost, and it rests almost entirely on judgments (worm burden, child share, evidence, morbidity share). What would most improve the model:
- Measured infection intensity for Helen Keller's NTD districts against Busia's baseline.
- A costed partner share for the VAS campaigns (findings 1 and 7, which pull in opposite directions).
- The VAS income gain the site counts for every other child-survival programme (finding 2).
