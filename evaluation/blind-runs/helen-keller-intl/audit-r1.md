# Audit: helen-keller-intl-charityevals, round 1

Bottom line: 0.0254 uov/$ = 2.96× GiveDirectly (joint 0.16–37.6×; 0.26–24.6× with shared assumptions at their defaults). Verdict: **fix needed** (1 high finding).
Previous round: none (first round).

Checks run: `npx tsx scripts/audit-model.ts models/global-health/helen-keller-intl-charityevals.json` (2 low lint findings, no high/medium) and `npx tsx scripts/check.ts helen-keller-intl-charityevals` ("all models valid"; warnings only: joint range spans 240×, 80% of the uncertainty is from judgments, 23 point inputs). Effects below come from scratch copies of the model re-run through `audit-model.ts`. The model and write-up were not edited.

Template family: `direct-health` is right for VAS (deaths averted by a commodity delivered to children). The causal chain follows the template. The added rows (nutrition_expenses, vas_share_of_nutrition, doses_per_child_year, cost_per_dose, full_cost_per_dose, the 13 country weights and mortality rows) all feed canonical rows. None of them bypasses one.

## Drivers

1. **mortality_reduction** (estimate, sourced: Cochrane FE 12%, range 7–24%): 1.7× to 5.9×. Sourced and verified, but see finding 2 on how it combines with evidence_adj.
2. **full_credit / attribution via full_cost_per_dose** (accounting toggle; full cost is a judgment anchored on two Helen Keller-authored costing studies): 2.2× to 4.3×, or 6.0× with full credit. Under this formula, Helen Keller's own spending cancels out of the result. The model is in effect "deaths averted per dollar of total program cost". That makes the full cost per surveyed dose the real cost input; it needs the consistency fix (finding 1) and a wider high end (finding 4).
3. **evidence_adj** (judgment, 0.5–1.0): 2.0× to 3.9×. Has a partial reference class (VAD trend, DEVTA); needs the subgroup reference class and the double-discount fix (finding 2).
4. **counterfactual_adj** (judgment, 0.5–0.9): 2.1× to 3.8×. Anchored on Baker's routine coverage; needs a reference class for what happens when campaigns lose support (finding 11), and should stop overlapping with funging (finding 3).
5. **implementation_adj** (judgment, 0.6–0.95): 2.2× to 3.5× today. After fix 1 it should drop out of the result entirely.

Comparability: at 2.96× the model sits between New Incentives (1.03×) and AMF/SMC (about 5.7×) in the uov reference class, well within 10× of peers. No charityevals-built peer of the same family exists to compare canonical rows with. Shared numbers are shared: the under-five moral weight comes from `@moral_weights`, life value from `$life_value_multiplier`, mortality from `@<country>` facts, and morbidity is routed through `$uov_per_daly` (zero here). No inline moral weights or retyped exchange rates were found.

What would most improve the model: fix 1 (consistent dose basis for attribution); a Helen Keller VAS-specific spending figure or its routine-versus-campaign split, to pin down full cost per dose; and a documented counterfactual from countries where external VAS campaign support ended. With fixes 1–3 as suggested (and before the −8% overhead fix), the best guess would be roughly 4.5–5× (2.96 × 1.25 × 1.2 × 1.05–1.11).
