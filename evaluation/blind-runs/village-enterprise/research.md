## Effect
- IDinsight DIB evaluation, 30 Nov 2021: https://www.idinsight.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/FinalReport-Village-Enterprise.pdf
  - Exec summary p.7: "treatment households consumed 9.9 USD (or 6.3%) more per month than the control group"; Kenya 15.2 (7.3%), Uganda 3.9 (3.6%). Endline May-Aug 2021, 6 months to 2.5 years after program completion; 9,888 households, 241 T / 241 C villages.
  - Table B1 p.37: control mean 158.007; ITT 9.875 (SE 1.700); TOT consumption 10.290 (SE 1.752); assets ITT 40.490 (SE 13.638).
  - Table 1 p.12: household size 6.492 (T) 6.505 (C).
  - p.8: grants $100 + $50 per business group (~$50 per household); some Kenya BGs $300+$150. Appendix A: weighted average seed capital per household $77.14; m = 24.3 months from transfers to data collection.
  - Cohort effects: earlier cohorts similar or larger consumption effects (no decay over 6 to 30 months).
  - COVID: Uganda post-lockdown consumption effect -0.1 vs 5.9 pre-lockdown (p.24 text).
  - Appendix L: Sedlmayr et al. 2020 Uganda effect UGX 26,061 per capita per year = 3.94 USD/hh/month, nearly identical to 3.87 here.
  - Appendix A also contains IDinsight's "lifetime impact" projection ($9.88 x 12 / 0.1 perpetuity) — a DIB payment/benefit projection, not used.
- IDinsight DREAMS Uganda Endline 1, March 2026: https://europe.mercycorps.org/sites/default/files/2026-06/dreams-uganda-impact-report-1.pdf
  - p.5: household-level RCT, 6,560 households, endline Apr-Jul 2025, "approximately six months to 1.5 years after implementation"; SB grant USD 138.26 + PR grant 66.36.
  - p.6: "USD 19.10 (UGX 69,205) higher average monthly household consumption than control households, a 17.1% difference"; cohort effects 15.90 to 22.88, no trend.
  - Table C1 p.86: control mean 112.06; ITT 19.10; TOT broad 20.21; TOT narrow 18.86. Assets ITT 184.10 (control 930.35).
  - Endline household size 7.96 (p.29 table); targeting 6.71 (p.28 table).
