## Drivers

1. `$daly_per_wellby` (shared slider, 3.5–18.1): the site's, not a finding.
2. `dose_shape_factor` (judgment, 2.2–7.1): the share of a full course that 1.75 sessions deliver. It is the largest non-shared driver, and its best guess is anchored outside the data's range (finding 2).
3. `effect_duration_years` (judgment, 2.7–7.0): anchored on the trial's 6 months and HAP's 12 months.
4. `evidence_adj` (judgment, 2.2–5.6): caseness and response bias, anchored on the charity's follow-up shares (96–99% of those reached).
5. `follow_up_sessions` (estimate, 3.0–5.3): the charity's own counts disagree by a factor of 4.7.

`trial_weight_discount` (3.3–5.6) is sixth, now with a reference class. Ranges look like real disagreement, not reflexive ±. The 7,000× joint spread is mostly the shared WELLBY rate and the three judgments above.

What would most improve the model: one record of sessions received per client for 2024 (the LHW register or the 10% follow-up sample asked how many sessions each attended). It would settle both `follow_up_sessions` and much of the dose question. Next, the Zimbabwe PVO's audited 2024 statements, for the other-funder share and the support split.
