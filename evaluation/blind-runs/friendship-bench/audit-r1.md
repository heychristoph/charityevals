# Audit: friendship-bench-charityevals, round 1

Result: 0.04199 wellby/$ = 2.741 years of healthy life per $1,000 (joint 0.037–243; model's own ranges 0.093–37.2); global-health median 7.505 (×0.37). Verdict: **pass** (no high findings; five medium findings to fix).

Scope: talk therapy in Zimbabwe (benches at 385 clinics, Open Line, Youth Friendship Bench, CKT groups), 2024, both organisations' spending (the Zimbabwe PVO built from the US affiliate's Form 990 grant ÷ (1 − other funders' share), plus the US affiliate's own spending). Research and the Friendship Bench in a Box trainings are left out (best guess 10% of costs) and stated as partial coverage in the summary and `program_spend` (Convention 12). The Zimbabwe PVO doesn't publish statements, so no audited functional split exists; the build is reasonable and matches the charity's own scale (2026 ask: $4.1M for 380,000 people, about $11 per person; model: $12 per client seen). Reach and spending are from the same year (2024).

Mechanical: lint 0 high / 0 medium / 3 low; invariants: none; normalization-audit: not run for this model (it is excluded with its charity id, as briefed); `check.ts friendship-bench-charityevals`: all models valid.

Previous round: none (round 1).

Blind-run note: no web searches were run; only the charity's reports, the IRS e-file, the trial, the meta-analysis, the pilot, the RE-AIM paper and the World Bank API were fetched. No evaluator cost-effectiveness figure for Friendship Bench was seen.

## Drivers

1. `$daly_per_wellby` (shared slider): the site's, not a finding.
2. `implementation_adj` (judgment, 0.12–0.55): the dose share a client seen gets. Anchored on follow-ups ÷ clients, a count the charity defines differently from year to year (47,073 in 2024, 3,284 in 2025). Findings 1, 3 and 6.
3. `effect_duration_years` (judgment, 1–4): its anchors point above the best guess (finding 4).
4. `evidence_adj` (judgment, 0.55–1): the caseness of clients seen is unanchored (finding 2).
5. `trial_weight_discount` (judgment, 0.4–1): reasoned, and anchored on the precision weight as the template asks.

What would most improve the model: a split of 2024 clients seen by SSQ-14 caseness and by number of sessions (the charity's LHW books, or the 10% random follow-up sample). That would replace the two biggest judgments with observed shares. Next would be the Zimbabwe PVO's audited statements, which would replace `zimbabwe_other_funding_share` and `zimbabwe_service_share`.
