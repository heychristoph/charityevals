## Structure and other checks

- **Template:** wellbeing is the right family. The chain cost → reach → effect → adjustments → outcome → donor is intact. Two departures, both explained:
  - Duration is an input and decay a formula. This is the inverse of the template, and it avoids counting the effect size twice.
  - `implementation_adj` is a formula built from session shares.

  The added rows feed canonical rows. `program_share_of_total` cancels out of `cost_total`, as the explainer says.
- **Double counting:** none found.
  - The counterfactual adjustment covers only other providers, since the trials are already net of usual care.
  - The publication-bias correction is applied once, through the prior.
  - Overhead is allocated once (`cost_total = total_expenses × bench_share`).
  - Household spillovers appear only in the multiplier.
- **Units:** SD → SD-years (triangle) → WELLBYs at 2 per SD-year; per person and per year are consistent. The one timing nuance is finding 8.
- **Shared numbers:** WELLBY conversion, household size and spillover come from `@wellby_factors`; the units of value come from the shared `uov_per_wellby`. Nothing is retyped.
- **Comparability:** 12.2× sits next to against-malaria-foundation-hli (9.96×) and above givedirectly-hli (1.01×) in the WELLBY reference class, well within 10× of peers. Fixing findings 1 and 2 together gives about 16.7× (scratch run: duration 5 years, single-session 0.85), still within range.
- **Joint range:** 0.39–178× with shared assumptions at their defaults. That is very wide, but it reflects stacked judgments (duration, evidence, attribution, sessions, trial weight), not reflexive ±20% ranges. The duration range is too narrow at the top (finding 1).

## Drivers

1. **`uov_per_wellby`** (shared assumption, 0.4–3 → 4.2–31.8×). The visitor's to set, not the builder's.
2. **`effect_duration_years`** (judgment, 1.5–5 → 6.1–20.3×). The cited reference class points above the current high end (finding 1). This is the single most consequential fix.
3. **`evidence_adj`** (judgment, 0.55–1 → 8.4–15.3×). It has no reference class (finding 3).
4. **`@wellby_factors.household_spillover_ratio_psychotherapy` and `wellbys_per_sd_year`** (shared estimates, 9.0–16.1× and 9.2–15.3×).
5. **`attribution_share_shared`** (judgment, 9.2–15.3×), **`total_expenses`** (judgment bracketed by sourced anchors, 10.0–15.7×) and **`meta_prior_effect_sd`** (sourced, verified, 10.4–14.4×).

What would most improve the model:

- **Duration:** re-anchor it on the Cuijpers 2026 trajectory.
- **Sessions:** use the charity's own session counts, and ask Friendship Bench for the definition of "follow-up session" and the distribution of sessions per client.
- **Costs:** obtain the audited Friendship Bench Zimbabwe accounts (Vista Chartered Accountants), which would turn `total_expenses` from a judgment into an observed value.
