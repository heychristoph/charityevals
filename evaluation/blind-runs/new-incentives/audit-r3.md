# Audit: new-incentives-charityevals, round 3
Result: 0.02861 uov/$ = 14.30 years of healthy life per $1,000 (joint 0.38–337; the model's own ranges 1.77–63.0); cause median 6.36 (×2.25). Verdict: **pass** (no high or medium findings; 4 low).

Scope: unchanged and still right. The model covers cash incentives for routine vaccination, with the awareness and vaccine-supply work that goes with it, in calendar 2025. That is $24,998,369 of programme services out of $26,886,948 total expenses (audited), with 1,845,641 infants enrolled in the same year. The ORS pilot is under 1% of spending and is stated as not modelled.

Mechanical: lint 0 high, 0 medium, 0 low. Invariants: no violations. normalization-audit (`--exclude new-incentives-founders-pledge`): 0 violations in every invariant, with 128 models checked.
