# Audit: new-incentives-charityevals, round 1
Result: 0.02378 uov/$ = 11.89 years of healthy life per $1,000 (joint 0.26–319; the model's own ranges 1.13–63.9); cause median 6.36 (×1.87). Verdict: **fix needed** (2 high).

Scope: the one programme (cash incentives for routine vaccination, with its awareness and vaccine-supply work), calendar 2025. Audited programme services were $24,998,369 of $26,886,948 total expenses, and 1,845,641 infants were enrolled in the same year. The ORS pilot (41,000 sachets, 43 clinics, 1 LGA) is well under a tenth of spending, so leaving it out is fine. The scope matches the audited functional-expense statement and the annual report.

Mechanical: lint 0 high, 0 medium, 0 low. Invariants: no violations. normalization-audit: no violations for this model. With the prescribed `--exclude new-incentives,new-incentives-founders-pledge` the script also dropped this model, because it excludes by charity id too and this model's charity is `new-incentives` (the run printed "Excluded 1 models: new-incentives-charityevals"). I re-ran it with `--exclude new-incentives-founders-pledge,shrimp-welfare-project-charityevals`; no evaluator model of New Incentives is in the sandbox. That run checked this model: 0 violations, and no waiver covers it. The allowlist is `{}`. For later rounds: pass only evaluator model ids to `--exclude`, never the charity id.

Previous round: none (round 1).

## Drivers
- **Shared sliders**, the site's: `$uov_per_daly`, `$life_value_multiplier`, `$discount_rate`.
- **leverage_funging_adj** (judgment, 0.85, 0.6–1): 8.4–14.0. It is anchored on the audited 68%-from-one-grantor share and the annual report's "funding … to March 2028 is in place". That's reasonable, but no reference class is named beyond the template's 0.85–0.95. Name one: the other top-charity models' funging.
- **mortality_now_vs_bauchi_2021** (judgment, 0.95): anchored on NDHS 2023-24 zone U5MR and the trend. Fine.
- **external_validity_adj** (judgment, 0.9): needs the real-incentive and newer-cohort anchoring (finding 7).
- **pcv_efficacy** (sourced, Cochrane) and **pneumococcal_share_of_pneumonia** (source mismatch, finding 5).
- **attribution_share_shared** (formula from shared partner costs): the largest single model-specific lever. With finding 3 it rises from 40% to 48%.
- **rota_availability** (judgment, about ±10%): unanchored (finding 4).

The model would improve most from three things: partner cost on the delivered increase (finding 3), a measured rotavirus coverage ratio (finding 4), and anchoring external validity on the 2025 cohorts at today's real incentive (finding 7). With findings 1, 3 and 6 together the result moves from 11.89 to 14.45 (+21.5%).

Plausibility: the result is 1.87× the cause median, with no INV-15 issue. Without the attribution share, the cost is about $3,200 per death averted ($26.9M for 8,312 deaths, all of them credited before sharing). Counting partners' costs it is about $4,800. That is in line with other top-rated child-survival programmes. People reached (2.84M) is consistent with births in 205 of the 11 states' about 259 LGAs (about 3.6M births a year in those states × 0.79).
