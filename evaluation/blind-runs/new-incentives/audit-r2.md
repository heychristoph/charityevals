# Audit: new-incentives-charityevals, round 2
Result: 0.02709 uov/$ = 13.55 years of healthy life per $1,000 (joint 0.40–298; the model's own ranges 1.80–57.6); cause median 6.36 (×2.13). Verdict: **pass** (no high findings; 2 medium, 4 low).

Scope: unchanged since round 1 and still right. The model covers the one programme, cash incentives for routine vaccination with the awareness and vaccine-supply work that goes with it, in calendar 2025. That is $24,998,369 of programme services out of $26,886,948 total expenses (audited), with 1,845,641 infants enrolled in the same year. The ORS pilot is under 1% of spending and is stated as not modelled.

Mechanical: lint 0 high, 0 medium, 0 low. Invariants: no violations. normalization-audit (`--exclude new-incentives-founders-pledge`): 0 violations in every invariant, and no waiver covers this model. The allowlist is `{}`. After printing the report the script crashed, with ENOENT writing `generated/normalization-audit.json`, because the sandbox has no `generated/` folder. That is a sandbox artefact, not a finding.

Previous round:
1. internal_validity_adj on the shared trial figure: **resolved** (`@givewell_common.internal_validity_adjustment_new_incentives`, 0.88).
2. Deaths after five built by band: **resolved**. There is one row per band from the shared Bauchi risks, pathogen shares, efficacies and the shared waning, past-coverage and trend adjustments. Each band is discounted for its longer wait, and the shares are now formulas. The shared set's only waning figures for 15-49 and 50-74 are BCG's (−0.8, −0.9). Applying them to every vaccine is a defensible best guess: infant PCV, Hib, pertussis and tetanus protection is mostly gone by adulthood. With the 5-14 figure (−0.1) instead, the result moves by only about +1%. Not a finding.
3. Partner cost on the delivered increase: **resolved** (attribution 49.8%).

6. `lower_likelihood_of_infecting_others`: **resolved**.
7. External validity: **resolved**. Both points are in the explainer and the value is re-derived to 0.85 (0.65–1). Annual report p. 23 shows the newer cohorts (9, 10, 12–17, 19) only as a chart with no numbers in its text, so the builder couldn't anchor on them.
