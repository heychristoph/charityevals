## Effect
- IDinsight evaluation brief (NI-hosted): https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5f7c51bf9fac9b5ed62aa37b/5fae20fa406525d30087de19_New%20Incentives%20Evaluation%20Brief.pdf
  - p.3 Figure 1: BCG "+16pp [95% CI: 12, 21]", Penta 1 "+21pp [95% CI: 16, 26]", Measles 1 "+14pp [95% CI: 10, 18]", Fully immunized "+27pp [95% CI: 23, 32]".
  - p.3 design: 167 clinics (84 T, 83 C), Katsina, Zamfara, Jigawa; endline Nov 2019-Feb 2020; 5,173 children 12-16 months; caregiver-reported, cross-checked against cards.
  - p.4 non-incentivised vaccines at the same visits rose similarly (Hep B +16, IPV +18, YF +18pp).
  - p.2 incentive schedule: ₦500 BCG, ₦500 Penta1/PCV1, ₦500 Penta2/PCV2, ₦500 Penta3/PCV3, ₦2000 measles.
  - Same text in IDinsight full PDF at https://www.idinsight.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/NewIncentivesReport_13thNovember.pdf (6 pages, the brief).
  - The IDinsight "Final Report" is hosted only on files.givewell.org: forbidden, not opened. Penta 3 / PCV RCT effects therefore not available.
- NI coverage report 2024 (drive 1nJkNCgCkJ6s30yJVEyUpWcTCKa5pUT0g), pre-post, no control, cohorts 1-8, 11 (Bauchi, Katsina, Sokoto, Jigawa), Table 5 pooled 24-month differences: BCG 22.2 [18.4, 26.1]; Penta 1 25.4 [21.6, 29.3]; Penta 2 23.9 [20.0, 27.7]; Penta 3 27.2 [23.6, 30.7] (baseline 26.9 -> 54.1); Measles 1 17.8 [13.7, 21.9] (26.2 -> 44.0).
