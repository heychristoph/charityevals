## Drivers
1. **SADs per meal replaced** (shared estimate 0.084–1.72) × **Danish species-mix scale** (judgment 0.2–0.7). Together they give the largest swing. The scale now has sourced inputs (GfK shares, Tomasik lifespans, RP ranges), but it leaves out per-day intensity (#2) and egg and dairy in replacement dishes (#1), and both push it down.
2. **Shared moral-weight assumptions** (animal_moral_weight, daly_per_sad, uov_per_daly): the visitor's to set.
3. **(a) Share of meals switched** (judgment 2–20%), anchored on Garnett and Skanderborg, both more intensive than a one-day workshop. No DVF outcome data.
4. **(a) Meals per institution** (judgment 15k–100k). It could be sourced from participating institutions' student numbers.
5. **(a) Years of impact** (judgment 1–6). No follow-up data.

What would most improve the model: what the replacement dishes contain (DVF's or the projects' recipe guidance; #1), per-day intensity in the species scale (#2), and any before/after purchasing or plant-share data from Grøn køkkenrejse or Gkok kitchens. The Gkok project says it measures legume and plant shares and CO2 per kg of food from 2025.
