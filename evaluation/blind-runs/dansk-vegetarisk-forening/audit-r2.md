# Audit: dansk-vegetarisk-forening-charityevals, round 2

Result: 0.7681 ced/$ = 0.2805 years of healthy life per $1,000 (joint 7.4e-10 to 2.4e+4; model's own ranges, shared figures at their best: 6.2e-4 to 26.2); cause median 10.21 (×0.027). Verdict: **fix needed**. One high finding remains: the meals are still discounted only to the start of each project, not to its middle. It moves the result about -4%, but an undiscounted wait is high under section 6.

Scope: unchanged since round 1 and still right. The model covers DVF's domestic policy work over 2020-2024 as a past average, with 2024 the latest audited year. The two wins are the plant-based food fund (675m DKK, 2023-2030) and its top-up and permanence (420m DKK, 2025-2030). The other 2021 wins now enter through `unmodelled_benefits_adj`. The accounts are by nature, with no programme split. The other five programme areas publish no outcomes in meals or animals, so leaving them out is partial coverage under Convention 12, and the summary and `program_spend` say so (finding 6 fixes their share).
