Bottom line: 0.00899 SAD per donor $ (0.0105 SAD per $ of cost) = 0.002× GiveDirectly (joint 4.2e-10–14.9×; with shared assumptions at their defaults 6.9e-8–1.9×). Verdict: **pass** (no high or medium findings; 5 low).

Previous round:
- #1 (high, eggs and dairy in the replacement dish): **resolved**. New row `replacement_offset` {0, 0.25, 0.6} feeds `sads_per_meal = @sad_factors.sads_per_meal_replaced * species_mix_scale * (1 - replacement_offset)`. - #7 (low, Green January and network meals): resolved. The explainer says these meals aren't counted (conservative).

- #9 (low, outlier and spread): carried as #5 below.

Mechanical pass: `scripts/audit-model.ts` shows no high findings and two medium ones: the outlier (>10× below 7 peers, 6.83–614 SAD/$) and the spread (2.7×10⁷ at shared defaults). Both are explained in the summary, in the write-up and in the SVB decomposition, so they are carried as low (#5). `scripts/check.ts dansk-vegetarisk-forening-charityevals` reports "✓ all models valid".

I recomputed the new values. `species_mix_scale`: 0.4×(0.13×½) + 0.4×(0.2×⅔) + 0.2 = 0.027 + 0.053 + 0.2 = 0.28 ✓. `sads_per_meal`: 0.902 × 0.28 × 0.75 = 0.189 ✓ (write-up: 0.19). Outcome: 9,364 × 0.8 × 0.75 ≈ 5,620 SADs ✓ (write-up: about 5,600). SAD/$: 5,620 / $535,976 = 0.0105, and × donor share 0.858 = 0.0090 ✓. The result is 0.0150 × 0.8 × 0.75 = 0.0090, so it moved exactly by the two changes. The write-up's tornado figures (0.0002–0.004 for SADs/meal; 0.001–0.005 for the scale; 0.003–0.001 for the offset; and the others) match the script.

## Drivers
1. **SADs per meal replaced** (shared estimate 0.084–1.72): sourced, with ACE's own 10× disagreement as the range. Together with the **species-mix scale** (judgment 0.15–0.6, now with per-day intensity that Tomasik's column 5 corroborates) and the **replacement offset** (judgment 0–0.6, roughly supported by the care-home recipes), this sets what one switched meal is worth.
2. **Shared moral-weight assumptions** (animal_moral_weight, daly_per_sad, uov_per_daly): the visitor's to set.
3. **(a) Share of meals switched** (judgment 2–20%): anchored on Garnett 2019 and Skanderborg, both more intensive than DVF's workshops. No DVF outcome data.
4. **(a) Meals per kitchen** (judgment 15k–100k): anchored on efterskole pupil numbers.
5. **(a) Years of impact** (judgment 1–6): no follow-up data.

What would most improve the model: measured before/after plant shares or purchases from Grøn køkkenrejse or Gkok kitchens (Gkok says it measures legume and plant shares from 2025). With that data, the three largest DVF-specific judgments (share switched, implementation, persistence) would become observations.
