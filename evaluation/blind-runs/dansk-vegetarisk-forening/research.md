## Effect evidence
- Skanderborg Kommune, https://www.skanderborg.dk/nyheder/flere-boenner-faerre-boeffer-kommunale-koekkener-er-meget-groennere-end-for-fire-aar-siden
  (updated 23 Jan 2026): 4-year municipal kitchen conversion (FØL funded): "sænket deres indkøb af oksekød med 61%"; other meat −15%; legumes more than doubled; 15,000 meals/day. Upper reference class (intensive, multi-year, municipal mandate).
- Green, Smith & Mathur (2025) meta-analysis, PMC11957195: "The pooled effect of all 112 interventions on MAP consumption is quite small (standardized mean difference [SMD] = 0.07"; choice-architecture SMD 0.21 [−0.99, 1.42] (2 studies); marginal studies raise to 0.2.
- Garnett et al. 2019 PNAS (PMC6800350, via search summary; PNAS page 403): doubling vegetarian options 25%→50% raised vegetarian sales 40.8–78.8%; little change in overall sales; no rebound at dinner.
- Efterskoler: 32,368 students at 242 schools Aug 2023, avg 134 (Efterskoleforeningen, via search snippet; notat elevtal 2026 efterskolerne.dk).
