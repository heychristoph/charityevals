# Audit: dansk-vegetarisk-forening-charityevals, round 1

Result: 0.6634 ced/$ = 0.2423 years of healthy life per $1,000 (joint 1.4e-9 to 1.4e+4; model's own ranges, shared figures at their best: 0.0011 to 16.7); cause median 10.21 (x0.024). Verdict: **fix needed** (one high finding, on discounting).

Scope: DVF's policy work 2020-2024, as a past average: average real yearly spending on policy (30% of programme spending, a judgment) against two wins, the Fund for Plant-Based Foods (675m DKK, 2023-2030, won October 2021) and its top-up and permanence (420m DKK, 2025-2030, November 2024). 2024 is the latest audited year: the accounts listing has nothing newer than regnskab-2024, uploaded November 2025. DVF's accounts are by nature, with no programme split. The other five programme areas (business, education, inspiration, cooperation, international; about 70% of programme spending) publish no outcomes in meals or animals, so leaving them out under Convention 12 is allowed, and the summary and the `program_spend` explainer say so. One gap inside the modelled programme: the policy cost pays for wins the model doesn't credit (finding 4).

Mechanical: lint 0 high / 1 medium (far-below) / 1 low (spread). Invariants on the model: none. check.ts: all models valid. normalization-audit: with `--exclude dansk-vegetarisk-forening` the script dropped **this model too**, because it matches on charity id ("Excluded 1 models: dansk-vegetarisk-forening-charityevals"). No other DVF model is in the sandbox, so I also ran it with no exclude. That run covered this model and found no violations (0 in every invariant). It then crashed writing generated/normalization-audit.json, which doesn't exist in the sandbox; that is not a finding about the model. For later rounds, run it without `--exclude` or with an id that doesn't match this model's charity.

Previous round: none (round 1).

## Drivers

1. Shared figures that dominate the spread: `$chicken_vs_person`, the fish welfare range, `@sad_factors.sads_per_meal_replaced`, `$win_years_counted`, `$daly_per_sad` and `$marginal_vs_past_average`. These are the site's figures, not findings.
2. `meals_replaced_per_dkk` (judgment, 0.005-0.25; tornado 0.024-1.21) is the model's largest own uncertainty. Its anchor is a verified target, not a measured outcome. The best improvement is Plantefonden's effect reporting from its 2025 effect model ("Effektmodel"). Once the 19 completed projects or the 2025 projects report meals served, rebuild the row from those reports. Fix its discounting first (finding 1).
3. `attribution_share_shared` (judgment, 0.1-0.5) needs a worked resource count (finding 3).
4. `meal_mix_vs_reference` (judgment, 1, 0.5-2) follows the site's rule for Denmark.
5. `policy_share` (judgment): the anchor needs correcting (finding 2, +20%).

Net effect of the fixes with a number (1, 2, 4, 6): about +15% to +25%, to roughly 0.28-0.30 years of healthy life per $1,000. Finding 3 could move the result either way.
