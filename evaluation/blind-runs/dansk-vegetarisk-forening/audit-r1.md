## Drivers
1. **SADs per meal replaced** (shared estimate, 0.084–1.72): the largest swing (0.001–0.02×). It is sourced, but not for Danish canteen food. Adapting it to the Danish species mix (#1) is the single most important improvement and probably moves the result down several-fold.
2. **Shared moral-weight assumptions** (animal_moral_weight, daly_per_sad, uov_per_daly): the visitor's to set, not the builder's.
3. **Years of impact** (judgment, 1–6): no follow-up data. Should be split by branch (#3). The kostskole project's own CO2 and plant-share measurements (2025 onward) could later give persistence data.
4. **(a) share of meals switched** (judgment, 2–20%): anchored on Garnett and Skanderborg, both more intensive. Asking DVF for any before/after purchasing data from Grøn køkkenrejse participants would improve the model most.
5. **(a) meals per institution** (judgment, 15k–100k): could be sourced from the participating institutions' student numbers (gymnasier and erhvervsskoler) via Danish ministry statistics (uddannelsesstatistik.dk).

What would most improve the model: a Danish species-mix scaling of SADs/meal (#1), branch-specific persistence (#3), and any measured meal or purchasing data from DVF's kitchen projects.
