## Drivers

1. **SADs per shrimp stunned** (`@sad_factors`, an estimate built from judgments): tornado 0.35–70×. Inside it, in order: (a) the **AIM method version**: v1 with ×3.18 and moral weight 0.186, against v2 with no ×3.18 and moral weight 0.083, worth 6.8× (#1); (b) the **excruciating-to-disabling weight**: AIM 11.85 against RP's mean 502, worth about 9× (#2); (c) duration, now settled, since RP and AIM v2 agree at about 4–5 minutes. Both (a) and (b) are judgments. Each is now reasoned, but the write-up should show the result under each.
2. **Shared exchange rates** (`animal_moral_weight`, `daly_per_sad`, `uov_per_daly`): the visitor sets these.
3. **Years brought forward** (judgment, 1.7–7.1×): reasoned from "indefinite usage" and retailer pressure. The accounting-life anchor is misworded (#5).
4. **Evidence adjustment** (judgment, 2.2–5.0×): well argued from Somerville et al. 2026.
5. **Shrimp per tonne and tonnes per stunner** (sourced estimates, 2.9–5.9×): verified, and the May 2025 figure suggests they're slightly conservative.

What would most improve the model: decide and state which AIM SAD version the site uses for every SAD fact (#1). Moving hens and shrimp to v2 together would be best, and until then v1 should be labelled as v1. Then show the three readings side by side in the write-up: AIM 2026 about 0.6×, AIM 2024 with RP durations 3.9× (current best), RP weights about 35×. The best guess of 3.9× is defensible only as the v1-consistent figure.
