## Drivers

1. **SADs per shrimp stunned** (`@sad_factors`, an estimate built from judgments): tornado 0.35–21.6×. Within it, the **excruciating:disabling weight** (AIM 11.85 against RP's 5–1000) is the dominant judgment, worth 10–30× on its own (#1). Next comes the ×3.18 convention (#2, 3.18×), then the duration (RP's mean against AIM's 20 min).
2. **Shared exchange rates** (`animal_moral_weight`, `daly_per_sad`, `uov_per_daly`): the visitor sets these.
3. **Years brought forward** (judgment, 0.53–2.3×): now argued from equipment life. Cite the "indefinite usage" term (#8).
4. **Evidence adjustment** (judgment, 0.72–1.6×): well argued from Somerville et al. 2026.
5. **Shrimp per tonne and tonnes per stunner** (sourced estimates, 0.93–1.9×): verified against SWP's figures.

What would most improve the model: make the pain-intensity weight an explicit, ranged choice between AIM and RP and fix the high end (#1). Then settle the ×3.18 consistently with the hen and pig facts (#2), ideally with an answer from AIM. Taken together, a corrected best is probably about 4–15× GiveDirectly. It is 1.25× only if AIM's lowest intensity weight is used without its ×3.18, and about 35–110× if RP's mean weight is combined with AIM's ×3.18.
