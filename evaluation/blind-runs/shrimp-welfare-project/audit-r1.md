# Audit: shrimp-welfare-project-charityevals, round 1

Bottom line: 224.2 SADs/$ = 57.4× GiveDirectly (joint 0.0001–8,614×; 0.017–1,362× with shared assumptions at their defaults). Verdict: **fix needed**.
Previous round: none (first round).

The cost and reach chain is well built and checks out against the accounts and SWP's own figures. The problems are in the effect: the new shared fact `@sad_factors.sads_per_shrimp_stunned` takes Ambitious Impact's 0.097 SADs/shrimp at face value. Its own source spreadsheet says that figure contains an ad-hoc ×3.18 multiplier that "leans towards overestimating". Its 20-minute pain duration is a minimum immersion time, not a time to insensibility. The "Rethink Priorities" low end is a median where the rest of the comparison uses central or point values. Together these drive the headline "90× disagreement" and the "0.65× GiveDirectly with RP's figure" claim in the summary.

## Drivers

1. **SADs per shrimp stunned** (`@sad_factors`, estimate from a judgment-laden external model): tornado 0.65–118×. This is by far the biggest driver, and it has the source problems in findings 1–3. Fixing it is the single most useful change. The best is likely 0.03 or lower (about 18× or less), and the honest RP comparison is a mean, not a median.
2. **Shared exchange rates** (`animal_moral_weight`, `daly_per_sad`, `uov_per_daly`): the visitor sets these, and they move every animal model equally.
3. **Years brought forward** (judgment, 21–103×): no reference class for donated equipment, and inconsistent with the 4-year accounting life (finding 6).
4. **Evidence and delivery adjustments** (judgments, about 33–79× each): the delivery adjustment rests on evidence about agreements, not purchases (finding 5). The evidence adjustment is reasonably argued from Somerville et al.
5. **Shrimp per tonne and tonnes per stunner** (sourced estimates, 43–87×): well sourced from SWP's own figures and cross-checked.

What would most improve the model: rebuild the SAD fact transparently, with AIM's formula shown, the ×3.18 removed or justified, the duration sourced, and an RP mean. Then check whether the hen SAD facts share the 3.18. After that, resolve the retailer-pressure triple count and anchor `years_of_impact` to equipment life. Taken individually: removing ×3.18 gives about 18×, attribution 1.0 gives +25%, delivery 0.8 gives +23%, and years 4 gives −31%. Combined, a plausible corrected best guess is about 15–20× GiveDirectly. It could fall to about 1–5× if RP's slaughter durations are used for the central case.
