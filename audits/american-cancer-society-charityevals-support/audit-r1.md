## Drivers

1. `$missing_data_discount`: the shared slider; every branch is thin. This is correct for judged effects. The money saved at Hope Lodge is borderline, since ACS's own price is used, but it is consistent with Habitat and the Red Cross's meals and nights.
2. `lodge_deaths_averted_per_household`: a judgment with no named anchor (finding 4). This is the most useful thing to improve.
3. `recipients_consumption_share`, `lodge_nights_per_stay`, `cash_equivalent_share` and `lodge_counterfactual_adj`: judgments on the lodge money branch, about half the result. The per-night value is sourced and verified.
4. `@american_cancer_society.prevention_share_of_support`: a shared judgment that sets the cost denominator (0.0030–0.0050 over its range). Its anchors are verified.
5. The information doses, `info_sd_gain_helpline` and `info_sd_gain_other` (finding 2).

The result is about a hundredth of the cause median. The summary gives the reason: small money gains to US households and a small dose of support per contact. This is consistent with the domestic-services peers (Red Cross 0.0045, Habitat 0.0054, Salvation Army 0.0052).

Verdict: pass. Findings: 0 high, 4 medium, 5 low.
