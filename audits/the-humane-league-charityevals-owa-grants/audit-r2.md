1. Shared sliders: chicken vs person, DALYs per SAD, units of value per DALY. These now act in both directions, since the compliance costs stay fixed in healthy years while the animal gains scale with the sliders.
2. `@sad_factors.sads_per_broiler_space_only` (shared judgment). The UK space commitments are about twice the net result.
3. `@compliance_costs.ca_prop12_net_cost_per_year` (estimate, $150M–$1,195M) with `apadef_cost_egg_share` (judgment; M3). At the high end the result turns negative.
4. Credit shares: `ukbcc_coalition_share`, `ukban_coalition_share` and `apadef_coalition_share`, all judgments. The last two now lower the result as they rise, because their branches are net harms.
5. `apadef_opposition_risk_cut` (judgment 0.17, anchored on Bergan and Cole 2015).

- the pork part of the preemption branch (H1);
- THL UK's actual non-THL income (M1);
- a sourced split of Proposition 12's net cost between eggs and pork (M3), which now decides the US branch's sign.
