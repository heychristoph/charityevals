## Drivers
1. `$win_years_counted` (shared slider): the site's, not the builder's. Under finding 1's fix the democracy branch scales with it nearly linearly instead of quadratically.
2. `$missing_data_discount` (shared slider), both branches thin: correctly applied.
3. `@next_dollar.brennan_center_for_justice` (0.32, sourced estimate, verified).
4. `dem_field_cut` (judgment, 5%, 1–20%): the largest of the builder's own uncertainties, with no reference class (finding 8).
5. `dem_p_breakdown` (15%, filed as an estimate but a judgment above the 11% source; finding 3), then `jus_people` (period, finding 6) and `dem_severity_share` (judgment, premise wrong; finding 4).

What would most improve the model: fix the build-up method (finding 1, ×3–4), add the tariff ruling (finding 2), and give the field's cut in breakdown risk a reference class.
