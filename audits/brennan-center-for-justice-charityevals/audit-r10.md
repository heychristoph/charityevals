## Checks of the scope
- **No dollar in both slide and reform branches.** dem_direct_share = (lns × lns_direct_share + dem × dem_direct_in_democracy) / (dem + lns); dem_slide_cost = dem_cost × dem_direct_share and ref_cost = dem_cost × (1 - dem_direct_share): a partition of dem_cost (17.29M + 33.37M = 50.65M). dem_attribution_share = dem_slide_cost / dem_field_spending (0.66%, was 1.9%); ref_attribution_share reads ref_cost only. Verified by running the model. The family rule (rights-advocacy.md, Democracy and governance: only direct anti-backsliding spending counts against a slide; reform dollars in their reform branch alone) is met.
- **Tariff branch.** Valued by briefs / all amicus briefs × amici's share, not by dollars of cost; no tar_* row reads dem_cost or ref_cost; its cost (LNS emergency powers) overlaps only slightly with dem_slide_cost (finding 4). tar_dalys 10,314 of 12,612 (82%, "four fifths" as the summary and write-up say).
- **Wiring.** uov_bridged = dem_uov_adj + tar_uov_adj + ref_uov_adj + jus_uov_adj (25,205); raw_total = dem_raw + tar_raw + ref_raw + jus_raw; ref_dalys and ref_raw follow dem/tar's pattern (weights for the averages = branch healthy years before adjustments), and ref_uov_adj is in the outcome section. The branch DALYs sum to outcome_total (12,612).
- **Formulas and units.** ref_people_per_year = (55.39M + 23.70M) / 18 = 4.39M per year; ref_ln_gain = ln(1 + 0.2124 × 0.01) = 0.00212; ref_rate = discount rate + repeal rate; ref_discounted_years the shifted ramp (1.75), as in the family recipe; ref_value 23,522 UoV per movement-year; × 0.25 relative effect = 5,880; ref_movement_real = $22.0M (2024) × CPI 2025/2024 = $22.58M. ref_implementation_adj = (strong + weak × 0.3) / (strong + weak) = 0.79. Units agree (people, years, UoV, USD in 2025 prices). ref_p_success is 1 and unused (template row), harmless.
- **ref_attribution_share above 1 (1.48).** The movement's value is a flow per year of a movement spending $22.6M; the Center's reform spending is $33.4M, so the dollars are 1.48 times the movement's, and the ×0.25 relative effect brings the branch to 0.37 movement-years. Defensible: it is the same linear-in-dollars rule as in Common Cause Education Fund's model (same movement figure, same relative effect), the row's label says "as a multiple of the redistricting movement's yearly spending", and its explainer says it is not a share of anything and that diminishing returns are not modelled. The only softness is that the canonical slot is named attribution_share while the value exceeds 1, which the label and explainer cover; and that "diminishing returns are not modelled" may flatter a programme larger than the movement it is scaled to, partly offset by the ×0.25 and the 0.5 missing-data step. The branch is 9% of the result, and relative effect at its ends moves the result 0.065 to 0.089. Accepted, honestly labelled.
