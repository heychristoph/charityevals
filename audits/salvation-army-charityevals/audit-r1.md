## Peers: the far-below lint (0.047× the global-health median)

The low result is right, and it doesn't come from scope, reach or a shared figure. The model sits in the middle of its own template family's models of US services (0.0012-0.029 years per $1,000). The global-health median is set mostly by programmes in poor countries. Three rows make the gap, all on the site's rules:

1. **The log scale at US consumption.** The clients' baseline is $10,075 a person (survey mean × the shared food-aid share). The $27 of help per person is a 0.27% gain. The same dollars to someone on $500-1,000 a year would be a 10-30× larger log gain.
2. **Help valued per dollar spent.** About $760M of food, goods, bills and services is valued (in-kind at its cash equivalent) against $3.01B of cost. Much of the money pays for case management, worship, staff and buildings. The audited functional expenses of the one public territory show 40% staff pay outside rehabilitation. The meal count holds up against recorded food (implementation_adj's cross-check), so reach is not undercounted.
3. **Standard adjustments.** Half of the help is judged additional (aid_counterfactual_adj), every branch takes the shared missing-data discount (0.5), and 73% of the credit is the charity's (donated goods and volunteer hours).

No programme is missing, the year matches across cost and reach, and the shared figures (survey consumption, shelter-night value, in-kind ratios) are read correctly. The fixes above move the headline by −5% to +4%; none changes this picture.

## Drivers
1. `$missing_data_discount` (shared slider): every branch is thin, so the result moves one for one with it. Correct: no Salvation Army service has a controlled evaluation.
2. `$uov_per_daly` (shared slider): almost everything is income and deaths in units of value.
3. `aid_counterfactual_adj` (judgment, 0.5, 0.3-0.8; −23% / +35%): anchored on Hunger in America's turn-away rates, a reasoned reference class.
4. `@united_states.food_aid_recipients_consumption_share` (shared, 0.3; −23% / +29%).
5. `implementation_adj` (judgment, 0.9, 0.7-1): anchored on the meals-versus-recorded-food cross-check.

What would most improve the model: a published split of the rehabilitation line between stores and centres, and the share of shelter nights in recovery centres. A national (not Western-only) figure for cash assistance and in-kind goods by territory. Any follow-up of Salvation Army clients' food security or housing against a comparison group, which would also make a branch measured.
