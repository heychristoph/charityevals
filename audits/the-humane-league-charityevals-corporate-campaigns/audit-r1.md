# Audit: the-humane-league-charityevals-corporate-campaigns, round 1

Result: 1.915 ced/$ = 2.107e-4 years of healthy life per $1,000 (combined range 6.6e-6 to 6.7e-3); cause median 7.62e-4 (×0.28). Verdict: **fix needed** (3 high).

Scope: THL's 2025 "cage-free accountability (US and global)" line, $5,108,314 = ACE's two 2024 programme costs ($1,604,910 + $3,372,446) × CPI 2025/2024. The three THL lines add up to the Form 990's programme expenses: 5,108,314 + 4,261,891 + 5,249,176 = 14,619,381. charities/the-humane-league.json `lines[corporate-campaigns].dollars` (5,108,314) matches `program_spend` (5,108,314.47). The label is right. The source text has the US and global amounts the wrong way round (H1).

Branches at the defaults: THL's own switches (acc_) give 85.6% of the SADs; the alliance's new policies abroad (owanew_) 11.9%; restored pledges abroad (owaacc_) 2.1%; broiler policies abroad (owabcc_) 0.3%.

Mechanical: lint 0/0/0, invariants none. normalization-audit: no violation (every invariant 0). Running it rewrote generated/normalization-audit.json, which I restored with git checkout, so no repo file is changed. simulation-check: stable (5% 0.23×, 50% 1.0×, 95% 3.05×); the two other THL models are stable too.

Previous round: none (round 1).

## Double counting across the three THL models (checked)

- **Switches at companies THL pressed itself.** This model and other-programmes' vol_ branch use the same pool: 7.5M less the UK's 1.85M less the members' 1.64M = 4.01M hens. They share it with the same denominators (this line + mobilisation + volunteers: $11.19M here, $11.21M there). Shares: 0.306 here + 0.293 there = 0.600, within thl_acc_share 0.86. Not over-credited.
- **The alliance's wins abroad** (owanew/owaacc/owabcc). The pool and its judgments are identical to the grants model. Shares: 0.062 here (owa_coord_in_line / owa_resources) + 0.098 grants + 0.017 other-programmes' trn_ (on another denominator, M2) = 0.18, well under 1. Not over-credited, but whether this line holds the alliance's running at all is wrong (H1).
- **UK switches and the members' wins** are taken out of THL's 7.5M before crediting. There is no double count, but the UK figure taken out is too large (H2).
- **THL's 4 new commitments** are credited in other-programmes' new_ branch, and some are probably also inside the 216 alliance policies here and in grants (L4, negligible).
