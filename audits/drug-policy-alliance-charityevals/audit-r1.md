## Drivers
1. can_user_ln_gain (judgment, unsourced anchor; #6) and can_record_ln_gain (judgment; #7): the two biggest positive terms.
2. can_cud_or_26plus (sourced, Cerdá's 95% CI), can_health_evidence_adj and can_dependence_share (judgments; #7): the CUD loss.
3. The attribution shares and their period basis (#1): these scale every branch together.
4. m110_od_evidence (judgment, sourced to Zoorob): the Measure 110 loss.

What would most improve the model: settle the period basis of the credit (#1), add the traffic harm (#2), and correct the records count and unit (#3, #4). Each of these moves the result by more than its whole size. Then source the two ln-gain judgments, which now carry the positive side alone.

Verdict: fix needed (5 high, 2 medium, 6 low).
