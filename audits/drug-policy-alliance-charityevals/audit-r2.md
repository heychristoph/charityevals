## Drivers
1. `can_user_ln_gain` (judgment, anchored on RAND spending): the largest gain and the one input that flips the sign alone. Fixing its arithmetic (#4) is the cheapest improvement. A published consumer-surplus estimate for a legal cannabis market would help most.
2. `can_traffic_deaths_per_million` (estimate) and `can_traffic_evidence_adj` (judgment): together the second-largest harm. Re-derive them in the sources' units and split "caused" from "lasts" (#2).
3. `can_health_evidence_adj` (judgment, three designs named) and `can_cud_or_26plus` (Cerdá's 95% CI): the largest harm.
4. `m110_od_evidence` (judgment, anchored on Zoorob et al. 2024): the third harm. It belongs in the main chain (#3).
5. `can_attribution_share` and the period shares (judgments): they scale the gains and harms of each branch together, so they move the size of the result, not its sign.

What would most improve the model: apply each evidence doubt once (#1, which deepens the net harm by about a third) and read the traffic studies in their own units (#2). After those two fixes the sign and size rest on four judgments, users' gain and the three harms' causal shares, each with a named reference class.

Verdict: fix needed (2 high, 2 medium, 5 low).
