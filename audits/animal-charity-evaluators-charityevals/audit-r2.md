## Drivers
1. `$chicken_vs_person` and `$daly_per_sad`: shared judgments. The result is exactly proportional to them (×1.5 → ×1.5000).
2. `@next_dollar.animal_charity_evaluators`, 36% (15–70%): an estimate built from room × use × run. Its use factor predates ACE's October 2026 restructuring (finding 2). Range 0.00087–0.0041.
3. The Humane League's ACE figure, which is 76% of `value_destinations` at a 16.4% fund weight. The fund split is sourced, but the explainer's assumption that direct gifts split the same way is a judgment.
4. `@meta.survey_nonrespondent_influence` (shared) and `survey_response_share` (judgment, 20–70%): 0.0017–0.0028 and 0.0018–0.0025.
5. `moved_side_effects` (judgment, −10% to +30%, anchored on the GiveWell and Giving What We Can models): 0.0017–0.0025.

What would most improve the model: the engine change in finding 1, so the shifted-money rule is shared and checked; a next-dollar figure that reflects ACE's restructuring (finding 2); and, once its accounts are audited, moving to FY2025-26.
