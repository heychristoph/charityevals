The largest of the model's own inputs is the nuclear share (judgment; 1.07–2.42), and nuclear's figures are 34× the AI charities'. Two things would improve the model most. First, a sourced 2025 split of the money Longview directed by area: its 2025 annual report, or its fund reports to donors. Second, the destination of the $5.69M Europe grant. Both would replace judgments that together move the result by up to about 40%.
