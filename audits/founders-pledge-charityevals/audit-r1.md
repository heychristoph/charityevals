## Drivers
1. `@meta.prior_vs_cash` (shared judgment, 0–3×): where shifted money would have gone. This is the largest swing (5.7 to −0.5).
2. `$missing_data_discount` (shared): only the partner branch reads it (0.96–7.9).
3. `@next_dollar.founders_pledge`, 33% (14–71%): sourced estimate from the accounts' reserves and growth plan. It is in line with Effektiv Spenden 32% and Longview 36%.
4. `@meta.own_effect_share`, 30% (15–45%): shared, since there is no Founders Pledge survey. A member or partner survey is what would most improve the model.
5. The valuation of destinations: health at `#cause:global-health` against its named grantees (finding 1, +76%), and the members' non-recommended giving (finding 2, +46–71%). These are structural choices; neither shows in the tornado.
The model's own judgments (`partner_fund_overlap`, `moved_side_effects`) move it only ±15%. The model's own range (2.7–4.9) is narrow because the counts are audited and the uncertainty sits in shared figures.

Peers: Giving What We Can 55.6, Effektiv Spenden 23.7, Longview 1.22. Founders Pledge moves more per dollar (16× gross to recommended charities, 4.9× counting only what it caused or redirected). It sits low because about a third of its money is climate (`#cause:climate` is 300× below global health) and its health and education money is valued at the global-health cause mean (0.0125), not at its grantees. Finding 1 is the part of that gap that comes from the counting rule. The climate third is supported by the evidence and is stated in the summary.

Verdict: fix needed (2 high: findings 1 and 2).
