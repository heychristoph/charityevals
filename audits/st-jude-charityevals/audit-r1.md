## Drivers
- **Top inputs.**
  1. `$missing_data_discount`: shared slider; every branch is thin, so it scales the result in proportion.
  2. `gl_mortality_reduction`: judgment, anchored on Recife (Howard, JAMA 2004) and Ribeiro's account. No cross-site series exists.
  3. `gl_local_cost_per_child_2018`: judgment, anchored on Fung et al.'s costing studies (sourced).
  4. `gl_evidence_adj`: judgment, no reference class (#1).
  5. `daly_per_child_death`: shared.
  6. `gl_baseline_mortality`: judgment with an unsourced middle (#2).
- **Structure of the St. Jude Global branch.** The result is nearly independent of reach. As `gl_unit_children` grows, `gl_attribution_share` falls in step, so credited deaths approach St. Jude Global's spending ÷ the per-child spending of the hospitals and other partners × the gain per child. This is why local cost per child is a top driver and reach (8,000–60,000) moves the result only −20% / +11%.
- **Spending against reach.** At the best guesses St. Jude Global spends about $3,600 per child reached ($105M for 29,000 children). Ribeiro (2014) puts a strong demonstration twinning at about US$100,000 a year plus St. Jude staff time for a unit of about 200 new cases, about $500 a child plus time. Either reach is larger than 25,000, or much of the budget (the Platform's medicines, the Alliance's central programmes, the master's programme) doesn't reach children as twinning support. Neither moves the result much, for the reason above.
- **Ranges.** The model's own ranges span 3,308× jointly, and the combined range is about 58×. That is wide but honest for an outcome St. Jude doesn't measure. No range looks arbitrary. `new_patients_growth` is a little narrow (#5).
- **What would most improve the model:**
  - a published outcome series across St. Jude Global Alliance sites, from St. Jude's own registry or the Global Platform's monitoring, with a count of children at actively supported units;
  - a reference class for the before-and-after evidence discount;
  - a sourced survival baseline for the partners' regions.
