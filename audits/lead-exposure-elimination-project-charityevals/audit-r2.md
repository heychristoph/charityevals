## Drivers
1. `$discount_rate` (shared slider; 2.9–110). The benefits start about 17 years after the exposure, and the exposure is spread over the paint's 25-year life.
2. `$win_years_counted` (shared; 3.1–44).
3. `@lead.paint_share_of_child_bll_lmic`: a judgment, 15% (4–40%; 3.8–38). It is still the biggest own uncertainty with no measurement. A source-apportionment (isotope) study in a LEEP country would improve the model most.
4. `$missing_data_discount` (shared; 2.9–29).
5. `@lead.iq_points_per_ug_dl`: an estimate, 0.5 from Lanphear's pooled analysis (4.3–23). It is sourced and verified.

Next come `$uov_per_daly`, the next-dollar figure (sourced, 6.8–26), `cohorts_per_win_year`, the credit share (a judgment with its resources listed) and the household multiplier. After finding 1's fix, the value-of-life slider joins them.

Peers: 14.3 is 209× the global-health median of 112 models (0.0686). As in round 1, the gap is reach. About 18.6M births a year across 14 countries (Nigeria at half; 7.3M of them in the regulation branch) are protected for 5 counted years, against about $786,000 of real spending a year, and both the regulations and the switches are measured by LEEP's own testing. The gap comes from the evidence and the site's counting rules, not from a different programme, year or counting rule, so it is not a finding. (Audit-only note, never for site text.)
