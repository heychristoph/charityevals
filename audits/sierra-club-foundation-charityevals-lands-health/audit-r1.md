Answers to the two questions:
- **Lands wins in the other-work share.** In magnitude, roughly right: the rules were mostly rescinded in 2025, and a fossil-supply branch for the Thompson Divide would add only a few percent. The reasoning is not right, though. The no-carbon claim, the unsourced full leakage and a still-standing withdrawal count as nothing, and the share is borrowed from EDF. Findings 1 and 3 give the fix.
- **The outdoors-trips branch.** The reach is verified (5,127 people on 254 trips, Sierra Club 990 2024, Sch. O). The 0.005 WELLBY judgment (0.001 to 0.03) is reasonable for one or a few day trips. The evidence adjustment of 0.8 and the missing-data step are applied correctly, and the period arithmetic (× period_years, then ÷ period_years) is consistent. Only the credit rule (finding 4) and the chapter outings (finding 5) are open, and both are negligible in effect.
