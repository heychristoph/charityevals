## Checks with no finding
- **Credit share of donated goods**: cash only in the cost; the $10.44M goods are in the credit share at their local value (`em_gik_local_ratio` 0.4, 0.15–0.8), and the same 0.4 sets the goods' share of value delivered, so no inconsistency. Effect is small: without goods the credit would be 0.667 against 0.604 (+10%) but the value delivered falls by 9%, a net change near zero; no double penalty.
- **Overhead**: ($133,068k + $15,629k + $21,991k) / $133,068k = 1.283, verified to the statement of activities p. 7.
- **Chain** follows the humanitarian-relief template; no template row replaced by a typed number; added rows (gik, goods share, place weights) feed canonical rows; value per death uses the shared moral weights by age with judged age shares, as in the American Red Cross peer; wait discounted at `$discount_rate`; `$missing_data_discount` applied; salary_sacrifice and donor_cost_share present.
- **Double counting**: deaths/illness and goods are valued on the same people, but the budget is split by `em_goods_share` (goods vs health care) and the health effects are not scaled by resources, so no resource is counted twice. Counterfactual (others would respond) and partners' resources (credit) are different questions. `estimate_basis` unit_cost equals the Red Cross and MSF relief peers.
- **Plausibility**: 0.0367 is 0.50 times the Red Cross international model (0.073), 0.052 times MSF relief (0.70), 0.16 times Salvation Army international (0.23). Against MSF the gap is the health care (MSF: 5.5% crisis mortality, 0.75% averted share of a much bigger excess). Against the Red Cross: $19 delivered per person here against $70 there, the Red Cross's 150,000 against Americares' 684,700 people reached on similar spending. Cost per survivor helped is $75 ($51.4M / 684,700); goods on the log scale are about 89% of the value and deaths about 10%. ×0.11 of the cause median is not under a tenth, and the `summary` gives the reason.
- **Missing data**: every effect is a judgment, so the shared missing-data slider is right.
- **Wait**: `gift_to_grant_years` 0.32 uses all cash and investments ($54,958k) over total cash expenses; effect about 1%.
