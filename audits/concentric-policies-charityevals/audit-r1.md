## Drivers
- Shared settings lead the tornado: `$mobiliser_share`, `$win_years_counted`, `$missing_data_discount`.
- `eo_p_success`: a judgment with a stated basis (no record of the order), but it double counts with credit (#2).
- `@next_dollar.concentric_policies`: an estimate on stale evidence (#4).
- `eo_alc_price_change`: a judgment with no anchor of its own (#6).
- `@alcohol.harm_price_response`: sourced (meta-analysis, verified).
- The stopped order's years counted: the largest error (#1). The legislated April 2025 beverage-excise cut means the alcohol half of the main branch counts about one year, not five.

What would most improve the model: the text or a dated report of the 2024 order, and how its rates compare with the 2025 amendment's. Liberia's pre- and post-2025 beer and spirits excise rates. Recorded versus unrecorded alcohol from WHO. A current funding statement from the charity.

Peers: the result sits at ×0.58 of the global-health median and about level with the other tobacco-tax advocacy build (0.063). After #1 to #4 it would fall to roughly 0.03–0.04, still within a tenth of the median, so no gap row needs explaining. This belongs in the audit only, never in site text.
