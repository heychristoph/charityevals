Most improvement: a sourced SAM/MAM split and untreated-fatality anchor for nutrition, and a country-weighted crisis death rate for other health care. Peers: 0.45 sits between Save the Children (0.21) and MSF relief (0.70) and under the 10× threshold; the gap to Save the Children is explained in the summary (more nutrition and cash, whose reach is counted).
