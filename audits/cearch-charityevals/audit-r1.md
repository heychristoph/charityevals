5. `daly_per_child_death` (shared): 2.66–5.13. Next come `$discount_rate`, `commission_share_changed` and `moved_own_effect`. The last two should read the shared own-effect figure (findings 1–2).

The model's own ranges span 0.87–96 at the joint ends. That is wide but real: no accounts, and no client has published how a report changed its grants. The point inputs on the path (the two grant amounts, the period) are observed counts.

What would most improve the model: CEARCH's budget, which it offers to share with donors (its archived financials page). Next would be the amounts of the four GHPF grants and the post-May 2025 rounds, then any client's statement of the funding a report changed.

Peers: 34.8× the median of 144 global-health models. The gap comes from what the model is. A money mover's dollar moved is valued at GiveWell's Top Charities, live (0.0615 uov/$, about 31 healthy years per $1,000), and it moves and changes about $1.12 per dollar of running costs (`moved_per_dollar`). The result sits among the site's other global-health money movers (One for the World 1.95, The Life You Can Save 2.64, Effektiv Spenden 3.45, Giving What We Can 10.2). No row from another programme, year or counting rule explains the gap, so it is noted here only, never in site text.
