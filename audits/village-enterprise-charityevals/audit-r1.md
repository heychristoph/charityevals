## Drivers

1. `persistence_years` (judgment, 3–12, 1.77–4.92): anchored on the programme's own 2.5-year follow-up and on graduation and cash-grant long-run studies. The second DREAMS endline (IDinsight, due 2026) is the best improvement.
2. `fade_rate` (judgment, 0–20%, 4.50–2.45): anchored on the core trial's cohorts and the outside studies. The same endline would narrow it.
3. `unmodelled_benefits_adj` (judgment, 3.20–4.87): needs the computed government row and independent ranges (finding 4).
4. `attribution_share_shared` (judgment, 3.15–4.51): needs to be built from partners' resources (finding 1).
5. `spillover_ratio` (judgment, 0–40%, 3.48–4.87): neither trial measured it. The DREAMS second endline plans to.

`core_uganda_itt_month` and `core_kenya_itt_month` (observed) come next, then the shared value of a year of healthy life and the log-consumption rate.

## Plausibility

The result is 55.8× the global-health median of 112 models (lint far-above, medium). That median is dominated by rich-country service and relief models. Against models of the same kind, consumption gains for the extreme poor valued on the log scale, it sits beside GiveWell's GiveDirectly (3.96).

The row that explains the gap is `ln_gain_per_person` (0.40 per person, present value): 4–18% consumption gains for households at about $380 per person a year, lasting about 6 discounted full-gain years, at $442 per household. Both RCTs measure the gains, and IDinsight's own DREAMS CEA (break-even in under two years at a 10% discount rate) is consistent with this. The evidence supports the gap, so no fix follows from it.

The summary and write-up contain no comparison with other charities, medians or peers. The persistence studies and the evidence-adjustment anchor are named only in row explainers, which is allowed.

Missing-data markers against 2.12:
- DREAMS Tanzania thin: correct (community-based organisations deliver; untested).
- DREAMS Ethiopia measured: correct (same programme, same delivery by Village Enterprise with Mercy Corps; the setting is in `dreams_ethiopia_evidence_adj`).
- DREAMS Uganda measured: correct.
- Core measured: except SPRINT, which is finding 2.

Side-effect tagging (2.16): spillovers and the unmodelled share are tagged `added`; mortality, the counterfactual and delivery stay in the main chain. That is correct.
