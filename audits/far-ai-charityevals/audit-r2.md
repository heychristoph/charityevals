Misuse work cuts the deaths in catastrophes the world survives about 450 times more per dollar than misalignment work, so it carries nearly all the value.

4. `@next_dollar.far_ai`: our sourced estimate. It moves the result from 0.37 to 2.4. Its "use" factor ignores the restricted grant money (finding 2).
5. `ai_misuse_cost_share`: this model's own judgment, anchored on FAR.AI's 12 papers, the 990's red-teaming and the grantee's project page. It moves the result from 0.59 to 1.84. The grant's part is understated (finding 1).

Findings 1 and 2 pull in opposite directions and largely cancel (together 1.080 → 0.84). Both should be applied, because each is that row's own question answered from evidence. What would most improve the model is a spending split of FAR.AI's research between misuse and misalignment work, such as staff-time or compute by project, from FAR.AI itself. That would replace the paper count, which is the one model-specific judgment among the top five. Everything else that drives the result is shared and moves every catastrophic-risk model alike.
