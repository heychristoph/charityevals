## Drivers
1. `$missing_data_discount` (shared slider; every branch is thin, correctly so under the glossary: reach is spending over a judged cost, or the effect has no trial).
2. `$uov_per_daly` (shared).
3. `occ_inkind_equivalence`: a judgment with no external reference class (finding 2).
4. `$uov_per_ln_consumption` (shared estimate).
5. `relief_cost_ratio_vs_ss` and `food_price_per_kg`: judgments that set relief's people reached and value per person. They are anchored on the one response (South Sudan) with both spending and reach published.

Most improvement would come from three things. First, a sourced in-kind equivalence for the shoeboxes. Second, a consumption baseline that follows the relief's regional mix (finding 1). Third, Samaritan's Purse's own count of people reached abroad by response, which would replace the South Sudan cost-per-person extrapolation and could make the relief branch measured. The result is a third of the cause median, explained in the summary: mostly goods valued at consumption, few deaths averted, and evangelism at zero value with its cost kept in. No finding moves it more than 25%.
