## Drivers

1. `def_risk_cut` (judgment, 0.17; tornado 2.20e-5 – 1.42e-4): anchored on THL's models and the lobbying literature. It is the weakest link, and no measured outcome exists.
2. `def_revert_share` (judgment, 0.3; 2.46e-5 – 1.38e-4): shared with THL's models.
3. `@next_dollar.aspca` (estimate, 0.3; 2.80e-5 – 1.40e-4): sourced from the accounts.
4. `def_p_override_without` (judgment, 0.3; 2.46e-5 – 1.16e-4): needs its source fixed (finding 4).
5. `def_others_spend` and `def_spend_share` (judgments; together 3.5e-5 – 1.2e-4): the ASPCA's credit. They are the inputs most open to data, through the Schedule C lobbying figures of the coalition's members (findings 1 and 2).

What would most improve the model: tie the defence credit to the coalition members' reported lobbying (one shared coalition figure, each charity's own Schedule C share), then keep the event's draw the same as THL's.
