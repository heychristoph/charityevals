## Double counting (with feeding-america-charityevals)

- Cost: disjoint lines (member services, food procurement and programs there; policy, research and awareness here), same overhead ratio, both summing to the record's $270.395m. No overlap.
- SNAP: the food network model counts individual enrolments into existing SNAP from application help; this model counts policy-level money (a new programme, a smaller cut). The enrolees' benefits are not in the money won. No overlap.
- Research and awareness side benefits (+2% each) are not credited in the food network model's food or SNAP branches. No overlap; finding 6 concerns how the donations are valued, not double counting.
- Shared figures: both read `@public_finance.mcpf`, the same `payer_multiple` (1.4, 1–2) and the CRS benefit level. Consistent except finding 10.
