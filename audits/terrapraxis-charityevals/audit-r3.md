## Drivers
1. `@climate.advanced_nuclear_premium_usd_per_mwh_ind` (shared judgment, PCGE-anchored): the top swing. Its discount rate (finding 1), its coal row (finding 2) and its comparator (finding 4) can each flip the sign on their own.
2. `nuc_attribution_share_shared` and `p_success` (judgments): they scale tonnes and money alike, so they set the result's size, not its sign.
3. `$win_years_counted`, `$missing_data_discount`, `$discount_rate` (shared). The discount rate moves the money only through discounting today, not through the LCOE (finding 1).
4. `build_share_ind` (judgment, IEA's under-construction list): India is 10% of the build but 80% of the cost, because of 1 / $628.
5. `@climate.advanced_nuclear_premium_usd_per_mwh_chn` (shared judgment): its MWh weights are wrong (finding 4).

Checked and sound:
- Payers are valued by 2.21's formula, at 2.14's survey scale. Premium and consumption are both in nominal market-rate dollars, and the carbon cost is removed from the comparators, so the tonnes are not counted twice.
- The extra MWh take the same chain as the tonnes: the coal-site extra share, all of the other line, p_success, credit, the discounted years and the wait. The moved reactors carry no MWh.
- The evidence and missing-data steps apply to the money as to the tonnes. Here the evidence question is whether the deployment happens and whether enabling work moves it, which governs the costs as much as the tonnes. 2.21's exclusion is for rules whose evidence is about benefits only.
- No peer comparison in site text. The summary's and write-up's figures match the live model: -0.03, four fifths from India, savings a quarter of the cost, money several times the tonnes, $630 / $4,200 / $32,700, and still negative at the low end of every other input.
- The new shared facts are sound to read where they reproduce PCGE. For other models (CATF reads the US premium), findings 1, 4 and 6 apply to the shared facts themselves: the rate, the MWh weights and the 2018 dollars.

What would most improve the model: one replaced-power mix per region, used for the tonnes, the smoke and the cost comparator, with the LCOE at the site's discount rate (findings 1 and 4). After those two fixes the sign is a real open question rather than an artefact of the 7% rate.
