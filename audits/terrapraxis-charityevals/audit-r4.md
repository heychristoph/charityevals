## Far-above (medium from audit-model)
The 17.6x gap to the cause's median traces to one chain, and the explanation (money effects at Indian payers' consumption) holds mechanically. Money is 81% of the net (155,399 of 191,500 tonnes-equivalent), 51% of the money falls on India, and India's payers' consumption is $628 a year, so a dollar saved there counts about 52 times a US dollar under 2.21's rule. The tonnes alone (36,100 a year) would be 0.006 years per $1,000, near the cause's higher models. The explanation holds only conditionally: the money exists because India's gap is -5.76 at 4% (the average of two Indian coal plants, at a rate below PCGE's 7%) and the clean part is taken at no gap. With the plant-level clean gap of finding 1 the money is near zero or negative, and the headline falls to the same order as the tonnes' value or below it. The gap is therefore a consequence of finding 1, not independent evidence for a high result. Say so in this audit file, not in the summary or write-up.
