## Far-above (medium from audit-model)
The 11.7x gap to the cause's median is explained and the reasons hold. Money is 72% of the value (91,059 of 127,172 before the 5% other work; money 2.5 times the tonnes, as the write-up says). It is mostly construction saved on coal-site reactors: the siting saving on reactors moved from other sites (208 of 343) plus the extras' saving per MWh (182), less a net gap against the power replaced (47, most of it India's clean-power gap of -90). It falls on payers with low consumption: China and the middle-income builders at $4,218 a year count about 7.8 times a US payer's dollar under the site's 1 / consumption rule, which is where the money gets its weight. The input behind it, a 25% saving (DOE, sourced; 15-35% range), is a sourced fact, not a typed number. The tonnes alone give about 0.006 years per 1,000 dollars, near the cause's higher models; the rest is the money at low-consumption payers. It is not independent evidence of a high result: with the shared uncertainty at +15 per MWh or the system cost at 35 the result is negative or near zero (tornado), and the summary says the sign turns on cost. Not a finding against the model; say it here, not in the site text.
