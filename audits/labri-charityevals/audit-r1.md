5. `$missing_data_discount` (slider), then `implementation_adj` (judgment, 0.37–2.24; finding 1) and `@next_dollar.labri` (sourced estimate).

Ranges: the model's own ranges span 4.4e-4 to 194 jointly (audit-model's spread note). Each range is a stated judgment with its reasons, and the summary already says the result is very uncertain, so no change is needed beyond findings 1–3. Point values on the path (seed_grant, births, older adults) are observed and verified.

Peers: ×9.4 the global-health median, inside the 10× band. Against the lead models, the gap with the other battery-recycling model comes mostly from the policy's chance (issue_record_adj 1 here vs 0.4 there; a later stage and an industry endorsement, both sourced), the Philippines' larger birth cohort and `exposure_life_years` (finding 3). No gap comes from a different counting rule. Not for site text.

What would most improve the model: a measured share of Philippine children's blood lead from unlicensed smelting (the charity's planned environmental study around Metro Manila), a published draft of the EPR rule, and the charity's first accounts.
