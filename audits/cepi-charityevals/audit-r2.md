Judgment on the cancellation. `bio_scale_adj` = mission cut ÷ (the shared catastrophe cut × funders' dollars × years), and the catastrophe-size parts multiply it by that same shared cut, so they cancel. The extinction parts multiply it by `bio_extinction_cut_per_million`, so the result moves with the ratio of the two Rethink cuts (tornado: extinction cut low/high 0.27/3.06, catastrophe cut low/high 3.06/0.27). This is acceptable and I would not change it: (a) it is what the anchor implies, since CEPI's cut is the mission's estimated cut, which doesn't depend on the typical rate, and the one thing taken from Rethink is how extinction risk relates to catastrophe risk; (b) every shared slider still moves the model; (c) the units are consistent (the mission's cut over the whole effort's spend against the typical rate on the same spend, both per dollar, the developers' co-investment kept in `bio_attribution_share` only); (d) the cap at 1 is not reached anywhere in the ranges (0.69 at the catastrophe cut's low end). The cost is a tornado entry that runs the "wrong" way for the catastrophe cut; the explainer, summary and write-up say so. A better form would need a sourced extinction cut for the mission, which does not exist.
