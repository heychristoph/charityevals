2. `@next_dollar.the_bail_project` (estimate, 29%): sourced to the 2025 report and the 990, dated within the year.

5. `tx_attribution_share` and `tx_counterfactual_adj` (judgments, 10% and 30%): unanchored coalition shares (finding 6).

The model would improve most from fixing the defence years for the blocked bills (finding 1) and the Texas dates (finding 2), anchoring the Texas expansion on the Harris County Prop 3 figures, and building the coalition shares from members' spending.
