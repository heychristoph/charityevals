## Points checked and found right
1. **Co-advocates and co-deliverers only.** attr_research (75%): the shares named (the International AI Safety Report led by Bengio with two GovAI writers, the forecasts with the Forecasting Research Institute, STREAM with outside co-authors, the thresholds survey with the OECD) are co-authors and co-researchers; no adopter, regulator or company that uses the research is in it; no co-advocate dropped. attr_talent (95%): GovAI pays salaries, supervisors and office; outside speakers and secondment hosts are co-deliverers; the alumni's employers are explicitly left to the mobiliser's share. Only attr_policy fails (finding 1).
2. **fb_credit_share = 1 (0.5 to 1) is a defensible best guess.** The share of alumni work credited to the programme (fb_share_working) comes from the alumni's own answer about what the fellowship added (career a year sooner, impact +50%, so a third of later work), against their other options; cf_talent (0.9) asks only whether another organisation would have run these places (IAPS, ERA, Pivotal, all smaller). No source names another programme that trained or placed the same fellows or scholars, so there is no overlap to put a number on; asking it again would cut the same alumni twice. Range 0.5 to 1 leaves room for overlap nobody has measured.
3. **Other field-building rows were not set to make up for the old cut.** fb_work_years (the shared 5 years, 2-10), fb_counterfactual (cf_talent 0.9, a different question), talent_evidence_adj (1, 0.7-1.2), attr_talent (0.95) and fb_cost_share (29%, 17-40%) are each argued on their own grounds; none mentions the old credit share. Only fb_share_working has the inconsistency in finding 2, and it predates the change. fb_work_dollars = 96 x 22% x 4.6 discounted years x $250,000 = $24.4M, 7 times the training's $3.5M; the chain after it is the template's (typical cut per $1M, years counted).
4. **Class.** `attribution_share` (class research) = attribution_share_shared x mobiliser_share, read once in `total_adj`; the same row serves the research, the training (alumni's employers) and the Code (a win, which also reads the setting), so one class is right for all three. Nothing delivery-type is in the model (no own services, grants or purchases). Linear check: the headline at mobiliser_share 5% is 0.5325, exactly 5% of 10.65 (tornado), so the share is taken once. No compliance cost row (pol_wins explainer: none sourced, so none counted).
