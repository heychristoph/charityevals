## Drivers

1. `$missing_data_discount` (shared slider; thin is right: reach is judged and the effect comes from judge-leniency studies, not the charity's clients).
2. @incarceration.jail_mortality_vs_prison, a judgment behind the deaths channel (60% of the result); its anchor needs rewriting (finding 5).
3. @the_bail_project.detention_counterfactual_share, a judgment anchored on Dobbie et al.'s 55% three-day release; how it relates to the charity's days figure needs settling (finding 4).
4. @incarceration.life_years_lost_per_year_incarcerated, a sourced estimate (Patterson 2013), shared with Prison Policy Initiative and Brennan.
5. @incarceration.release_earnings_gain, a sourced estimate (DGY Table 7), imprecise.

What would most improve the model: the fiscal-2025 rebuild on the charity's own yearly days count (finding 1), and the charity's method for "days of pretrial incarceration prevented", which would settle findings 2 and 4 together.
