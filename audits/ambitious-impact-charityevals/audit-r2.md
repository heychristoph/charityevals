## Drivers
1. `$missing_data_discount` (shared slider, 0.6–42): the projected fundraising, the destinations' idea-stage figures and the small informed branches.
2. `@meta.prior_vs_cash` (shared): most of the founded money is institutional and counts only for its gain.
3. `$discount_rate` (shared): the charities' money comes over years.
4. `@next_dollar.ambitious_impact`, 36% (sourced estimate, verified).
5. `founding_counterfactual_share`, 60% (judgment, anchored on the site's meta counterfactual shares; 9.2–18.6). The best improvement would be a reference class from its own ideas: how many of its recommended ideas others launched independently.

The Effective Giving Initiatives branch is the biggest structural gap (finding 1). Built on a money-moved chain, it would likely become a top-5 driver.

Peers: the result sits below the money-moving peers that value money the same way (giving-what-we-can-charityevals 55.9, givewell-charityevals 36.0, effektiv-spenden-charityevals 25.6). It is more than ten times the cause's median, and the summary states why. Fixing findings 1–3 would raise it to about 20–35, still in the range of those peers.

Verdict: fix needed (1 high: finding 1; 1 medium: finding 2; 3 low).
