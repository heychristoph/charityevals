## Drivers
1. `$missing_data_discount` (shared slider): reaches the result only through the destinations' own thin steps and the small informed branches. It would also reach founded money with finding 1's fix.
2. `@next_dollar.ambitious_impact`, 36% (sourced estimate, room 0.6 × use 0.6 × run 1.0, verified against the accounts).
3. `$discount_rate` (shared): the charities' money comes over years.
4. `lifetime_vs_to_date`, 2.5 (judgment, no reference class). This would gain most from data: the cohorts' fundraising by age, from GiveWell and Open Philanthropy grants and the charities' accounts.
5. `aim_share_of_founding`, 50% (judgment answering two questions, finding 6).

Peers: giving-what-we-can-charityevals 56.0, givewell-charityevals 36.0 and effektiv-spenden-charityevals 25.7 value money moved the same way, and this model sits just below them. rethink-priorities-charityevals-cross-cause (0.025) is research that informs money, like this model's research and grantmaker-training branches. Those branches are valued the same way here (3% of $5M, evidence 0.6, missing-data step) and are just as small. Taken together, findings 1–5 move the result roughly from 20 to 13–16. That stays in the range of the money-moving peers, more than ten times the cause's median, for the stated reason.
