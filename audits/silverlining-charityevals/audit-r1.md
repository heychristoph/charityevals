Cooling into tonnes, checked: cooling C for Y years ÷ (TCRE × the 280 years a tonne's heat deaths are counted) is exact if a tonne's warming stays level and each degree-year kills the same number of people over 2020-2300. ## Drivers
- Top inputs: `$discount_rate` (shared; heat deaths decades away, and finding 1 changes how strongly), `decision_value_share` (judgment with no reference class; the largest own driver), `$deaths_per_tco2e` (shared), `cooling_c`, `p_deployment` and `deployment_years` (judgments anchored on ARISE-SAI, IPCC Table SPM.1 and the 2025 survey), and the field denominator (`field_growth`, `field_years_to_decision`, judgments around the sourced $44M a year).
- Double counting: none beyond findings 8 and 12. Overhead is spread once; each branch's counterfactual and credit are separate questions. The federal line's counterfactual (displaced NOAA science) and the side effect (science useful beyond cooling) point opposite ways and don't overlap. The directed money is net of what would have happened anyway once.
- What would most improve the model: fixes 1-3 (timing, the single event, the shared advocacy share). Then the tracker's NOAA line (finding 4). Then an anchor for `decision_value_share`: the value-of-information literature on solar geoengineering, or expert elicitation on how research changes deployment outcomes. A further, smaller assumption is that heat deaths per degree-year during 2040-2090 equal their 2020-2300 average. Population then is near its peak, so this likely understates; state it in `evidence_adj`'s explainer.
