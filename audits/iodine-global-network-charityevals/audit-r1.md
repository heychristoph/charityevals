## Drivers
- Shared: `$win_years_counted`, `$discount_rate`, `$missing_data_discount`, `$uov_per_daly`. Not the builder's to narrow.
- `net_share_protected` (judgment, 0.05%, the largest of the model's own swings: 3.15–11.4). Anchored on one Moldova law. It would most improve with a count of the laws and standards changed after IGN reviews or PAT use in 2019–2025 (IGN's own tool reports), turned into wins a year (finding 12).
- `severity_vs_meta` (judgment, 0.5: 2.22–7.87). Reference class: Feyrer et al. and Gowachirapant et al. 2017. A meta-analysis of iodine trials in mildly deficient pregnant women would settle it.
- `@next_dollar.iodine_global_network` (estimate, 0.56: 2.36–6.70). Sourced to the accounts. Published reserves would firm up room.
- `iodine_evidence_adj` (judgment, 0.7: 2.84–5.68). Anchored on GiveWell's salt model.
- `qa_quality_gain` and `vnm_share_protected` (judgments). Producers' or national salt-iodine survey results before and after the support (CENIN, SUNIBROM, Madagascar's control agencies) would turn the QA branch from judgment into measurement. The final Decree 09 text decides the Vietnam branch (finding 5).
