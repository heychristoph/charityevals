## Drivers
1. `$discount_rate` (shared): 0.1% → +0.011, 8% → -0.0024. It moves the late benefits and, through `rate_position`, the amortized capital.
2. `$uov_per_daly` and `@public_funds.compliance_cost_payer_multiple` (shared): they set how much a dollar of compliance cost weighs against a death.
3. `@compliance_costs.scaqmd_rule_1146_2_capital_cost_factor`: judgment, flips the sign at 0.8. Needs its reference class (#5).
4. `evidence_adj`: judgment, flips the sign at 1.1. Once it also scales the NOx deaths (#1), the net is less sensitive to it and is pushed further negative.
5. `@climate.south_coast_deaths_per_nox_ton_per_day`: sourced (the district's BenMAP, via staff slides). The final 2022 AQMP socioeconomic report itself, or CARB's South Coast incidence-per-ton factors as a cross-check, would most improve it.

Peers: negative, and below a tenth of the climate median in absolute terms only because benefits and costs nearly cancel under the 2.21 rule. Benefits alone are of the order of the cause median, and the gap comes from counting compliance costs, which the evidence supports, so the gap itself is not a finding. Findings #1, #3, #4 and #6 are small corrections to the benefit side, but each moves the thin net a lot. Applied together in a scratch copy (#1 to #4, #6 left out), they give -0.00217: still negative, about 2.5× today's harm.

Verdict: fix needed (1 high: #1).
