## Drivers
1. `missing_data_discount` (shared slider, 0.0019–0.0189). Which branches take it is the model's choice; finding 1 says Peru shouldn't.
2. `ter_counterfactual_adj` (judgment, 0.0052–0.0165). Anchored on RFUS's own RCT, scaled for thinner cover. A per-country split would ground it better (finding 1).
3. The shared catastrophe facts: deforestation share of land-use risk, pandemic probability, land-use share of emergence. They give 57% of the result and are the same for every forest model.
4. `permanence_share` (judgment 0.2–0.9, anchored on the trial's year-2 fade and on Cool Earth).
5. `discount_rate` (shared).

What would most improve the model:
- RFUS's own monitoring data for 2025: alerts acted on, and patrol days and pay per patroller. That would ground the pay branch's 60 days and $8.
- A tree-cover-loss comparison of patrolled against unpatrolled communities (from Global Forest Watch, which RFUS already uses). That could make more of the territories branch measured.
- Partner budgets, to build the attribution share from resources.
