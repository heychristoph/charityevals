## Drivers
1. `$missing_data_discount` (shared slider): scales every branch. It is the site's, not the builder's.
2. `$uov_per_daly`, `$uov_per_ln_consumption` (shared): the conversions behind 92% of the result.
3. `displaced_consumption_share` (judgment, 45%, 30–70%): the 4th-largest swing. Anchored on "the site's other relief models", which is a peer convention, not a measured reference class. Survey data on camp refugees' consumption in eastern Chad (WFP/UNHCR food-security or vulnerability assessments) would anchor it, and finding 2 tells it which population to describe.
4. `cva_counterfactual_adj` (judgment, 70%, 45–90%) and `cva_share` (judgment, 30%, 20–40%): the cash branch's size. Finding 6 would turn `cva_share` into an accounts-based figure.
5. `eco_consumption_gain` and `leg_status_uplift` (judgments): both anchored (graduation trials; PEP natural experiment), with gaps covered in findings 3 and 7.

What would most improve the model: the RSC Austria branch (finding 1); the cash branch on its recipients' own locations (finding 2); reach matched to the fiscal year (finding 4).
