## Drivers
1. `$ai_catastrophe_chance` (shared slider, the tournament's experts; 0.012–0.65 across its range).
2. `$discount_rate` (shared; 1.57 at the 0.1% floor, where the future dominates).
3. The AI cut per $1 million (shared estimate, Rethink's placeholder preset, ×10 either way; finding 1).
4. `$win_years_counted` and `$missing_data_discount` (shared).
5. The model's own: `@next_dollar` 0.35 (sourced estimate), `outreach_evidence_adj` 1 (judgment anchored on NTI's 0.8 and the book's measured reach), `counterfactual_adj` 0.75 (judgment, NTI's 0.7 the anchor).

The result is set almost entirely by shared figures; MIRI's own judgments move it within about 2× either way. What would most improve the model: a sourced reason for AI's cut per dollar beyond Rethink's linear extrapolation from one megaproject (for example, the implied cost per basis point against published AI funders' bars, in the write-up only), and any measured outcome of the book on officials or legislation (testimony, citations in bills), which would set `outreach_evidence_adj` on evidence rather than reach.
