## Drivers
1. `imo_jet_share`: judgment from T&E's revenue chart (verified). It drives the result from −0.07 to 1.01. The real uncertainty is the Fund's undecided split. The ends (10%, 70%) are reasonable.
2. `@aid_value.oda_vs_cash`: shared judgment, but the wrong shared figure for this use (finding 1).
3. `$win_years_counted` and `$missing_data_discount`: shared sliders.
4. `imo_attribution_share_shared`: judgment, 3% (1–8%). It is anchored on the shipping budget (about a third to half of £2.5M, which the restricted-fund purposes on p. 36 support) against a field of "tens of millions". A sourced total for the field (ClimateWorks' and ECF's shipping grantmaking, the Clean Shipping Coalition members' IMO budgets) would most improve it.
5. `@climate.advocacy_share_of_policy_win`, `imo_p_adopted`, and the compliance-cost payers (`imo_payer_low_income_share`, `imo_low_income_payer_consumption`), which set the size of the negative term.

Peers: ×309 the climate median. The row that makes the gap is `imo_jet_uov`. In the site's tonnes a tonne is worth about 0.0036 uov, about one dollar of cash to the very poor. So a branch that moves aid money at about $1.1 of aid per dollar spent (before the next-dollar step) dominates any tonnes. Valuing aid won at what aid does is the site's rule (2.20), and the counting rule itself is not the gap. What is wrong in the gap is the figure (finding 1), the missing evidence step (finding 2) and the missing displacement (finding 3). After them the model sits near zero. The remaining distance from the median would then come from the negative compliance-cost term, which is the site's rule (2.21) applied to a framework whose cost per tonne is well above a tonne's value.

What would most improve the model: the IMO's own revenue-distribution decision when it comes (it would replace both the just-transition share and the aid figure with a known use), and a volume-weighted incidence of shipping costs by country income from the IMO's comprehensive impact assessment, which costed trade and food-price effects by state. That would set the payer share and the payers' consumption from one source.

Verdict: fix needed (2 high, 4 medium, 8 low).
