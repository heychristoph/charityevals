## Drivers
1. `$missing_data_discount` (shared slider; both branches thin, correctly: no controlled evaluation of Habitat's homes or repairs, and the value per family rests on judgments).
2. `$uov_per_daly` (shared): the whole result is income.
3. `affiliate_cost_ratio` (judgment, 2.1, 1.5–3.2): its reference class is seven affiliate-years of three large urban affiliates' audited statements (the Houston and San Diego figures are re-verified above). It sets HFHI's credit share. Audited statements from one or two rural or small affiliates would improve it most.
4. `$uov_per_ln_consumption` and `@aid_value.inkind_housing_vs_cash` (shared).
5. `hr_counterfactual_adj` (judgment, 0.8, 0.6–0.95, anchored on CBPP's share of eligible renters with no assistance), then `unmodelled_benefits_adj` and `new_household_size`.

The model's own ranges (joint 0.00032–0.022, combined 0.0012–0.024) reflect real uncertainty; none is arbitrary. The point inputs are audited or reported counts. After the drivers, the most useful improvement is a national figure for Habitat buyers' incomes (finding 2), for example from HFHI's affiliate reporting or HUD's Self-Help Homeownership Opportunity Program (SHOP) data.
