## Checks
- **Corporate branch, no double counting.** The year's $1.55M splits into 70% recurring ($1.085M) and 30% one-off ($465K). The corporate $82,922 one-off is inside the 30% and credited only by `oneoff_moved`. The corporate $252,909 recurring is inside the 70%, taken out of the university numerator once, and the year's recruits are credited once through `corporate_new_run_rate` ($55K, a flow, not the $252,909 stock). The university pledges get $1,074 a year × 166 pledges, with no corporate giving in the gift. The $55K is 22% of the existing corporate recurring book and 24% of `money_moved`; its range moves the result 11.9–17.0, so it is a top-10 driver and a judgment with one anchor (the March 2022 post, $2,800 per talk, 47 talks). That is acceptable, and the one weak step is finding 3.
- **Formula for `active_pledger_years`.** Recent group (415 ÷ 3 per cohort) × S(0,3) and older group (2,498 ÷ 6 per cohort) × S(3,9), with S(a,b) = floor × (b − a) + (first year − floor) × ((1−decay)^a − (1−decay)^b) / decay; correct as written. `cohort_years_giving` (9) less `recent_cohort_years` (3) = 6 older cohorts: 2014-15 to 2019-20 (six full years, though 2014-15 was its first full year; 2,498 includes the 2018-19 total of 1,728, which counts earlier cohorts too). The split of the 2,913 − 2,498 = 415 over three cohorts is 138 a year, below the 166 and 770 of the surrounding years; plausible after the 2020 shock. At the high end (4,000) the extra 1,087 all lands in the three recent cohorts (500 a year), which is implausible; but the high end only lowers the result (a stated downside), and the tornado does not rank it.
- **`stock_retention_link`.** Direction right: faster attrition thins the book, so fewer pledgers give, and the gift per pledger rises; own_retention_adj = 1 gives no change. At the best guess the factor is 0.895; at 0.45 it is 0.835.
- **`activation_wait_years`.** Applied to `pledge_moved` only, as 1.04^−1.5; the curve's 59% first-year share already allows for some non-activation, so the wait is a timing shift on top. Slight overlap, not worth a change (effect about 1% either way).
- **Anchors in the new text.** All verified (Sources checked below).
