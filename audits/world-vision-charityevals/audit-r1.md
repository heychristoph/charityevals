# Audit: world-vision-charityevals, round 1

Result: 3.382e-4 daly/$ = 0.338 years of healthy life per $1,000 (combined range 0.079–1.44); global-health median 0.338 (×1.00). Verdict: fail (1 high).
Model-sha: 5ecd476744fe9ff7b22b0a91463517f014d23921779571449b00351609763949

Scope: the "international" line of charities/world-vision.json. That is World Vision, Inc.'s Form 990 Part III lines 4a (international programmes, $987,725,050) and 4c (public awareness and education, $3,372,141), fiscal year to 30 September 2025. Both match the 990 and the KPMG combined statements. The domestic line ($230,992,460) is the sister model, so the lines add up to programme services of $1,222,089,651 (Part IX line 25). There is no overlap with the sister model. The international line holds $673.4M of cash grants abroad and $260.9M of noncash grants (Schedule F Part II). The noncash grants are $217.2M of food commodities and $43.0M of donated goods (apparel, furniture, toys, house fixtures, medical products) at US wholesale value. They are 71.4% Sub-Saharan Africa (Schedule F Part I), as `beneficiaries` has it. The model splits the line into relief (45%, a judgment) and five development branches (health, water, livelihoods, education, faith at zero value) plus advocacy. Each branch is on its family's chain, as Convention 4 allows for a cross-family portfolio. The latest year with audited financials is used.

## Drivers

1. `$missing_data_discount` (shared slider, 0.5): 0.068–0.677. Every branch is correctly thin: reach is spending over a judged cost, and there is no controlled evaluation of World Vision's own delivery.
2. `$uov_per_daly` (shared): 0.588–0.213.
3. `livelihood_consumption_gain` (judgment, 1.5%): anchored on the savings-group trials. Its reach is overstated (finding 2), and graduation's stronger evidence is lumped in with it.
4. `relief_counterfactual_adj` (judgment, 0.6): needs FTS coverage numbers (finding 5).
5. `$uov_per_ln_consumption` (shared estimate); then `health_cost_per_child` (judgment, $150 per birth) and `affected_consumption_share` (judgment, 0.55).

What would most improve the model: tie each development branch's reach to the partnership's published counts × World Vision US's share (finding 2), give the overseas goods their own branch (finding 3), and add relief attribution for WFP and US government supply chains (finding 4). Together these lower the result by roughly 20–25% (to about 0.26–0.28). That still sits within 2× of the global-health median and between the humanitarian peers ARC international (0.073) and MSF relief (0.70). At 1.00× the cause median, the result needs no outlier statement.

Findings by severity: high 1, medium 5, low 4.
