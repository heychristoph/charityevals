## Drivers
1. `$missing_data_discount` (shared slider, 0.0027–0.027): read as is.
2. `bld_refill_years` (judgment, 0.0034–0.0225): unanchored (finding 1). A count of how fast US centres have added collection and testing capacity would settle it best.
3. `bld_death_risk_without` (judgment, 0.0057–0.025): anchored on Carson 2002. It would improve with a cohort of massive-haemorrhage patients during a shortage.
4. `bld_rationed_value_share` (judgment, 0.0068–0.024): anchored on restrictive thresholds and NBCUS slack. Hospital data on which procedures were postponed during the 2022 crisis would help.
5. `bld_initial_unreplaced_share` (judgment, 0.0068–0.019): anchored on the national shortage declarations.

All five top drivers are judgments, or the shared slider. What would most improve the model is evidence on how fast other blood centres can replace lost supply.

Verdict: pass (no high findings; 1 medium, 5 low).
