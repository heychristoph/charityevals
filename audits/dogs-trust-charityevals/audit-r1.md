# Audit: dogs-trust-charityevals, round 1

Result: 0.0626 ced/$ = 6.88e-6 years of healthy life per $1,000 (combined range 9.3e-7 – 5.1e-5); cause median 7.57e-4 (×0.009). Verdict: **fix needed**.

Scope: rehoming (£64.407m), prevention (£19.975m), brand (£6.460m) and 75% of international work (£26.099m × (1 − `@dogs_trust.rabies_share_of_international` 0.25)), year to 31 Dec 2025, with fundraising spread by `overhead_ratio` 1.392. cost_total $202.5m. With the rabies model ($8.60m × 1.392 = $11.97m) the two together come to total expenditure £162.805m × 1.3175 = $214.5m. Costs aren't double counted. charities/dogs-trust.json `spending` ($154.07m = £116.941m) and `lines` (145.47m + 8.60m) match the SOFA (PDF p. 48) and note 7 (PDF p. 52).

Mechanical: lint 0 high / 0 medium / 1 low (far-below; the summary gives the reason). check.ts: all models valid. normalization-audit: no violation for this model (every INV section 0 violations). simulation-check: stable (5% 0.24×, 95% 3.26× the best guess).

Previous round: none (round 1).

Where the result comes from at the defaults: the Freedom survivors' WELLBYs give 0.0568 of the 0.0626 ced/$ (91%). The dogs' days give the rest. Of the dogs' 756,226 adjusted days, neutering gives 662,396 (88%), treatment 62,967, rehoming 20,070, prevention 5,861, policy 4,045 and support schemes 888. The human channels therefore decide the result, and any human channel left out matters far more than any dog branch.

## Double counting with the rabies model
- Costs: the shared fact splits international work 25/75, and the two cost_totals sum to group total expenditure. No overlap.
- Outcomes: this model counts no vaccinations or rabies deaths. The rabies model counts dogs spared rabies only as 1% (`other_unmodelled_share`) and no treatment or sterilisation. No overlap.
- One small overlap: the £3m behind `grant_sterilisations` includes parts of the Soi Dog and Hope & Animal Trust grants that the fact gives to rabies (finding 9c).
- `salary_gap` differs between the two models (`gap_uk_charity` here, `gap_local_delivery` in the rabies model) for the same group payroll. It has no effect at the defaults (`donor_cost_share` = 1). The rabies audit should check it.

## Drivers
1. `freedom_credit` (judgment, no reference class beyond the report's own text): 2.7e-6 – 1.7e-5.
2. `freedom_ls_gain` (judgment, unsourced): 2.2e-6 – 1.2e-5.
3. `$missing_data_discount` and `$daly_per_wellby` (shared sliders), through the people branch.
4. The hidden one-year duration of the people gain (finding 2), worth ×1.87 at two years.
5. On the dogs' side, `pain_days_per_unowned_life` and `births_averted_per_neuter` (template placeholders, finding 8).

What would most improve the model: findings 1–4. The model's result is effectively a people-wellbeing model attached to a dog charity, so every channel to people (owners, children, tenants) needs a row, and the Freedom row needs its duration, its split credit and a sourced effect. The dog branches matter little until the conversion of animal days changes.

Findings: 4 high, 4 medium, 6 low.
