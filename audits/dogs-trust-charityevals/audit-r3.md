## Double counting
- Bites against the rabies model: the bite row counts non-rabies injuries only and the rabies model counts no neutering; no overlap. The same dog-years feed the bite row and the dog rows' pain days (neutering), but they value different beneficiaries (people bitten vs the dogs' own pain), so no double count.
- Freedom: one counterfactual (`freedom_credit`) and a persistence in years; no overlap. Children and survivors share both, as they should.
- Owners (prevention) and dogs (prevention) are separate beneficiaries; the owners' formula carries the branch's counterfactual, attribution and delivery. It leaves out `prev_evidence_adj` on purpose (the effect is the judgment itself); say so in the row's explainer.
