## Drivers
1. Legal-branch changed-case share `legal_outcome_change` (judgment, 3–25%): anchor misquoted; needs a reference class.
2. `legal_ln_gain_per_year` and `legal_years` (judgments 1.5 and 20 years): no removal-probability step (finding 2).
3. The shared `missing_data_discount` and `uov_per_daly` (site-wide sliders): move the result 10× and 4×.
4. `svc_counterfactual_adj`, `recipients_consumption_share` and the assistance at cost ($270 a person from the accounts, observed): the services branch is sourced on cost and reach, judged on the earnings brought forward (4% × $8,000).
5. `legal_evidence_adj` (judgment 0.6): partly redundant with the 10%.

What would most improve the model: findings 1 and 2 (value the side effects as rows; add the step from changed outcome to removal), then 3 and 4 (the legal clients' count and the Eagly & Shafer anchor).
