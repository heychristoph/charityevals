## Drivers
1. `$discount_rate` (shared slider, 67 to 4.5) and `$win_years_counted` (shared, 1.2 to 16): the site's assumptions, not this model's.
2. `$missing_data_discount` (shared, 1.1 to 10.8): applies to every branch, rightly, since no outcome of the work is measured.
3. `@catastrophe.bio_extinction_cut_per_million` / `bio_catastrophe_cut_per_million` (shared estimates, Rethink's placeholders ×10 either way: 3.0 to 28.8): the area's typical dollar, which carries 84% of the result.
4. `@next_dollar.centre_for_long_term_resilience`, 0.53 (0.21–0.9): a sourced estimate built from room, use and run. Its quote and figures are verified.
5. `bio_evidence_adj`, 0.85 (0.3–1.3): a judgment with a reference class (the Council on Strategic Risks 0.8, the Johns Hopkins Center 0.9) and named past uptake (Founders Pledge 2023, verified).

What would most improve the model:
- Evidence of uptake of the biosecurity unit's recommendations in the year, such as a government response to "Two Years On" or screening guidance citing CLTR. That would replace the 0.85 judgment with an observed figure.
- CLTR's own income and expenditure figures (from the charity, or a full set of accounts). These would replace the built cost.
- A published budget or funding gap for the room factor of the next-dollar figure.
