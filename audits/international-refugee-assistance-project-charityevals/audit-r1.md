## Drivers
1. `$missing_data_discount` (shared; correctly applied: no count of clients reaching safety, no evaluation).
2. `share_reaching_safety`: a judgment, 3–20%. The best improvement would be IRAP's own outcome tracking (arrivals and status wins per year by programme), which IRAP's earlier reports show it keeps ("percentage of positive outcomes among cases with known outcomes"). Asking IRAP for FY2025 arrivals would turn the top judgment into a count and could lift the missing-data discount.
3. `counterfactual_adj`: a judgment, 20–70%; it needs the speed-up term (finding 3).
4. `$uov_per_daly` and `$discount_rate` (shared).
5. `@irap.years_gain_lasts` and `@irap.client_consumption_ppp_day`: judgments in shared facts; the consumption needs the client-mix weight (finding 5).

Net effect of the medium fixes at the suggested values: roughly +25% (2), +12% (3) and −12% (5), together about +25%. The result stays about half the global-health median, explained by about $4,700 of cost per client and the FY2025 suspension of US refugee admissions, as the summary says.

Verdict: fix needed (one high: the INV-4 violation that facts/irap.json introduced; four medium).
