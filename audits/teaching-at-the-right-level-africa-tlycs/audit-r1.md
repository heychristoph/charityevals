## Notes on the points the audit checked and found sound

- Evaluator layer: every sheet cell reproduces (B4-B74, 38 expected values; native `lays_per_100_average` 3.5688 = cell B74). Spot-checked from the downloaded xlsx: costs B5-B7, B9-B11, reach B13-B15, B20-B22, B24, B25-B34 (hlo scores, Singapore 625), B74 and the notes C20, C21, C24, C74. Their typed `B4` is rebuilt as a sum (equal).
- Our view: pooling by money is algebraically adjusted_sd x all pupils / all money, correct; central costs enter as 20% of TaRL's own spending (5-40%), applied once; staff pay follows `$resource_shares` (both `@staff_pay.*` shared defaults); governments' money is in the denominator at full cost, which is the same as credit by share of resources in both settings; the earnings chain reads the shared figures (`@youth_programmes`, `@givewell_shared.long_term_benefit_years`, `resource_sharing_multiplier`, `$discount_rate`, `$uov_per_ln_consumption`) and discounts the wait; the 9-year wait has a reason and an anchor; `estimate_basis: unit_cost` is the classification in normalization.md 2.10; `missing_data` thin 0 is right (reach counted, effect an RCT of the same intervention incl. government-run delivery); no funding counterfactual subtracted; no unit error found (0.075 SD x 10% -> ln(1.0075); a pupil costs about 6.1 dollars; 0.28 uov a pupil).
- Plausibility (lint `far-above`, 312x the median of 114 global-health models): explained by the channel, not an error. 6.1 dollars per pupil buys a 0.75% earnings gain over 40 working years: 0.0075 x 14.5 discounted working years x 2 (sharing) x 1.44 (uov per ln) x 0.8 x 1.1 = 0.28 uov, divided by 6.1 = 0.045 uov per dollar. The median is dominated by US charities whose chains reach few years of healthy life per dollar; the models with earnings or mortality chains on cheap delivery sit at 4-60. The 20,000x plausible range comes from the discount rate, the earnings-per-SD and the effect-size ranges stacked, 76% of it judgment: acceptable for an effect that no study has followed to adult earnings.
- Write-up: no comparison with other charities, medians or peers, no multiple of GiveDirectly; the headline is a live figure.

## Drivers
1. Learning gain per pupil at scale: `sd_increase` x `scale_fidelity_adj` (0.075 SD), estimate and judgment; at the bottom of the trial range, the single biggest lever (finding 1).
2. Adult earnings per SD (shared estimate, US children; no study in this setting) and the discount rate (shared judgment): together set about 14.5 discounted working-year units per ln point.
3. Household sharing (2) and `$uov_per_ln_consumption`: shared.
4. Cost per pupil, 6.1 dollars: charity-supplied, with government money two thirds of it (finding 2).
5. `counterfactual_adoption` 80%: judgment without a reference-class number (finding 3).
The most useful improvement: a central, government-delivered effect size with its source for finding 1, and an account of what the governments' costs contain.
