The XPT numbers are right, but taking the domain experts alone as the best guess is the optimistic end of two groups of equal standing (finding 2).

## Double counting

- `counterfactual_adj` (0.7, "would no one else have done it now") against `years_of_impact` (would it have come later): two different questions, as in every won-reform family on the site. Not a double count.
- `evidence_adj` (NTI against a typical project) against `missing_data_adj` (nothing measured): different questions. 0.8 nets NTI's senior access in diplomacy against communications' longer path. Communications is 25% of the area spending, 38% of the nuclear spending.
- The harm rows against RP's backfire: if finding 3 is applied without zeroing them, dual-use harm counts twice (about 2%).
- XPT causes overlap (an AI-caused nuclear war counts under both, Table 2 note). Each branch applies only its own spending's cut to its own area's risk, so there is no double count.
- `fb_cost_share` 0 with the young leaders inside NTI | bio, and `pol_cost_share` 0 with IBBIS launched in 2024: correct, no overlap.
