Result: 1.92e-5 daly/$ = 0.0192 years of healthy life per $1,000 (combined range 0.0030–0.121); cause median 0.338 (×0.057). Verdict: **fix needed**.

## Drivers

1. `$missing_data_discount` (shared slider): 0.0042–0.038. Every branch but the grants is thin, correctly.
2. `$uov_per_daly` (shared).
3. `ben_income_years` (judgment, 3–25): years of payments an award brings, averaged over awards that are won outright or brought forward. Its anchor is reasoned (lifetime ratings against timing gains) but has no reference class. A VBA or GAO figure on how much sooner represented claims are granted would improve it most.
4. `ben_consumption_share` (judgment, 0.5–1.1): anchored on the CE income fifths and WWP's survey. A sourced income figure for post-9/11 veterans with a service-connected disability rating (ACS, or VA's National Survey of Veterans) would replace the judgment.
5. `ben_counterfactual_adj` (judgment, 0.2–0.7): no anchor beyond a description of free representation. VA or GAO data on grant rates by representation (VSO-represented against unrepresented claimants), with a selection discount, is the reference class to cite.

What would most improve the model: the three medium fixes to the benefits and advocacy branches (2–4), which together could move the result by up to about a third; the INV-14 high, by valuing the side effects as rows; and a sourced counterfactual for the benefits service.

Verdict: fix needed. Findings: 1 high, 5 medium, 7 low.
