**Far below the climate median (×0.052): the gap is supported by the evidence.** It comes from three rows, none a scope, year or counting-rule error. (a) The tonne is worth the least a tonne can be on the site. Removed and stored CO₂ carries no smoke and no methane (`air_pollution_weight` 0 and `methane_share` 0, which is correct under normalization.md 2.5), while most climate peers' tonnes are methane (4.3× a CO₂ tonne) or smoky fuel. (b) The credit the charity gets is small. It gets 0.3% of the advocacy behind the large 45Q credit, which is half the result, and 10% of only 2.5 Mt a year of DAC design capacity, 45% of it delivered. (c) The next dollar counts 0.27 of the average (sourced). Fixing findings 1-3 moves the result about +30%, −11% and −10 to −20%. It stays near 0.05× the median. Peers or the median never go into the summary or write-up, which is correct as written.

## Drivers
1. Shared value judgments: `$discount_rate` (0.0013–7e-6), `$climate_heat_death_delay`, `$win_years_counted`, `$deaths_per_tco2e`, `$missing_data_discount`. They are the site's. They move this model hard because every tonne is valued only for its late heat deaths.
2. `@next_dollar.carbon180` (estimate, 0.10–0.67): sourced, dated and arithmetically right.
3. `@climate.advocacy_share_of_policy_win` (shared judgment, 0.33×–2×).
4. `q45_attribution_share_shared` (judgment, 0.67×–3.0×) and `@climate.ira_45q_capture_tco2_per_year` (shared judgment): the 45Q branch is half the result. The attribution needs a resource-based reference class (finding 5), and the branch should stop discounting the shared fact's build-out again (finding 1).
5. `evidence_adj` (judgment, 0.57×–1.43×): splitting it by branch (finding 1) removes most of its swing.
What would most improve the model: the branch-level evidence and implementation rows (finding 1); real land and ocean branches in place of the 25% (finding 2); LDA-based attribution shares (finding 5).
