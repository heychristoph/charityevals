## Drivers
1. `$missing_data_discount` (shared slider, 0.028–0.283): all three branches thin, correctly (no measured outcome; reach is the charity's count, effect judged).
2. `$uov_per_daly` (shared).
3. `gz_counterfactual_adj` (judgment, 0.088–0.206): needs the truck-share anchor (finding 3).
4. `baseline_consumption` (judgment, 0.219–0.095): anchored on UNCTAD's 161 dollars plus aid; sourced anchor, wide range, fine.
5. `$uov_per_ln_consumption` (shared), then `gz_evidence_adj`, `attribution_share_shared`, `implementation_adj` (judgments, each ±15%).

What would most improve the model: the time unit of the food gain (finding 1) and valuing each branch's food from its own spending (finding 2), together -33% (0.142 → 0.094, checked in a scratch copy); then a quantified Gaza counterfactual from crossing data. The result stays below the cause median either way (0.68× now, about 0.45× after both fixes), which the evidence supports: most of the value is a few months of food for people with almost nothing, and a large share of it others would have brought in.
