## Drivers
1. `$missing_data_discount` (shared slider, 0.5 default; finding 1 says part of it is misapplied).
2. `ff_attribution_share` (10%, judgment with a reference class in the explainer but no link and no tie to spend; finding 3) and `ff_counterfactual_adj` (25%, judgment; its reference class is GiveWell's note that salt iodization lapsed where support stopped, a figure for lapse after donor exit would anchor it).
3. `$discount_rate` and `$uov_per_daly` (shared judgments; the 40-year earnings annuity makes the model sensitive to the rate).
4. The next-dollar figure (0.6; 0.3-0.85; estimate from the accounts, sourced and dated within a year: ok).
5. IQ gain, lasting share and validity adjustments (estimates matching GiveWell's iodization model).
Most improvement: split the missing-data step (1), move the supplement adjustments to the terms they belong to (2), tie attribution to the model's own fortification spend (3).

Peers: 8.5x the cause's median (within 10x). The repository's GiveWell import of GAIN's salt iodization is about 11x higher; the row-level difference is the credit given (GiveWell 50% chance GAIN makes a difference vs 2.5% here = 25% x 10%) and the halving for missing data; a difference of rule, not an error, and kept out of site text. Ranges: judgment ranges on shares are wide but plausible; the 5,300x joint span is the site's own warning, already in the summary.

Verdict: pass (no high findings).
