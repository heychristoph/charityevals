Result: 9.951e-7 daly/$ = 9.95e-4 years of healthy life per $1,000 (combined range 2.17e-4 – 4.57e-3); cause median 0.3585 (×0.0028). Verdict: fix needed.

Scope: all three programmes in the audited accounts for 2024 (the latest audited year), as branches: Response (98 operations and 902 service projects, valued through the 284,485 volunteer hours), Resilience (training and recruiting, costed against the response result), Long-term recovery (16 rebuilt homes, plus the $1 million SBP grant as its own branch), and the volunteers' wellbeing as a side effect. Team Rubicon International LLC ($987,488 of income) has no programme spending abroad in 2024 worth a branch. No programme is missing.

Mechanical: lint 0 high / 0 medium / 2 low (unused `audited_program_expenses`, which is context; far-below, with the summary's reason), invariants none, normalization-audit none for this model (generated/ restored afterwards), simulation stable (5% 0.49×, 95% 1.99× the best guess), check.ts all valid.

Previous round: none (round 1).

Where the result comes from: Response ≈ two-thirds, rebuilds and SBP ≈ 30%, volunteers' wellbeing 4% (1.46 of 38.6 healthy years), times 1.1 for unmodelled effects. The gap to the cause's median (×0.003) comes from the households' US consumption on the log scale and the cash cost per volunteer hour ($136 against $34.79 of value), both stated in the summary; it is in line with the domestic peers (American Red Cross 0.0045, Habitat 0.0054, Salvation Army 0.0052, each with a cash or housing transfer rather than labour at the wage).

## Drivers
1. `$missing_data_discount` (shared slider): every branch is thin. That is right for Response (households and the value per hour are judged) and for the rebuilds (value judged).
2. `$uov_per_daly` (shared).
3. `resp_price_ratio` (judgment, unsourced, finding 1): the largest of the model's own inputs.
4. `resp_counterfactual_adj` (judgment, 0.55): names the other voluntary groups but gives no data.
5. `$uov_per_ln_consumption` (shared). After these come `resp_inkind_value`, `resp_vs_poorest_fifth` and `client_hours_share` (judgments, each about ±20%).

What would most improve the model: a sourced contractor price per hour of the same work (finding 1). After that, a count of households or work orders. If Team Rubicon claims its jobs in the Crisis Cleanup network, the network's work orders by organisation would give the households served, and the share no other group claimed would bear on the counterfactual. Last, corrected rebuild-cost anchors (finding 2).

Verdict: fix needed. Findings: 1 high, 1 medium, 7 low.
