## Drivers
1. `phil_gain_share` (judgment, +30%, 3%-150%): no reference class. #1 replaces it with the shared gain over an ordinary climate charity.
2. `$win_years_counted` (shared slider, 5): the funders branch should not read it under the money-moved method (#1).
3. `phil_share_changed` (judgment, 0.3% of $15B): anchored only on Drawdown's own testimonials. A survey of its Capital Coalition funders on dollars changed would improve the model most.
4. `$missing_data_discount` (shared, 0.5): correctly on every branch, since nothing is counted.
5. `phil_tco2e_per_dollar_before` (judgment, 0.01 t/$): an own conversion standing in for the shared `prior_dollar_value` (#1).

After #1-#3 the result would sit at roughly 0.0016 × 3.1 × 1.4 (evidence) ≈ 0.007 years of healthy life per $1,000. #3's counterfactual change interacts with #1's per-year counting. That is about 4× the cause median, within 10×.

Verdict: fix needed (3 high, 2 medium, 6 low).
