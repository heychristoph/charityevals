Checked and found sound:
- Peers. DNA synthesis screening gets the same credit as SecureBio's SecureDNA branch: evidence 0.8 vs 0.75, counterfactual 0.7 both, years until effect 1 vs 1.5, hazard −3% both. Each model values its own year's dollars, SecureBio 2024 and IBBIS 2025.
- NTI. Its $38,343 grant to IBBIS is in both models' costs. Per dollar this is not a double count, and in aggregate it is 1.5% of IBBIS's cost. NTI's model cites the Common Mechanism only as a track record for its own evidence multiplier, not as a 2025 outcome.
- Policy branch. Leaving the DSSC, SBRC and map out of it is right: they are IBBIS's own running work, valued through its spending, not an outside win.
- Result. 5.6× the cause median, so no INV-15 statement is needed. It sits a little above the other pandemic models (5.9–7.5).

What would most improve the model is a published budget or accounts for 2025, from IBBIS or a funder's grant record, in place of the staff build. Next would be a count of providers screening with commec because of IBBIS, which would let the missing-data discount go.

Verdict: fix needed (one high: template placeholder sources on 10 rows).
