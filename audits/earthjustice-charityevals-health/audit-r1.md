## Drivers
1. `$win_years_counted` (shared judgment, 1–20 → 0.004–0.055).
2. `$missing_data_discount` (shared).
3. `@next_dollar.earthjustice` (estimate, 11–79%, sourced to note 3 and dated this month).
4. `@climate.advocacy_share_of_policy_win` (shared judgment, 30%). It is labelled a *climate* policy's share but is read here for EPA health rules. Relabel the fact "a won US federal environmental policy" so its scope matches its readers.
5. `pm_attribution_share_shared` (judgment, 25%, 10–45%). Its reference class is the client list in the D.C. Circuit defence (#6).

The PM2.5 branch carries almost all the value, so the model's own judgments that matter are PM's attribution, implementation (60%) and evidence (0.7). What would most improve the model: a PM-specific wait from the designation timeline (#3), US age and illness figures for PM deaths (#1, #4), and the side-effect tagging (#2). Together #3 and #4 lower the result by about 15–18%. #1 and #2 change little at the defaults but make the value-of-life and side-effects sliders move this model the site's way.
