Note on plausibility (audit only, not for site text): the 40× gap to the cause median comes from that median being dominated by high-income and advocacy models. Against models of the same kind (later-income gains in low-income countries) the result is in range. Two rows explain the gap, both supported by the sources: a cost of about $53 a child-year (cost_total $22.9M over 434,612 children), and a 0.30 SD effect from RCTs of the same software, against 0.1 SD at $130 a child in Save the Children's education branch. Findings 1 and 3 are the parts of the gap that aren't supported. No other fix is needed.

Double counting checked and clear: overhead (programme expenses × total/programme ratio, applied once); counterfactual (RCT effects already net of usual teaching, so counterfactual_adj covers only roll-out by others, Convention-compliant); discounting (one wait, then an annuity due on `$discount_rate`); household sharing (resource_sharing_multiplier for earnings, spillover_ratio only for parents and classmates learning, household_size for reading only); mortality (0, with the health of the children's own children only in unmodelled_benefits_adj); equipment (purchases out, yearly cost of everything in service in; the 2023 990 confirms equipment is expensed rather than capitalised); GPE money (leverage at 1, not also in attribution).

## Drivers
1. `$discount_rate`, `$uov_per_daly`, `@givewell_shared.resource_sharing_multiplier`: shared assumptions and facts.
2. edu_earnings_per_sd (0.2) and edu_persistence (0.4): judgments matching the other charityevals education models, to be repointed to the shared education fact.
3. rct_learning_gain_sd (0.30): sourced, but anchored on an after-school trial (#1).
4. evidence_adj (0.6) and mw_implementation_adj (0.7): judgments (#2).
5. mw_children (369,360): Imagine's own count, used as child-years (#3).

What would most improve the model: the World Bank quasi-experiment (finished end of 2025) and the University of Chicago RCT of BEFIT at scale. When published, they replace the after-school trial as the effect, and replace evidence_adj and mw_implementation_adj with a measured at-scale effect. Next after that, a BEFIT budget split of one-off launch costs from running costs.
