## Drivers
1. fund_aid_moved_share (judgment, 1%, 0.2–4%): 0.0035–0.069. Its reference class is the 2025 cut and how little advocacy moved it. No study measures it.
2. attribution_share_shared (judgment, 0.6%, 0.2–2%): 0.0058–0.058. Anchored on a count, but see finding 2 for how the count should be scoped.
3. $missing_data_discount (shared slider): 0.0035–0.035.
4. fund_displaced_share (judgment, 60%, 30–90%): 0.030–0.0043, shared with NRC's and Save the Children's models.
5. aid_mix_vs_jrs (judgment, 0.6, 0.25–1.5): 0.0072–0.043, anchored on the sister model's branch values (checked).

The cost and reach inputs are all verified against the sources. The result is close to NRC's advocacy model (0.0146), which is expected since the two share the method and most judgments. What would most improve the model is a sourced estimate of NGOs' collective effect on humanitarian budgets, such as a donor-government case study of budget lines kept after NGO campaigns, and fixing findings 1–3, which make the method consistent with NRC's funding branch.
