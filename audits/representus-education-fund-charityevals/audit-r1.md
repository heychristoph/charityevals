The model's own judgments (bs_direct_share, rcv_reform_share, maps_reform_share) hardly move the result: per dollar, the slide, RCV and other-reform branches are within a factor of two of one another, and only redistricting stands out (about 2.6× the average per dollar). So the joint range of the model's own inputs is narrow (0.021–0.036); the uncertainty sits in the shared figures. What would most improve the model: the volunteer time in the credit (finding 1), and a split of the education programme's 2024 spending by campaign from RepresentUs's own budget, which would replace the campaign-count judgment behind rcv_reform_share and maps_reform_share.
