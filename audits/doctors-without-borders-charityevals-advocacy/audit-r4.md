## Reasoning checks (no finding)

- Credit: MSF Access at a quarter of the advocates' resources, the counterfactual a separate question (the share that wouldn't have come yet), the years brought forward the shared `$win_years_counted` (Convention 5 held: one question each). - Thin: all three branches at `$missing_data_discount`; none has a charity count meeting a trial of the same work, so none is over-applied.
