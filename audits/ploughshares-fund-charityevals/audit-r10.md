## Checks on the listed rows
1. fb_credit_share: now a judged input and the employers are out of its denominator. Correct by 2.24: the employers' pay for the added work-years is a mobilised career, valued at a typical dollar of work inside fb_work_dollars and under the single `attribution_share`, which multiplies by mobiliser_share once. Horizon 2045 (co-creates the programme and its tools, per the fellows page) stays in fb_attribution_share_shared. 1 (0.5 to 1) is a defensible best guess: the cap at 1 sits at the best, so the range is one-sided, which is right for a share that cannot exceed 1. See findings 1 and 2.
2. fb_attribution_share_shared 0.6 to 0.65 (0.4-0.8 to 0.45-0.85): the employers' release time was the only party taken out, Horizon 2045 (the one named co-deliverer) stays, and no co-deliverer named in the old text was dropped. Removing a party from a denominator raises the share, so the direction is right; 0.65 is a judgment of a small employer part beside Horizon 2045's design and coaching, within the explainer's own "0.6 less the employers' part". The employers' release time during the fellowship is correctly mobilised: they would not have released staff for it otherwise. The funder of the fellowship is not stated on the page (not checkable), so Horizon 2045's actual cash share is not verifiable; the range 0.45 to 0.85 covers it.
3. Class. The single `attribution_share` (class research) pools the grants (typical-dollar part), own work and fellowship. By 2.24 each path takes the mobiliser's share once: grants (pass-through co-funding, 0.95, then the funded research and advocacy is adopted by others, s); own work (partners' unpaid time in coalitions as co-advocates, 0.6, then s for adoption); fellowship (Horizon 2045, then s for the employers). Research is the right class for a grantmaker whose output is other organisations' research and advocacy. The four grl grantees are valued at their own models' figures, which already carry their own mobiliser class, multiplied only by gr_attribution_share_shared (0.95); grl_dalys does not read mobiliser_share and the dollars are taken out of nuclear_cost_share, so s is taken once on that path (inside the grantee's model), not twice and not zero times. The one point to hold in mind: grl takes the grantee's s but Ploughshares' own 0.95 co-funder share is not a mobilised share, which is consistent with the rule that pass-through grants are co-funding. No change.
4. gr_attribution_share_shared 0.95 (0.8-1): co-funders' dollars are already out of the cut per dollar and only unpaid partner time is left, so it counts co-funders and co-deliverers, with no adopter.
