## Drivers

2. `$uov_per_daly` and `$uov_per_ln_consumption` (shared).
3. `mkt_value_per_borrower` ($150, $30–400; judgment anchored on interest spreads and the microcredit trials).

5. `afr_targeting` (0.65, 0.45–1.0; judgment anchored on the World Bank bottom-40% ratios). Africa is half the result.

After finding 1, `nso_delivery_share` (the network's share of spending reaching families) and `dir_cost_share` join the drivers. The result then scales roughly with the delivery share. The most useful improvement is a published network figure for the national organisations' own international spending (Habitat's federated-network revenue, as the US model uses for the US) to anchor `int_network_resources`. Second is the Form 990's Schedule F split of the international grants by recipient, for `consolidated_transfer_share`. Peers: the model sits at 0.61× the global-health median now, and about 1.6× after finding 1, inside the 10× band either way.
