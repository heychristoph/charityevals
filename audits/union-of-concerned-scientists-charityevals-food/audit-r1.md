## Drivers
1. `attribution_share_shared` (judgment, 0.00066–0.013): its anchor is unsupported (finding 2). It would improve most with evidence of UCS's actual part in the defence, such as an intervenor or USDA brief citing its analysis.
2. `wetland_share_lost` (judgment, 0.0008–0.012): no reference class, and probably 5–10× high (finding 3). It would improve most with the farmed share of Upper Midwest wetlands outside state law (NWI/NRI) and historical drainage rates.
3. `p_success` (judgment, single event, 0.0008–0.012): the reference class needs naming (finding 5).
4. `$win_years_counted` (shared slider): should also drive the accumulated drainage (finding 3).
5. `counterfactual_adj` (judgment): stale, should be about 0.9 (finding 1).
