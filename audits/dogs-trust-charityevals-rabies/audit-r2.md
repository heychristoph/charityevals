## Drivers

1. `$missing_data_discount` (shared slider, 0.5): 0.104–1.04. The thin classification is right.
2. `incidence_ratio` (judgment 1.3): 0.24–1.20. Unquantified, and WHO GHE points lower per person (finding 4). This is the model's biggest open question.
3. `coverage_sustainment_adj` (judgment 0.6): 0.26–0.87. Reference classes are named (annual drives vs Goa's measured 70%).
4. `@dogs_trust.rabies_share_of_international` (judgment 0.20): 0.87–0.28. With finding 1 fixed it moves the result far less (0.374 → 0.325 over its range), because the credit then rises with the spend.
5. `goa_deaths_averted` (estimate 80): verified. 0.32–0.71.

What would most improve the model: fix the inverted attribution (finding 1) and value the donated vaccine in the full cost (finding 3); compute `incidence_ratio` from GHE and Hampson deaths and dog densities (finding 4); read the rabies age split from WHO GHE, weighted by reach (finding 2). With findings 1, 2 and 6 the result is 0.351 (scratch copy), 0.87× the global-health median, within the peer range: Gibson et al.'s Goa figure ($526 per DALY, about 1.9 per $1,000) sits above it before our adjustments, as it should.
