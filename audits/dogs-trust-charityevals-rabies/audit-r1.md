# Audit: dogs-trust-charityevals-rabies, round 1

Result: 4.478e-4 uov/$ = 0.224 years of healthy life per $1,000 (combined range 0.042–1.21); global-health median 0.403 (×0.56). Verdict: **fix needed** (1 high).

Scope: the "rabies" line of charities/dogs-trust.json: 25% (facts/dogs_trust.json `rabies_share_of_international`, a judgment) of the 2025 international work line, £26.099m (SOFA p. 94). That covers WVS/Mission Rabies dog vaccination, education and surveillance, and grants to rabies programmes, year to 31 December 2025. The lines add up to programme spending: $145,473,409 + $8,596,358 = $154,069,767, against £116.941m × 1.3175. The sister model takes `1 − share` of the same line and reads the same fact. **No double counting between the two models**: the sister model counts WVS treatment (158,647), WVS and grant sterilisations, Bosnia and Ireland, but no vaccination doses and no human-health effect. This model counts the dogs' own rabies suffering only inside `other_unmodelled_share` (1%), and the sister model doesn't count it. One cost/benefit misalignment between them is finding 8.

Mechanical: lint 0/0/0. Invariants: none violated. normalization-audit: 0 violations for every invariant. simulation-check: 5% 0.306, 50% 1.00, 95% 2.91 × best guess (stable). The audit scripts rewrote generated/*.json; I restored those files to HEAD, so no repo file changed.

## Drivers

1. `$missing_data_discount` (shared slider, 0.5): moves the result 0.045–0.448. The thin classification is correct.
2. `external_validity_adj` (judgment, 0.7): the incidence half can be sourced (finding 5).
3. `@dogs_trust.rabies_share_of_international` (judgment, 0.25): its anchor double counts grants and uses an uninflated, partly misattributed cost per dose (findings 3–4).
4. `goa_deaths_averted` (estimate, 80): verified. The range is ours, since the paper gives none.
5. `attribution_share_shared` (judgment, 0.75): inconsistent with the cost per dose the model implies (finding 3).

What would most improve the model: one stated full cost per dose in 2025 prices, used for both the cost share and the share of resources (findings 3–4); `implementation_adj` at 1 (finding 2); a sourced incidence ratio by country weighted by Mission Rabies' 2025 doses (finding 5); and GBD rabies age shares in `@death_ages` (finding 1). Together, findings 2–4 would raise the result by about 1.4–1.7× (to roughly 0.3–0.4), still under the global-health median and well within 10× of it. Peers: at 0.56× the cause median, the gap is explained by `total_adj` 0.127 (seven stacked multipliers, including the 0.5 thin discount) and the overhead ratio 1.39. Gibson et al.'s own estimate for Goa ($526 per DALY, about 1.9 per $1,000) sits above this before our adjustments, as expected.

Findings by severity: high 1, medium 4, low 8.
