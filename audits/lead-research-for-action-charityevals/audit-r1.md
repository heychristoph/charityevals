## Drivers
1. `$mobiliser_share`, `$discount_rate`, `$win_years_counted`, `$missing_data_discount`: shared sliders (the first four swings); the site's ranges, not the builder's.
2. `source_share` (0.037–0.37 years per $1,000 across its range): judgment, no measured reference class (M2). The model's most improvable input: LeRA/LEEP's plastics leachability results and a cookware-use × leaching calculation would replace most of it.
3. `issue_record_adj` (0.056–0.37): judgment anchored on the shared lobbying base rate and Malawi's record (verified facts); reasonable, though no LMIC lead-standard base rate is used.
4. `implementation_adj` (0.050–0.30): judgment whose anchor points higher (M5).
5. `@next_dollar.lead_research_for_action` (0.058–0.23): estimate above the neutral factors without evidence (M1).

Ranges: the model's own ranges span 1.2e-4 to 37.9 jointly (audit-model's spread note); each wide range is real (product share, chance of action, delivery, cost), and the summary already says the result is very uncertain, so no finding. Peers: ×1.25 the cause median, no gap to explain. The ×46 gap to LEEP's charityevals model comes from rows that differ for real reasons (won regulations, p_success 1 against 0.16; LEEP's larger credit share; no past_work_share cut), plus M4's double cut here; not a finding beyond M4 and L5.
