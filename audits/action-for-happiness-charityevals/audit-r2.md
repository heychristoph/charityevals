## Drivers
1. `$missing_data_discount` (shared, 0.041–0.41). Thin is right for every branch: the trial is of the eight-week predecessor; there are no data on the groups or the digital programme; Krekel's monitoring data are pre-post only.
2. `digital_effect_duration_years`, judgment 0.75 y (0.093–0.51). Below its anchor (finding 2).
3. `digital_implementation_adj`, judgment 15% (0.080–0.39), anchored on 25,000 10 Days sign-ups among 187,000.
4. `digital_evidence_adj`, judgment 0.55 (0.12–0.31). Its anchor should be the SWB-specific correction (finding 3).
5. `digital_initial_effect_sd`, estimate 0.33 (Bolier, self-help) and `$daly_per_wellby` (shared).

Peers: 0.204 is 2.8× the global-health median, within 10×. It sits far above the UK and US wellbeing service models (about 0.0016–0.0026) because the cost per person reached is $3.8. That gap rests on the digital branch's mass reach, and finding 1 changes how that reach is costed. Persistence and the share of users are anchored and ranged.

What would most improve the model: the charity's counts of 10 Days completers and monthly active app users (replacing the 15% judgment); its cost of recruiting a new member or 10 Days participant (for the next-dollar figure); and any follow-up of Happiness Habits participants beyond the course.
