**Is a past average for one branch correct under normalization.md 2.10 and INV-18? Yes.**
(a) 2.10 defines a past average by achievements that are "not items more money buys again at a known price", and gives as an example "a fixed service's lives saved". The digital branch fits: a year's actions from an audience built since 2011 (the accounts confirm the 2011 launch), served at mostly fixed cost. The RNLI precedent is the same construction: one year's spending over one year's lives saved, with the stations already built. 2.10 also names "a course" as a `unit_cost` item, so the courses rightly stay unfactored.
(b) Taking the factor on one part of a model has a precedent in 2.10: SoGive's MND Association and Versus Arthritis take it "only on research's share of their healthy years".
(c) INV-18 is met: a row on the path reads `@next_dollar.action_for_happiness`, halving it lowers the headline (by 43%), and the row has `published` 1. The normalization audit confirms it.
(d) Convention 11's same-period rule holds: the cost and the wins are both 2025's, so no `average_spend_vs_year` is needed.
(e) The factor reaches everything the digital branch produces: its WELLBYs, its side effects and its deaths.
The construction implicitly assumes that the next dollar is split between the branches as 2025's spending was (51/49). That is the right default when nothing says otherwise.
