## Drivers
1. `$daly_per_wellby` (shared slider): 0.0011–0.0060.
2. `effect_duration_years` (estimate, Held 2024): 0.00077–0.0027. Its anchor is misread (finding 2).
3. `$missing_data_discount` (shared): 0.00079–0.0023. The thin therapy branch is 48% of the result.
4. `prior_effect_sd_ptsd` (estimate, Kitchiner 2019): 0.00084–0.0021. Foa 2018 should enter the blend (finding 5).
5. `opt_dose_vs_iop` (judgment, no anchor): 0.0011–0.0019 (findings 3 and 7).

What would most improve the model: restate the duration from Held 2024's own effect sizes; blend in Foa 2018's controlled effect for the massed format; split the triage-and-counselling count from the centres' outpatient therapy, with reach from sessions and a cited dose; count the donated PSAs in WWP's share of the resources; and replace the 0.05 unmodelled share with the support branch's suicides plus itemised rows. Findings 2, 4 and 5 roughly offset each other (+36%, −19%, −28%). The result stays near 0.001 years of healthy life per $1,000, far below the cause's median, for the reason the summary gives.

Verdict: fix needed. Findings: 2 high, 6 medium (one of them mechanical and explained), 5 low.
