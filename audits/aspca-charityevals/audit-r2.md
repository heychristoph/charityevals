## Drivers

1. `own_ls_gain` (judgment, 0.05): the top swing (1.7e-5 – 3.5e-4). Its reference class is now right, but the shared SD figure is retyped and the counterfactual repeated (finding 1).
2. `$missing_data_discount` and `$daly_per_wellby` (shared): the owners' chain is thin, which is correct, and the WELLBY bridge carries 99.8% of the result.
3. `own_share_eased` (judgment, 0.5): anchored on the counterfactual's reference class (finding 2).
4. `vet_counterfactual_adj` (judgment, 0.5), now anchored on the AVCC survey.
5. `food_days_per_household` (judgment, 30 days; wrong format, finding 4) and `vet_owned_share` (judgment, 0.75).

Findings 1 and 3 raise the result (+33% and +11%, about 1.28e-4 together), and finding 5 lowers it by about 8%. Apply them by the rules, not netted. A count of the community clinics' sick-animal visits against routine visits, and of pet-food households, would most improve the model.
