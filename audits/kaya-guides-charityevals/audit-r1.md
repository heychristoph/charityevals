2. `effect_duration_years`: an estimate from 3-month follow-ups, the model's largest own swing (0.21–1.01). Finding 1 sets it from the pooled follow-ups. Kaya Guides' 2026 RCT with a longer follow-up would improve it most.

4. `implementation_adj`: a judgment (0.28–0.51), see finding 3.
5. `@wellby_factors.household_spillover_ratio_psychotherapy` and `wellbys_per_sd_year`: shared estimates.

The pooled effect (three trials, sourced and verified) moves the result less than any of these. Cost is the charity's own derived figure. No accounts exist, so a Development Consortium or Players Philanthropy Fund statement listing the project's 2025 spending would firm it up.
