## Drivers
1. `$win_years_counted` (shared slider, 1–20): 0.22–3.08.
2. `$missing_data_discount` (shared slider): 0.20–2.02. All three branches are rightly thin: the AMC's effect on the speed of PCV's spread is a judgment.
3. `@next_dollar.center_for_global_development` (estimate, sourced and dated 2026-11-14; 0.10–0.84): 0.28–2.35.
4. `a_cgd_share` (judgment, 15%, 5–35%): 0.49–2.05. Anchored on the working group's history, with no quantitative reference class.
5. `a_amc_share` (judgment, 40%, 15–70%): 0.52–1.59. Anchored on the evaluation's own caveat and the Hib and rotavirus comparison.

The biggest fixes are adding GiveWell's shared child-vaccine benefits to the AMC branch (finding 1) and valuing the vaccine money at Gavi's and governments' counterfactual figures (finding 2). Together they move the result to about 1.3 years per 1,000 dollars. Beyond that, the model would improve most from a sourced reference class for CGD's credit share. One candidate is the credit split in GiveWell's assessment of a policy coalition on the site (the Tax Justice Network model), stated in `a_cgd_share`'s explainer.
