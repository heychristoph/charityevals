Structure is otherwise sound. Wins and costs come from the same year (2024 arrivals and funding against 2024 spending; Costa Rica's win over 1.5 campaign years at the 2023–24 average in 2024 prices). Each branch's evidence, counterfactual, attribution and missing-data rows are applied once. Parolees' years are discounted as their own, the Costa Rica reform reads `$win_years_counted`, and waits are discounted. Deaths go through `@moral_weights.uov_per_death_age_15_49 × $life_value_multiplier`. The displaced aid, remittances and brain drain are side-effect rows. Money moved and careers are 0, each with its reason. Staff pay comes from the accounts with `@staff_pay.gap_advocacy`. policy-research-meta fits (all value in uov; rights-advocacy.md allows it). The result sits at the cause median. Findings 2 and 3 together move it to 0.144, within the same reference class. What would most improve the model: a sourced value of a humanitarian dollar shared across the advocacy models, and any evidence of advocacy's effect on response-plan funding (the Sudan counterfactual).

Verdict: fix needed (2 high: INV-14 career placeholders, Haiti PPP price year; 4 medium; 5 low).
