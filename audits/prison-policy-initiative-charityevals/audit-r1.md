2. `$missing_data_discount` (shared, all three branches thin, which is correct: reach is projections): 0.008–0.085.

5. `uov_per_dollar_saved`: not in the tornado as one input, but it carries the whole money chain, and its two consumption baselines are findings 1 and 2.

What would most improve the model: the per-person and national-accounts baselines (1, 2); one frame for the federal savings (3); a federal credit share counting the campaign's earlier years (4); and branches for the other wins in place of the ratio (5). At the defaults, 1+2 alone take the result to about 0.032. Adding 3 and 4 at the values above gives roughly 0.030 to 0.035, still well inside 10× of the cause's median.
