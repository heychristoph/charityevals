## Checks of the listed rows
1. fb_credit_share = 1 (0.5-1): right. No other programme that trained or placed the same people is named in the model, the 990 or anywhere I found; CAIS's own course page and its Coursera edition are the same teaching, not another provider (a web search found no named partner for the cohort course). The employers who pay for the alumni's work are no longer in a denominator; the mobiliser's share (fb_attribution_share, class research) covers them exactly once. Best guess 1 with the high at 1 is a one-sided range; acceptable as the template's.
2. fb_attribution_share = attr_other x mobiliser_share = 0.95 x s, class research: right (a trained person's career is a mobilised resource, 2.24). attr_other's 0.95 counts only CAIS and its own co-deliverers (Schmidt Sciences' SafeBench prizes, conference venues); no adopter. It is used directly for the branch where the template has a `fb_attribution_share_shared` row; low, no value change.
3. ai_attribution_share = ai_attribution_share_shared x s, class research: right. Parts: attr_cluster 0.41 counts CAIS's compute ($2.95M, less $256,598 of programme revenue) against outside researchers' time ($1.2M-7.5M, about $4M), who are co-deliverers of the papers; labs and others that take the papers up are not in it. Arithmetic fits (2.69/(2.69+4.0) = 0.40; range ends 0.26 and 0.69 against stated 0.27 and 0.65, close enough). attr_research 0.75 counts Scale AI and the ~1,000 contributors of Humanity's Last Exam and the co-authors, no adopter. attr_other 0.95: partners and venues. The weighting 0.58 recomputes: (2,947,497 x 0.41 + 1,168,082 x 0.75 + 777,874 x 0.95)/4,893,453 = 0.577.
4. Mobiliser's share enters once on each path: total_adj reads attribution_share, built from ai_attribution_share and fb_attribution_share; no other row reads `mobiliser_share`; cost rows are not netted (the deferred part of 2.24 for research branches).
5. "Would it have happened anyway": lives in fb_share_working (the "because of it" share), counterfactual_adj and years_of_impact; the change loses none of it and the credit share does not carry it. See finding 1 for the one place it is cut twice.
6. Text: the model's `summary` quotes no share or figure that moved (placeholders). Stale: finding 2 and 3. `fb_credit_share` explainer is right. The write-up was stale in six places; corrected (below).

## Is 10.7 a defensible best guess?
The structure now follows 2.24, and the 4.1x move is the course's value no longer cut by a pro-rata share that counted its employers: 12 people (5% of 240) working 5 years at $250,000 a year is $13.9M of typical work for a $0.5M course, at 95% credit. That is a high ratio, resting on a judgment the charity does not publish (no alumni survey); the range 1% to 15% is wide enough to show it. I consider it defensible as a best guess for the rule, not conservative. After finding 1 it would be about 13.1; no value change is demanded beyond the template's rows (do not tune toward a target).
