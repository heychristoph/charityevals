## Drivers
- **ipv_reduction** (judgment, 0.05–0.5): the main driver. It is anchored on the Uganda video trial and NOVAH's unpowered pilot. Finding 2 sets which audience it is for. The 2026 RCT with 2,400 couples (endline Sep 2026) will replace the judgment, and should then also settle the missing-data step (finding 1).
- **years_of_coverage** (judgment, 0.4–2): only eight months of follow-up exist (Uganda). The RCT's four-month and one-month windows won't settle it.
- **implementation_adj** (judgment, 0.3–0.9): should go to 1 under Convention 5 (finding 2).
- **burden_per_year_adj** (judgment, 0.4–1.2): needs a correct anchor (finding 7).
- **internal_validity_adj** (judgment, 0.35–1) and **@ipv.daly_rate_women_15_49_eastern_ssa** (GBD estimate): the place step in finding 6 would move the burden more than its own uncertainty interval does.

The spread is real uncertainty: no RCT of this drama has reported, and the judgment share is 87%. The summary says so. The result sits at ×0.92 of the global-health median, so no peer gap to explain. Applying the four highs together gives about 0.104 (missing data ×0.5, dose ×1.67, treatment costs ×1.15). Then findings 5 and 6 give about 0.07.
