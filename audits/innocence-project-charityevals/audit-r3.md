## Drivers
1. `$missing_data_discount` (shared slider): every branch is thin, correctly.
2. `$win_years_counted` (shared slider), on the reform branch (78% of the result).
3. `pol_innocent_share` (judgment, 0.5–5%; anchors Gross et al. 2014, Roman et al. 2012, Loeffler et al. 2019).
4. `@next_dollar.innocence_project` (estimate, 14–72%; sourced and verified).
5. `@incarceration.life_years_lost_per_year_incarcerated` (shared estimate, Patterson 2013), now only on the reform branch. Then `pol_coalition_share`, `pol_evidence_adj` and `pol_implementation_adj` (judgments).

The result is about a hundredth of the global-health median and of the other two US prison models (Prison Policy Initiative 0.063, Brennan Center 0.070). The summary gives the reason: about two million dollars of casework per exoneration, about 1% of wrongful imprisonment removed by the window's reforms, and a next dollar at a third of the average. The ranges reflect real uncertainty. A measured rate of innocence in the general prison population would improve the model most, since nothing outside death sentences exists. After that, a field evaluation of lineup or interrogation laws' effect on wrongful convictions.

Verdict: pass (no high, one medium, five low)
