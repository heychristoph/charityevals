## Ambitious Impact

Terms: <http://www.ambitiousimpact.com/terms>. Reports on [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/communities/ambitious-impact/)
are CC BY 4.0. Two models rest on reports that are only on AIM's website: `better-season-project-aim`
and `novah-aim`.

Open: when the two reports are on Zenodo, set each model's `source.license` to the CC BY 4.0 record,
as the other 14 have it. Until then their current note, that AIM's terms allow non-commercial use with
attribution, is wrong.
